## A09.1 Tel Var and Asset Protection

### A09.T Tel Var and Asset Protection

#### テルバー（Tel Var Stones） Loss and Beginner Asset Protection

[A0735][PATCH][CLAIM][IMPERIAL_CITY][TEL_VAR_STONES] On both the surface and in the sewers of 帝都（Imperial City）, assume a death risk of losing 50% of carried テルバー（Tel Var Stones） on either a PvE or PvP death.
[A0736][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][IMPERIAL_CITY][TEL_VAR_STONES][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Especially when the questioner appears to be trying to collect テルバー（Tel Var Stones）, treat not only the number of deaths but also the estimated amount at risk based on currently carried Tel Var as a major death risk, and do not downplay the Tel Var loss rule.
[A0737][REFERENCE_NOTE][PATCH][CLAIM][PATCH_HISTORY][IMPERIAL_CITY][TEL_VAR_STONES] Official PC/Mac Patch Notes v2.4.5 changed the テルバー（Tel Var Stones） loss on death in 帝都（Imperial City） from 10% on PvE death and 80% on PvP death to 50% on both PvE and PvP death.
[A0738][REFERENCE_NOTE][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][EVIDENCE][PATCH_HISTORY][IMPERIAL_CITY][TEL_VAR_STONES] Because ESO Support Answer 30730 retains the old statement of an 80% loss on PvP death, do not prioritize that answer over Official PC/Mac Patch Notes v2.4.5 as evidence for determining the current rule.
[A0739][REFERENCE_SOURCE][EVIDENCE][PATCH_HISTORY][IMPERIAL_CITY][TEL_VAR_STONES] Evidence target: A0735, A0737, A0738. Official PC/Mac Patch Notes v2.4.5: https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/268496/pc-mac-patch-notes-v2-4-5
[A0740][REFERENCE_SOURCE][EVIDENCE][PATCH_HISTORY][IMPERIAL_CITY][TEL_VAR_STONES] Evidence target: A0738. ESO Support Answer 30730（old 80% statement remains）: https://help.elderscrollsonline.com/#ja/answer/30730

#### Surface テルバー（Tel Var Stones） and District Flags

[A0741][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][IMPERIAL_CITY][TEL_VAR_STONES][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION] When the goal is efficient PvE acquisition of テルバー（Tel Var Stones） on the surface of 帝都（Imperial City）, treat looping between districts while defeating district bosses in succession as a strong high-efficiency option rather than broadly collecting ordinary mobs, chests, low-value detours, and similar targets.
[A0742][PATCH][CLAIM][IMPERIAL_CITY][TEL_VAR_STONES] The テルバー（Tel Var Stones） acquisition bonus from district ownership on the surface of 帝都（Imperial City） is not determined only by whether your alliance owns the district you are currently in; it accumulates across surface Tel Var gains according to the number of districts controlled by your alliance.
[A0743][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][PRIORITY][IMPERIAL_CITY] When actively engaging in PvP on the surface of 帝都（Imperial City）, if possible treat capturing a district flag before engaging so that 連続攻撃（Continuous Attack） is active as a strong option.
[A0744][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][IMPERIAL_CITY][SITUATIONAL_AWARENESS] Flipping a district flag in 帝都（Imperial City） gives enemy alliances indirect information that players from your alliance were active in that district, so treat the action as a trade between buff / テルバー（Tel Var Stones） benefits and the information cost of making your alliance's activity location easier to infer.

#### テルバー（Tel Var Stones） and Imperial Physique

[A0745][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][IMPERIAL_CITY][TEL_VAR_STONES][ROLE_GANKER] Do not infer that an experienced player or Ganker in 帝都（Imperial City） must be carrying a large amount of テルバー（Tel Var Stones）, and do not equate players active primarily for PvP itself with players carrying high-value Tel Var.
[A0746][PATCH][CLAIM][IMPERIAL_CITY][TEL_VAR_STONES][ROLE_GANKER] Because the acquisition multiplier from carried テルバー（Tel Var Stones） does not apply to Tel Var taken from enemy players, carrying large amounts of Tel Var solely to increase the amount stolen from players provides no multiplier benefit.

## A09.2 Terrain, LoS, and Situational Awareness

### A09.L Terrain and Situational Awareness

#### Terrain, LoS, and Situational Awareness

[A0747][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][IMPERIAL_CITY][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Players who fight regularly in 帝都（Imperial City） should preselect locations where they can fight and escape effectively, prepare both the LoS point to use from each location and the next retreat point as a set, and learn which terrain works by observing the actual movement, reversals, and retreat destinations of players experienced in Imperial City.
[A0748][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][IMPERIAL_CITY][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Before engaging on the surface of 帝都（Imperial City）, identify not only the LoS and disengagement destinations available from the current position but also the positions and major mechanics of nearby bosses, and prepare a rough response plan for the effects of a boss entering the fight.

#### Imperial City Terrain, Flags, and Awareness

[A0749][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][RULE][IMPERIAL_CITY][ROLE_BOMBER][SITUATIONAL_AWARENESS] In group ambushes in 帝都（Imperial City）, equipment, skills, and other effects capable of pulling or grouping an enemy cluster into area burst may be used, so do not assume a Bomber must always be concealed at point-blank range. 闇の収束（Dark Convergence） and 迫る苦痛（Rush of Agony） are examples of grouping effects; recheck specific examples under the current patch.
[A0750][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][IMPERIAL_CITY][ROLE_GANKER][ROLE_BOMBER] Because transition points in 帝都（Imperial City）—such as district gates, quest areas, vaults, and dungeons—make it easier to predict where and when players will pass, treat them as locations where ambushes and surprise attacks are easier to establish, and during events when groups concentrate there, remain alert to them as possible Bomber attack points as well.
[A0751][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][IMPERIAL_CITY][SITUATIONAL_AWARENESS][ROLE_GANKER][ROLE_BOMBER] When watching for ambushes in 帝都（Imperial City）, do not check only the same elevation and immediate surroundings; include as threat candidates enemies approaching while dropping from high ground and enemies capable of initiating area attacks or grouping effects from range.

#### Terrain, LoS, and Situational Awareness

[A0752][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][PRACTICE][IMPERIAL_CITY][MOVEMENT_SPEED] If 騎乗訓練（mount training） is sufficiently advanced, prefer fast mounted traversal over travel on foot as an option for relatively safe routing across the surface of 帝都（Imperial City）; if the 闇の一党（Dark Brotherhood） passive シャドウライダー（Shadow Rider） is available, use it to reduce detection and aggro from hostile NPCs and make it easier to maintain mounted movement.
[A0753][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][IMPERIAL_CITY] In the sewers of 帝都（Imperial City）, use 優先攻撃ターゲット（Target Lock） to track the relative position of enemies who move behind corners or obstacles and use that information to judge whether they will chase or flee, whether you can pursue, whether to transition into an ambush, or whether to give up the chase.
[A0754][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][IMPERIAL_CITY][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION] When fighting a surface boss in 帝都（Imperial City） with a safety-oriented approach, if possible lure the boss onto terrain favorable to you, use the terrain and boss to restrict approaches from behind or by enemy players, and avoid prolonged fighting in open terrain with all directions left exposed.

## A09.3 Bosses and the Center

### A09.B Bosses and the Center

#### Sewer Bosses, the Central Area, and Boss-Fight Risk

[A0755][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][CLAIM][IMPERIAL_CITY][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION] The モラグ・バルの幻影（Simulacrum of Molag Bal） in the central area of the 帝都（Imperial City） sewers produces a high-damage, wide-area explosion when defeated, so do not treat the moment of its defeat as a safe period.
[A0756][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][IMPERIAL_CITY][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][SITUATIONAL_AWARENESS] Immediately after defeating the モラグ・バルの幻影（Simulacrum of Molag Bal）, attention tends to concentrate on explosion damage, healing, resurrection, looting, and similar tasks, so remain alert to that period as a dangerous window in which hostile players may have a good opportunity to intervene.
[A0757][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][IMPERIAL_CITY][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] When giving safety information about bosses or the main quest in 帝都（Imperial City）, warn as needed about major mechanics that can unexpectedly kill a first-time player, such as healing, forced CC, heavy damage, or on-death effects; if the questioner wants to avoid spoilers, withhold specifics and limit the warning to a general one.
[A0758][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][COND][IMPERIAL_CITY] If you have already qualified for loot from a monster or surface boss in 帝都（Imperial City） and remain within loot range when it is defeated, loot rights may remain even while dead; when seeking equipment rewards, do not reflexively respawn far away, but check whether the loot can be collected before the corpse disappears.

## A09.5 Stealth Suppression

### A09.S Stealth Suppression

#### Supplement: Stealth / Invisibility Suppression by 帝都（Imperial City） Surface Bosses

[A0774][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][CLAIM][AI][LIMIT][IMPERIAL_CITY][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][STEALTH_COUNTER] While alive, the 恐怖の偵察隊（Patrolling Horror） on the surface of 帝都（Imperial City） and the コールドハーバーの精鋭衛兵（Coldharbour Elite Guard） in the Imperial City sewers have unique areas or abilities that detect or suppress stealth and invisibility nearby.
