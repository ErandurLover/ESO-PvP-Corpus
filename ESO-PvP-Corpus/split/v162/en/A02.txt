# A02 — Beginner Onboarding, Learning, Diagnosis, and Verification

[TAG_NOTE][OVERRIDE] Indicates that concrete additional information can override a default principle.

[TAG_NOTE][GROUP_CONTENT] Indicates content or operation in which the player participates as part of a group and interactions with allies are included in the evaluation.
[TAG_NOTE][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Indicates that effective contribution to the group as a whole, rather than individual freedom of action, is the evaluation axis.

[TAG_NOTE][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE] Indicates the amount of defense against Critical Damage. [TAG_NOTE][PVP_ASSUMPTION] Indicates that PvP self-defense evaluation must not fix enemy count, willingness to attack, range, mobility, pursuit capability, or similar threat conditions in the user's favor; include threat conditions that can realistically be encountered in the target environment.
[TAG_NOTE][VENGEANCE] Indicates an evaluation target specific to the 復讐（Vengeance） environment, which has dedicated rules, abilities, loadouts, or similar systems different from normal campaigns. ## A02.1 Initial Entry Conditions

### A02.10 Entry-Level Onboarding and Initial Success Conditions

#### Core Conclusions to Retrieve First

[A0090][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL] For entry-level beginners, before wins or maximum damage, use the ability to survive for a period on the battlefield and become familiar with the feel of PvP and basic controls as the main evaluation axis of initial builds and learning.
[A0091][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL] As a rule, verify that builds for entry-level beginners function in the order of ① survivability, ② practical sustain, ③ damage and other performance.
[A0092][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][PRACTICE][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not use simple operating steps or the fact that survival is possible with ideal judgment and positioning alone as evidence that a build or tactic is suitable for entry-level beginners. Evaluate survivability, practical sustain, tolerance for judgment and execution mistakes, and the margin to learn PvP fundamentals such as LoS, defensive transitions, CC, and switching between offense and defense during actual combat.
[A0093][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][PRACTICE] For entry-level beginners, prioritize methods that reduce danger while allowing continued combat participation and experience with offense, defense, LoS, regrouping with allies, and similar fundamentals over methods that secure survival by persistently avoiding meaningful combat participation.

#### Initial Goals, Success Criteria, and Combat Participation

[A0094][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][LIMIT][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In fixed-capacity small-group content, when an entry-level beginner personally prioritizes team victory and effective contribution, do not make it a standard recommendation to remain in positions difficult for enemies to attack, such as high ground near the spawn side, and continue only ranged attacks while persistently avoiding participation in normal combat.
[A0095][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][PRACTICE] Relying only on ranged attacks from positions difficult for enemies to attack reduces opportunities to repeat defensive transitions under enemy pressure, LoS, resource management, CC, positional adjustment with allies, and similar fundamentals in real combat, making it limited as a basic learning method for entry-level beginners.
[A0096][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][PRACTICE] Unless the entry-level beginner themselves wants immediate participation, do not make “just join first even if you die repeatedly” the standard advice; first present a build that can absorb beginner mistakes more easily and minimum operational preparation.
[A0097][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER] An entry-level beginner unfamiliar with both PvP and two-bar PvE operation should equip the PvP build and, in an open-world area or similar environment, execute the skills on each bar, healing, defense, 打破（Break Free）, equipment effects, and similar mechanics at least once before entering real PvP as initial preparation.

#### Initial Builds, Sustain, and Defensive Margin

[A0098][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL] For entry-level beginner builds based mainly on 重装鎧（Heavy Armor）, verify the practical sustain lost by relying heavily on Heavy Armor and, if necessary, compensate through monster masks, 神話装備（Mythic）, ムンダス（Mundus）, food, or similar sources. In the initial stage, prioritize recovery and resource maintenance over small increases in damage.
[A0099][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL] For sustain adjustment of entry-level beginners, as a rule consider ムンダス（Mundus） → food → equipment → jewelry enchantments in that order. If collection constraints prevent use of monster masks, 神話装備（Mythic）, or similar equipment, the equipment-adjustment stage may be skipped and jewelry enchantments considered next.
[A0100][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL] When adjusting sustain through jewelry enchantments, do not change multiple pieces at once. As a rule, change one piece at a time, test it in practical combat for a meaningful period, and add further adjustment while confirming the required amount.
[A0101][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Do not make configurations that greatly increase only resources, or only resources and damage, without sufficiently supplementing survivability a standard recommendation for entry-level beginners. Do not make beginner survival depend on continuously maintaining high precision in early enemy detection, danger prediction, optimal positioning and escape-route selection, accurate resource management, and appropriate defensive inputs within short windows. Secure defensive margin that can absorb a certain amount of error when judgment or operation is delayed. Do not treat a low-defense configuration that survives only when ideal early detection, avoidance, and escape succeed as having sufficient survivability for beginners.
[A0102][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL][MOVEMENT_SPEED][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][ESCAPE_ROUTE] In builds for entry-level beginners, do not permit insufficient effective defense against the target PvP environment merely because the build has high mobility or escape capability. Survival through mobility depends on when danger is detected, positioning, terrain and route, remaining resources, enemy pursuit capability, and similar factors; separately evaluate defensive margin that can absorb some error when judgment or operation is delayed or when damage is taken before escape begins.
[A0103][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] For entry-level beginners active in PvP areas, do not make it a standard recommendation to shift armor traits heavily toward performance other than Critical Resistance without first confirming from data that sufficient Critical Resistance has been secured. In particular, do not make survival depend on anticipating enemy attacks in advance and defending appropriately.
[A0104][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] When effective defense is insufficient in a build for an entry-level beginner and the build has no equipment whose primary function is defense other than sustain, strongly consider introducing at least one defensive five-piece set as a major early improvement candidate. Do not assume that adding a single defensive skill can cover the entire deficit.

## A02.2 First Practical Judgments

### A02.30 Universal Beginner Fundamentals

#### Eight-Stage Initial Practice

[A0105][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] In the first stage of practice, make the primary goal that when you notice you are about to die, you stop attacking, first establish ブロック（Block）, and then move into the defensive sequence. Success is not required every time from the start; increase the number of times you notice danger and can switch from block into defense.
[A0106][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 2, make increasing situations where you retain enough emergency スタミナ（Stamina） for one 打破（Break Free） or ロール回避（Roll Dodge） the primary goal. Continuous maintenance is not required from the start; recognize situations where you spent it all and improve in the next match.
[A0107][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 3, aim to use obstacles to reduce the time you continue taking attacks from multiple enemies on open ground. Do not require continuous complete avoidance; treat partial reduction of exposure time as progress.
[A0108][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 4, aim to increase situations where CC can be used as the signal to begin offense.
[A0109][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 5, aim to combine multiple attacks after CC; do not require reproducing a complete combo every time from the start.
[A0110][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 6, aim to try continuing offense when the opponent is forced into healing and reduce unnecessary offense when you are forced into healing.
[A0111][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][GROUP_CONTENT][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING][ESCAPE_ROUTE] In Practice Stage 7, aim to gradually increase situations where you check ally positions and can think about both the direction to escape and the direction back toward allies.
[A0112][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 8, aim to find one bottleneck in your build or operation and improve problems one at a time rather than trying to fix many at once.

#### Learning Opponent State and Major Attacks

[A0113][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE] Once you can perform basic controls, resource management, and battlefield attention without consciously thinking through each of them, make observing the opponent's state the next learning target.
[A0114][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL] Ideally, make learning the major attack effects and operating feel of every class a goal.
[A0115][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE] If learning every class is difficult, at least learn the major attack effects and operating feel of high-threat classes in the current environment.
[A0116][CAUSE] Knowing the major attack effects and operating feel of an opponent's class greatly improves the accuracy of responses when facing that class.

#### Six-Stage Practical Decision Flow

[A0117][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][COND][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] 1. Determine whether “I am about to die right now.” If yes, stop attacking, first establish ブロック（Block）, then proceed as needed to healing, switching to the defensive bar, LoS, creating distance, and similar recovery, and check remaining resources. If not, proceed to the next step.
[A0118][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][DIAG][COND] ② Determine “is the opponent ignoring my attacks and continuing to hit me?” If your attacks are being ignored, suspect insufficient pressure and review buffs, debuffs, Penetration, CC, and combo timing. If the opponent is healing or defending, there is a high chance your offensive turn has begun.
[A0119][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][COND] ③ Determine “are CC and burst ready?” If ready, engage at a moment when the opponent's defense becomes thin. If not, do not forcibly commit all major resources; maintain pressure while preparing.
[A0120][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][COND] ④ Determine “do I have enough スタミナ（Stamina） left for 打破（Break Free） and ロール回避（Roll Dodge）?” If not, do not overpursue; create distance, recover, and readjust. If yes, continue normal decision-making.
[A0121][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][COND] ⑤ Determine “am I being targeted by multiple enemies?” If yes, do not take every attack head-on; split pursuers using obstacles, distance, and CC, and counterattack when one becomes isolated. If not targeted by multiple enemies, continue normal offense and defense.
[A0122][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][COND][RULE] ⑥ Determine “has a long time passed without being able to kill the opponent?” If the fight is stalled, do not fixate on the same opponent; shift attention to another target, allies, or an objective, and treat disengaging as legitimate when the matchup is poor. If it is not stalled, continue the exchange.

#### CC Pressure, Interruption, and Resource Load

[A0123][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] Through attack practice, beginners should aim to gradually increase the situations in which they can move from “use a major attack because I want the kill” toward thinking “land CC and concentrate strong attacks into the short control window”; they do not need to create an appropriate CC opening every time from the start.

#### Offense, Defense, Healing, and Emergency Resources

[A0124][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][PRACTICE][PVP_ASSUMPTION][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][PRIORITY] In defensive design for beginners, do not assume that the enemy player will wait for the questioner's inputs, hesitate to a similar degree, or voluntarily give up attack opportunities; assume an opponent who may input the next attack, CC, or burst faster than the questioner. Therefore prioritize an emergency opening action that provides defensive value immediately after input and continues to function during subsequent decisions, and treat Block as a representative initiation principle.
[A0125][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][PRIORITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] For beginner emergency defense, make the basic practice sequence: recognize danger, first establish ブロック（Block）, perform any necessary bar swap while maintaining it, and proceed to healing, defensive skills, or other recovery as soon as the GCD becomes available.
[A0126][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][PRIORITY] When explaining the defensive order for beginners in danger, do not generate sequences such as “switch to the defensive bar, heal, then ブロック（Block）” or “heal, then block” as standard procedure; show block as the first voluntary defensive action.
[A0127][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] For a beginner's decision to return to offense, do not treat the fact that remaining HP appears sufficiently high as a general reason to stop healing before reaching maximum HP.
[A0128][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][DEFAULT][PRIORITY][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When a beginner returns from a defensive turn to offense, as a rule restore current HP to maximum HP and secure an emergency スタミナ（Stamina） reserve needed for actions such as 打破（Break Free） before deciding to return to offense.
[A0129][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Beginning the next offensive/defensive exchange below maximum HP reduces the health margin available to absorb the next incoming pressure by the amount of unrecovered HP; therefore, do not treat it as the standard state for a beginner to return to offense.
[A0130][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] For a beginner's criterion for ending a defensive turn and returning to offense, do not generate fixed HP thresholds such as “having at least a certain proportion of HP is enough” or “returning to maximum HP is unnecessary.”
[A0131][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not generalize to the standard beginner procedure for ending defense and returning to offense the situational judgment of intermediate or advanced players who read initiative, remaining healing-over-time effects, enemy attack preparation, and similar factors and turn to offense below maximum HP.
[A0132][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES] Even on builds that are not スタミナ（Stamina）-based, treat Stamina as a survival resource used for 打破（Break Free）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, ブロック（Block）, and スプリント（Sprint）.

#### Initial Anti-Stealth Measures

[A0133][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][STEALTH_COUNTER] 光輝く灯明（Radiant Magelight） is particularly valuable for beginners as a strong defensive option against stealth gankers because, while slotted, it can prevent Slam Stun originating from stealth attacks. Do not, however, treat it as an effect that also nullifies inherent CC belonging to a skill itself when that skill is used from stealth.
[A0134][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][RULE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][STEALTH_COUNTER] When using the 暴きの閃光（Revealing Flare） line for anti-stealth purposes, evaluate it less as a tool for searching for an enemy hiding somewhere and more as area denial that creates a zone difficult for enemies to approach or pass through while remaining stealthed; for beginners, treat 残存する閃光（Lingering Flare）, which can create a persistent area, as a strong candidate.

## A02.3 Learning, Verification, and Death Diagnosis

### A02.20 Progressive Learning and Experimentation

#### Core Conclusions to Retrieve First

[A0135][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][PRACTICE][BUILD_GENERAL] Do not treat the versatility a character theoretically possesses as identical to the player's practical versatility; separately verify the execution and decision proficiency needed to use each function in actual combat.
[A0136][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][DIAG][RULE][PRACTICE][RANGE_COST][OPPORTUNITY_COST] When a ranged DD with extensive experience in large-scale シロディール（Cyrodiil） combat struggles in small-group combat, do not immediately reduce the cause to either player skill alone or build performance alone; diagnose proficiency in personal-combat skills and the opportunity cost of range separately.
[A0137][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][RANGE_COST] When the missing skill lies in the perception-action loop of personal combat, do not casually assume that “continuing more large-scale シロディール（Cyrodiil） combat will naturally teach it.” Prioritize high-task-fidelity practice that reproduces at high density the necessary decision frequency, time pressure, and offensive/defensive load.

#### Theoretical Performance and Practical Utilization

[A0138][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF] The build screen shows the upper limit of what a character “can do.”
[A0139][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE] Practical performance changes with buff maintenance, sustained attacks, timing of CC, ブロック（Block）, and ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, switching between front and back bars, use of obstacles, when healing begins, recognition of offensive turns, and remaining resources; even with the same build, the actual utilization rate of its performance changes.
[A0140][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][CAUSE][PRACTICE][BUILD_GENERAL] Even if a character has the performance to handle many use cases, if the player has not learned the execution and decisions required for each use case, only the portions of that versatility for which the player is proficient can be converted reliably into practical combat value.

#### Ranged-DD Experience Scope and Individual-Combat Skill

[A0141][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][PRACTICE][GROUP_CONTENT][RANGE_COST] Continuing to attack as a back-line ranged DD in large-scale field and keep battles in シロディール（Cyrodiil） is a valid PvP job in itself, and repeated operation in that role can develop proficiency in attacking large groups, managing range, and maintaining position relative to the frontline.
[A0142][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][RANGE_COST] Achieving results as a back-line ranged DD in large-scale combat does not necessarily expose lack of proficiency in defense under focus, offensive/defensive transitions, one-on-one combos, and similar personal-combat skills; a player may obtain results in that role while deficiencies in personal-combat skill remain.
[A0143][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][RANGE_COST] To operate a general-purpose ranged DD effectively across both large-scale and small-group combat, become proficient in foundational personal-combat skills that can sustain offense, defense, healing, CC, and resource decisions against one enemy's worth of combat power even when the benefit of range is not working sufficiently.
[A0144][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][RANGE_COST] Candidate environments for practicing the personal-combat skills of a general-purpose ranged DD include duels, small-number direct fights in バトルグラウンド（Battlegrounds）, and local fights in 帝都（Imperial City）, where defense against an individual opponent, offensive/defensive transitions, combos, and resource exchanges can be repeated at higher density than in large-scale back-line combat.
[A0145][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][PRACTICE][BUILD_GENERAL] Copying the equipment and skill configuration of a skilled general-purpose ranged DD does not copy the player's combat judgments, combo precision, handling under focus, and other player skills developed in personal and small-group combat.
[A0146][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][PRACTICE][RANGE_COST][ROLE_BRUISER][OPPORTUNITY_COST] When a ranged DD moving from large-scale シロディール（Cyrodiil） combat into smaller バトルグラウンド（Battlegrounds） or similar content repeatedly struggles in direct fights against melee builds optimized for small-group combat, diagnose separately underdeveloped personal-combat skills and the opportunity cost in frontal offense/defense paid for range, while allowing that both may be causes at the same time.
[A0147][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][PRACTICE][RANGE_COST] Being able to achieve sufficient results through a preferred large-scale ranged role alone can reduce the perceived need to practice missing personal-combat skills and become a learning factor that causes the player to repeat already-strong skills while progress in the skills needed for broader versatility stagnates.
[A0148][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][LIMIT][PRACTICE][EPISTEMIC_SEPARATION] Do not treat long participation in large-scale combat as evidence of equal proficiency in personal combat or bruiser skills. At the same time, do not use deficiencies in small-group combat alone to undervalue skills developed in large-scale combat such as range management, group pressure, and frontline awareness.
[A0149][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][DIAG][RULE][PRACTICE][BUILD_GENERAL] If personal-combat skill is diagnosed as the primary bottleneck, before repeatedly changing equipment, present as an improvement candidate a practice environment that demands that skill at high density.
[A0150][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PRACTICE][RANGE_COST][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] When advising on improvement of a general-purpose ranged DD, distinguish whether the goal is to increase frontal-combat capability as a ranged bruiser, convert range into other value such as skirmishing, off-healing, or debuffing, or maintain specialization for large-scale combat.

#### Diagnosing Practical Differences with the Same Configuration

[A0151][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG] If an opponent using the same equipment and skills alone seems much tougher or deals much higher damage, do not immediately attribute the cause to your overall weakness.
[A0152][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG] When the same equipment or skills produce different practical results, identify differences in utilization rate or timing one by one.

#### Use Duels as a Laboratory

[A0153][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][PRACTICE] Battlegrounds (BG) and シロディール（Cyrodiil） contain simultaneous variables such as player count, terrain, allies, lag, and attacks from other directions, making conditions complex for testing a single element; duels with acquaintances are therefore useful for build adjustment and mechanic verification.
[A0154][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE] For build adjustment, comparative testing that changes only one element at a time is useful.
[A0155][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE] When learning an offensive combo, first repeat it against a Target Dummy until the input order, bar swaps, and timing can be completed reliably, then add mid-sequence changes and cancellations in PvP in response to opponent movement and defensive reactions. Do not treat stable real-player execution through the more variable PvP environment alone as a sound premise while the basic sequence still cannot be reproduced reliably against a dummy.
[A0156][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][BUILD_REGRESSION_CHECK] After changing a build multiple times, re-check not only the changed parts but also whether major functions that worked before the changes have been unintentionally lost.
[A0157][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][FUNCTION_LOSS] When damage, durability, or similar performance worsens for unknown reasons, check not only numerical insufficiency but also whether major roles such as buffs, debuffs, CC, healing, movement, or resource supply have dropped out of the configuration during adjustment.
[A0158][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG] In comparative testing, for defense measure HP loss, the number of actions required to restore HP, and resources required; for offense measure not only maximum damage but whether you forced the opponent to heal, how many times, and how many offensive opportunities you created before resource depletion.

#### Display and Verification

[A0159][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][EX][RULE] Because PvP results can be misread from screen display or subjective feel alone, questions such as “Criticals do not occur,” “the effect does not apply,” or “only a specific skill is weak” should, when possible, be repeatedly checked through combat logs, recording tools, or duels; do not discard the entire build based on a single subjective impression.
[A0160][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND] Even when a build has high theoretical performance or numerical values, if repeated practical use continues to reveal difficulty operating it or realizing that performance, consider that it may not fit your playstyle or execution characteristics.
[A0161][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] When evaluating a build, do not judge it only by theoretical performance or numerical values; also evaluate practical operability, how readily its performance can be realized, and how it feels to use.

### A02.21 Failure and Death Diagnosis

#### Core Conclusions to Retrieve First

[A0162][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][DEATH_REVIEW] Narrowing the death cause to one is not meant to claim that only that cause produced the death; it is a learning procedure for identifying the behavioral bottleneck expected to produce the largest immediate improvement and fixing the next practice target to one item.
[A0163][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][DIAG][DEATH_REVIEW] Even when multiple major mistakes are confirmed in one death, do not treat listing all of them as superior diagnosis; prioritize the one factor most likely to change the result or the current behavioral bottleneck as the next improvement target.
[A0164][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][PRACTICE][DEATH_REVIEW] After the chosen behavioral bottleneck improves, re-analyze subsequent deaths and move to the next dominant bottleneck. Treat repeated focus on one factor at a time as data collection that keeps it interpretable which improvement changed practical results.

#### Prioritizing Causes of Death and Bottleneck Learning

[A0165][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][PRACTICE] When you die, do not end with the general summary “I am weak”; classify the death into one cause category.
[A0166][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][DEATH_REVIEW] When analyzing a death, if possible review the moments immediately before it from a recording instead of deciding the cause only from memory.
[A0167][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][DEATH_REVIEW] When reviewing a recording, inspect position, resources, buffs, defensive actions, and enemy attack tells before determining the death-cause category.
[A0168][EX] Example death-cause categories include failing to 打破（Break Free） CC, スタミナ（Stamina） depletion, delayed healing start, healing-over-time expiration, excessive front-bar time, failure to ブロック（Block） burst, being caught by multiple enemies on open ground, insufficient pressure, overpursuit away from allies, and excessive investment in an opponent you cannot kill.
[A0169][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][LIMIT] Choosing one death cause allows a concrete practice target to be set for the next match, while a diagnosis of “everything was bad” makes it difficult to decide what to fix next.
[A0170][CLAIM][DEATH_REVIEW] Multiple major factors commonly contribute to one death simultaneously; for beginners, delayed 打破（Break Free）, failed ブロック（Block）, delayed healing, unnecessary continued offense, positioning, insufficient resources, and similar problems often overlap in the same death.
[A0171][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM] The ability to understand concrete causes of defeat is a major learning asset that persists even when patches change.

## A02.4 Beginner Build Preparation

### A02.40 Beginner Build Preparation

#### Copying and Evaluating Complete Builds

[A0172][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] When a PvP beginner is comfortable using another person's design, recommend using a completed beginner-oriented build that secures survivability and execution tolerance as the baseline for an entry-level beginner, and a completed experienced-player build suited to the intended use as the baseline for a more proficient beginner.
[A0173][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] When using a completed build as a baseline, reproduce skills, food, potions, traits, equipment slots, and armor weights as faithfully as practical, experience the original build's intent first, and only then move to custom modifications.
[A0174][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][PRACTICE][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][BUILD_GENERAL] Do not treat inability to reproduce a completed build perfectly as making practice with that build impossible.
[A0175][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] Builds have intended use cases, so read the usage explanation and verify that your own use case matches the intended one.
[A0176][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][BUILD_GENERAL] Do not take exaggerated wording by a build author or promotional examples showing only favorable video clips at face value as evidence of the build's general performance.
[A0177][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][DIAG][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] Give weaknesses high priority in build descriptions; if they are not shown, consider them yourself, and do not use the build in use cases where known weaknesses become relevant.
[A0178][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] When using a build from a past patch, verify how well it functions in the current environment.

[A0188][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][LIMIT][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][LIGHTNING_STAFF_HEAVY_ATTACK_BUILD][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Unless there are special circumstances or an explicit preference from the player, do not make Lightning Staff heavy-attack builds a high-priority standard PvP recommendation for entry-level beginners.

## A02.5 Beginner Routes by Role

#### Tank

[A0190][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][SCOPE][OVERRIDE][ROLE_TANK][BEGINNER_TANK][BLOCK] For tanks whose primary defense is continuous ブロック（Block） maintenance, do not make LoS a standard primary defense requirement, especially for beginners; first establish block maintenance, healing, and sustain. [A0191][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][ROLE_TANK][BEGINNER_TANK][SUSTAIN] For beginner tanks, first secure minimum durability sufficient to survive initial pressure, then prepare minimum sustain support that allows continued defense and self-healing before the first real match, obtain enough survival time, and only then learn positioning, ブロック（Block） judgment, and additional support progressively as needed.
[A0192][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][GOAL][ROLE_TANK][BEGINNER_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] If an entry-stage tank can survive for a period while receiving continuous attacks from multiple enemies, evaluate that survival itself as success. [A0193][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_TANK][BEGINNER_TANK][BLOCK][HEAVY_ARMOR][OPPORTUNITY_COST][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] For beginner permanent-block tanks, first establish performance of the primary defense, ブロック（Block）, and do not standardly recommend increasing the share of 中装鎧（Medium Armor） or changing armor traits solely to reduce ロール回避（Roll Dodge） cost when doing so substantially lowers block durability. [A0194][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][ROLE_TANK][BEGINNER_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] As one goal, beginner tanks should gain experience taking part of the enemy's attention and attacks themselves and increase situations in which they can reduce pressure directed at allies.

### A02.H Beginner Healer and Support

#### Healer and Support

[A0195][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PRIORITY][LIMIT][BEGINNER_HEALER][HEALING_GEAR][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] For beginner healer builds intended to operate without belonging to a large group, including PUG situations, prioritize healer equipment whose effects are not limited only to group members. Do not recommend support equipment whose effect targets only group members as equipment for the purpose of support in that use case.

#### Initial Tasks for Beginner Support

[A0196][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When an entry-level beginner has no strong preference for role choice, treat a PvP healer centered on 重装鎧（Heavy Armor） and survivability-oriented equipment, with sustain reinforced as needed, as a strong initial candidate. The role can function even with low offensive performance, self-healing makes it easier to maintain high HP, and dependence on advanced offensive combos or aiming while moving can be reduced, making practical contribution easier to achieve relative to early execution demands.
[A0197][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Do not expand the high recommendation priority of a durable PvP healer for entry-level beginners into an evaluation that the same configuration is also high-performance or optimal at advanced levels.
[A0198][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] For entry-level beginners using a 回復の杖（Restoration Staff） in シロディール（Cyrodiil）, recommend 放射再生（Radiating Regeneration） as the highest-priority candidate if learning only one healing skill first. It can apply effective healing to multiple allies without requiring aim and readily connects battlefield participation to earning 同盟ポイント（Alliance Points／AP）.

#### Beginner Healer Survival and Healing Zone

[A0199][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT] Considering that PvP beginner healers are targeted at high priority by enemies, make it a goal first to gain experience healing while surviving in a build with a certain level of durability.
[A0200][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][COND][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] When a PvP beginner healer fights in situations with many allies, have them try using a position somewhat behind the front line as the default and aim to increase situations in which they can continue healing while surviving there; they do not need to maintain appropriate distance continuously from the start.
[A0201][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][COND][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] When a PvP beginner healer is targeted by enemies in situations with many allies, make it a goal to try actively disengaging with survival as the priority and gradually increase situations in which they can disengage appropriately.
[A0202][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] To some extent, accept reduced healing density or temporary interruption of healing to allies as a tradeoff for healer survival when escaping for survival; do not require beginners from the start to remain alive without ever stopping healing.

#### Beginner Support Continuity and Focus Fire

[A0203][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][DIAG][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT] Beginner healers should review situations after combat where healing did not reach an ally and make it a goal to first identify one major cause they can change next time, in order to avoid unnecessary positioning or build changes caused by misidentifying the reason. Do not require accurate causal diagnosis during combat or simultaneous analysis of multiple causes from the start.
[A0204][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][RULE][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] Beginner healers should aim to gradually learn the judgment not to chase allies unconditionally when those allies keep moving out of their healing range.
[A0205][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][LIMIT][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] Beginner healers should intend to heal allies and help those within a realistically helpable range, while aiming to gradually learn boundaries that prevent being controlled by every action an ally takes.
[A0206][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][DEF][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] Beginner healers should treat the area around themselves as a healing zone, practice using locations near obstacles that are easy to circle as their basic position, and aim to reduce unnecessary movement with experience even if they initially leave position or move too much.
[A0207][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][COND][PRIORITY][BEGINNER_HEALER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] Beginner healers should understand that they may make a large movement when required for survival and aim to gradually judge that movement not as permanently abandoning allies but as avoiding death now so healing can resume several seconds later.

### A02.B Beginner Bruiser

#### Bruiser

[A0208][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BEGINNER_BRUISER] When an entry-level beginner uses a bruiser, treat as an initial candidate a configuration based mainly on 重装鎧（Heavy Armor） with at least one five-piece set whose primary function is defense / survivability and at least one five-piece set whose primary function is offensive performance. [A0210][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BEGINNER_BRUISER] For an entry-level beginner bruiser unfamiliar even with PvE, treat securing a source of 強固（強）（Major Resolve） as an effectively mandatory initial recommendation. [A0211][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][DEF][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_BRUISER][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] Beginners should understand that bruisers can be pure-role or hybrid-role builds and set the long-term goal of first gaining basic front-line combat experience with a pure bruiser before developing into hybrids; do not require a fixed proficiency level from the start.
