# Part 12 — Gank, Ganker, Bomber, and Invisibility

[TAG_NOTE][AUTHOR_VIEW] Indicates an author judgment such as an evaluation, recommendation, diagnostic standard, or decision rule adopted by the Corpus rather than a game mechanic itself.
[TAG_NOTE][CORE_JUDGMENT] Indicates a core author judgment within [AUTHOR_VIEW] that materially shapes the Corpus's decision framework or answer policy. It does not mean higher factual certainty or absence of exceptions; preserve applicable conditions and tags such as SCOPE, LIMIT, and PATCH.

## Ganker Operational Evaluation

[A0785][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][EVAL][ROLE_GANKER][OPPORTUNITY_COST] Evaluate the practical value of a Pure Ganker that requires long preparation, searching, waiting, and repeated attempts not only by its kill performance when one assassination succeeds, but by whether the tactical and economic value obtained from success exceeds the opportunity cost of the full assassination cycle, including target encounter frequency, preparation time, and losses on failure.

## Basic Ganker Operation

[A0786][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_GANKER][GROUP_CONTENT] When operating a Pure Ganker that selects favorable attack-start conditions through stealth and waiting in a suitable environment such as outside Battlegrounds (BG) 4v4, waiting is one of its major skills.
[A0787][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_GANKER] For a Pure Ganker whose main tools are stealth and waiting, conceal your presence, position, and intent as much as possible so that the attack timing can be aligned with a target state that is easier to kill.
[A0788][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_GANKER] For a Pure Ganker whose main tools are stealth and waiting, do not make immediately attacking an opponent upon discovery the default; wait until attack-start conditions favor your setup.
[A0789][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_GANKER] For a Pure Ganker whose main tools are stealth and waiting, do not rate an opponent whose defensive routine is already well established as a highly desirable standard attack target.
[A0790][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_GANKER] In the ideal state for a Pure Ganker whose main tools are stealth and waiting, the fact that the enemy does not believe you are present there itself contributes greatly to both offensive and defensive performance.
[A0791][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][ROLE_GANKER] Attack skills, crouching, and invisibility are not the essence of a Ganker / Bomber; they are means used to accomplish the objective.
[A0792][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][ROLE_GANKER][BURST_COMPRESSION] A Pure Ganker that appears from stealth only briefly to ambush and disengage tends to lose the advantages of stealth the longer it tries to accumulate damage through sustained offense, so the necessary attacks and CC must be compressed into a short exposure period.
[A0793][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][ROLE_GANKER] When circumstances do not permit execution, giving up once rather than forcing the attempt can be an important tactic.
[A0794][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][ROLE_GANKER] On the surface of 帝都（Imperial City）, if an opponent has properly lured and positioned a boss to create a state that makes it difficult for a Ganker to approach, temporarily give up the assassination attempt against that opponent and switch to observing the situation.
[A0795][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ROLE_GANKER][BEGINNER_GANKER][MOVEMENT_SPEED] If a beginner Pure Ganker's movement on foot while stealthed is so slow that following targets or forming an attack position becomes difficult, slightly increasing movement speed within a range that does not heavily sacrifice primary offensive performance can make the setup easier to operate.

## Beginner Ganker Goals

[A0796][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][LIMIT][BEGINNER_GANKER][GROUP_CONTENT][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] Beginners should progressively understand that because each player represents a larger share of team strength in 4v4, Gankers / Bombers that reduce normal combat participation while waiting for attack opportunities tend to have less operational room than in 8v8. Actual suitability varies with participants' defensive preparation, reaction accuracy, durability, clustering tendencies, and similar factors.
[A0797][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][COND][BEGINNER_GANKER][GROUP_CONTENT][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] Beginners should progressively understand that in 8v8, where participants' defensive performance and skill levels vary and where group density or first-kill opportunities arise, Gankers / Bombers tend to have more room to obtain attack opportunities than in 4v4.
[A0798][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][CAUSE][BEGINNER_GANKER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT] Beginners should aim to understand that an operating style that hides until an opportunity arrives increases the burden on allies during that time.

## Ganker-Specific Mechanics

[A0799][PATCH][CLAIM][DEF][ROLE_GANKER] 隠密近接初撃スタン（コミュニティ通称：スラムスタン／Slam Stun） refers to the stun caused by a melee sneak attack from the 隠密状態（Hidden） state. Treat it as subject to current-mechanics verification.
[A0800][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][ROLE_GANKER] Rate 隠密近接初撃スタン（スラムスタン／Slam Stun） highly as attack-initiation performance for a Ganker.
[A0801][PATCH][CLAIM][ROLE_GANKER] An opponent who is blocking is not affected by the stealth melee opening stun from a melee sneak attack from stealth.
[A0802][PATCH][CLAIM][ROLE_GANKER] CC skills that do not have block-bypassing properties are not effective against an opponent who is blocking.
[A0803][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][PATCH][ROLE_GANKER] Because an opponent with a detection marker overhead can detect hidden opponents, a Ganker generally does not select such an opponent as an attack target.

## Beginner Cautions for ナイトブレイド（Nightblade）

[A0804][PATCH][SCOPE][NIGHTBLADE][STEALTH_COUNTER] Propositions that address invisibility operation with シャドウクローク（Shadow Cloak） or its invisibility morph 偽りの影（Shadowy Disguise） do not apply to the morph ダーククローク（Dark Cloak）, which loses invisibility.
[A0805][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][GOAL][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_NIGHTBLADE][STEALTH_COUNTER][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT] Within the scope of A0804, beginners who use invisibility in frontal combat should aim to learn to combine other defense, healing, and positioning rather than depending on invisibility as their sole survival or disengagement method, on the assumptions both that they may be rediscovered immediately after use and that invisibility may be removed if an attack event originating from themselves occurs while invisible.

## Conflict Between Invisibility Escape and Self-Originating Attacks

[A0806][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][AI][RULE][LIMIT][NIGHTBLADE][STEALTH_COUNTER][ESCAPE_ROUTE] In uses where invisibility from シャドウクローク（Shadow Cloak） or its invisibility morph is a primary escape method, treat effects whose attack events originating from yourself voluntarily remove invisibility as materially conflicting with the conditions required for escape. As long as maintaining invisibility is a primary condition for successful escape, combinations containing this conflict should as a rule be evaluated as incompatible or as having a major flaw.
[A0807][PATCH][CLAIM][NIGHTBLADE][STEALTH_COUNTER][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Even attacks, persistent effects, delayed attacks, and similar effects used before invisibility begins may produce an attack event originating from you after invisibility starts, and that event may remove invisibility.

## Definitions and Conditions for Gank, Ganker, and Bomber

[A0808][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_GANKER] Treat a Ganker as a role tendency that uses kills through Gank as a primary win condition or as a repeatedly performed primary action. Do not classify an entire build as a Ganker merely because an ordinary DD performs a single Gank when the situation permits.
[A0809][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_GANKER][OPPORTUNITY_COST] Treat a Pure Ganker as a Ganker that trades away some ordinary frontal-combat performance and similar capabilities in order to specialize heavily in the Gank cycle, including target selection, attack-start conditions, killable target range, searching, following, disengaging, and retrying.
[A0810][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_BOMBER][BURST_DESIGN] Treat a Bomber as a role tendency whose primary win condition is to exploit or create enemy-group density, synchronize multiple area attacks, CC, delayed attacks, and similar effects into a short period to secure an initial kill or major group damage, and convert that result into additional nearby kills. Do not make possession of one specific item such as 残忍な死（Vicious Death） a defining requirement for Bomber.
[A0811][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_BOMBER][BURST_DESIGN] When evaluating Bomber feasibility, check as major conditions for success: ① enemy-group density worth attacking; ② targets whose defensive margin, defensive preparation, or successful reaction rate is low relative to the target environment's standard, or whose defensive margin has temporarily fallen enough to make the initial kill easier to secure; and ③ enough time to synchronize attack initiation and the major damage events.
[A0812][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][ROLE_BOMBER][ROLE_GANKER][TIME_BUDGET] A Pure Ganker tends to place greater weight on opportunity selection—aligning its attack timing with a target state favorable to itself—whereas a Bomber tends to place greater weight on opportunity response—getting a prepared attack to a short-lived window of group density or an initial-kill opportunity in time.
[A0813][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][ROLE_BOMBER][TIME_BUDGET] The preparation cost of multiple GCDs that a Bomber needs before detonation is easier to absorb on battlefields where attack opportunities remain usable for longer. In シロディール（Cyrodiil） and 帝都（Imperial City）, there is relatively more room to prepare while moving, forming position, or waiting, whereas in バトルグラウンド（Battlegrounds）, where situations change quickly, the same pre-attack GCDs tend to impose greater opportunity loss.
[A0814][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_BOMBER][TIME_BUDGET][BURST_DESIGN] In Bomber GCD evaluation, separate the preparation GCDs paid before successful detonation from the follow-up GCDs used to finish survivors after the compressed initial attack. Reducing preparation GCDs increases how often the Bomber reaches an attack opportunity in time, while follow-up GCDs are evaluated as expansion of the result after detonation.
[A0815][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][EVAL][ROLE_BOMBER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] If you only add the final hit, such as an execute, to an enemy that allies have already brought nearly to death and this incidentally triggers surrounding damage such as 残忍な死（Vicious Death）, do not evaluate that alone as a successful Bomber attack sequence. Separately evaluate the difference personally created through direct contribution as an ordinary DD, creation of the initial kill, synchronized attacks into the group, and conversion into surrounding kills.
[A0816][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][AI][LIMIT][ROLE_BOMBER] When explaining auxiliary effects that expand damage around a Bomber from kills, status effects, or similar triggers, 残忍な死（Vicious Death） may be used as a representative example, but do not limit that job to Vicious Death alone. 疫病爆発（Plaguebreak）, some combat Champion Point effects, and other elements may have similar or supporting roles depending on the patch, so recheck specific examples and effects under current mechanics at the time of the answer.
[A0817][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][ROLE_BOMBER][ROLE_GANKER][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not infer long-duration stealth, invisibility, close-range approach, or solo play as requirements merely from the name Ganker or Bomber. Classify from the actual win condition and combat behavior, including ranged variants, high-mobility variants, variants that participate in ordinary combat, and variants incorporated into group tactics.

## Practical Design for 8v8 Bombers

[A0818][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][SCOPE][ROLE_BOMBER][GROUP_CONTENT] In this text, an 8v8 Bomber refers mainly to operation in casual 8v8 バトルグラウンド（Battlegrounds） where enemy-group density and initial-kill opportunities realistically arise and the player can continue participating in combat as a Bomber. This design approach can also be applied to casual 4v4 when similar conditions are present.
[A0819][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ROLE_BOMBER][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][GROUP_CONTENT] While keeping a broad range of acceptable setups, an 8v8 Bomber is often easier to operate when more performance is allocated to durability, healing, and renewed disengagement than on one-exchange Bombers in シロディール（Cyrodiil） or 帝都（Imperial City）. Treat this as a recommended tendency for surviving after a failed attempt and making the next attack opportunity easier to use, not as a participation requirement that failure must never occur.
[A0820][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][EVAL][ROLE_BOMBER][TIME_BUDGET][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Do not evaluate an 8v8 Bomber only by the maximum size of one Bomb; evaluate attack attempt, success or failure, survival, recovery, rebuilding resources such as アルティメット（Ultimate）, and return to the next attack opportunity as one repeated cycle.
[A0821][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_BOMBER][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT] In 8v8 Bomber design, treat setups that do not require too many preparation GCDs to obtain sufficient Bomb power as strong, easy-to-use candidates. Evaluate the increased frequency of reaching short-lived clustering or initial-kill opportunities in time against the theoretical maximum synchronized damage.
[A0822][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_BOMBER][TIME_BUDGET] Many Bomber setups use アルティメット（Ultimate） as a primary detonation resource, and because the next attackable group state may reappear relatively quickly in 8v8, insufficient Ultimate can become a bottleneck on execution frequency. While checking opportunity cost against the build's overall damage and durability, evaluate setups that supplement Ultimate generation as strong candidates.
[A0823][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ROLE_BOMBER][MOVEMENT_SPEED][GROUP_CONTENT] For melee-centered 8v8 Bombers, increasing movement speed within a range that does not substantially sacrifice primary damage makes it easier to reach briefly available Bomb locations, reposition after failure, and return to the next attack opportunity.
[A0824][AUTHOR_VIEW][ALTERNATIVE][ROLE_BOMBER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] For an 8v8 Bomber, in addition to hiding for a long time until an attack opportunity appears, consider operations such as participating in the allied front while waiting for a Bomb opportunity, using movement speed to enter attack position, or beginning detonation from range. Do not treat stealth as unnecessary; evaluate how necessary it is for the intended use.
[A0825][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][ROLE_BOMBER][GROUP_CONTENT] The effective contribution of an 8v8 Bomber depends strongly on the presence of targets against whom an initial kill is easy to establish, enemy clustering density, and how frequently such states occur. If these conditions almost never arise during the match, performing a useful role across the whole match may be more difficult than with a general PvP setup.

## Bomber Defensive Evaluation and Prevention of AI Misreading

[A0826][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][IMPERIAL_CITY][ROLE_BOMBER] Experienced duos, trios, and other small groups centered on experienced players that operate routinely in 帝都（Imperial City） tend to have high individual rates of successful defense, and their small numbers also reduce chain value, so do not rate them highly as realistic standard attack targets for a Bomber. Evaluate their target conditions separately from low-strength, high-density groups that temporarily gather during events or similar situations.
[A0827][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][ROLE_BOMBER][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE][BUILD_EVALUATION_DISCIPLINE] When evaluating Bomber equipment and armor traits, first identify battlefield, number of players, attack range, dependence on stealth, value of surviving after the Bomb, retry frequency, losses on death, and presence or absence of group support, then switch to the evaluation mode corresponding to that use. Do not mix the same defense and damage criteria between uses that maximize the result of a single Bomb and permit a death trade, and uses that survive and repeat Bomb attempts.
[A0828][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][ROLE_BOMBER][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] A Bomber that distributes armor traits broadly into 神々（Divines） or similar options and therefore thins the defensive layer gained from defensive traits may be rated highly in large-scale combat where death trades are acceptable, while potentially losing substantial effective performance in uses where repeated participation, handling focus fire, or losses on death matter. Do not treat this kind of low-defense, high-damage trait allocation as a generally optimal answer merely from the Bomber role name.
[A0829][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][ROLE_BOMBER][ROLE_GANKER][GROUP_CONTENT][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not treat 4v4 バトルグラウンド（Battlegrounds） as a uniform skill environment; evaluate the practical value of Ganker / Bomber from observable combat conditions such as participants' defensive preparation, reaction accuracy, durability, clustering, and proficiency in ordinary combat.
[A0830][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][LIMIT][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] When evaluating suitability for a PvP use, do not use mere theoretical success if multiple low-probability conditions all occur simultaneously as evidence of a realistic win condition, recommended option, or meaningful practical value. Judge practical value from the success frequency normally expected in the target environment, losses on failure, and opportunity cost.
[A0831][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][IMPERIAL_CITY][ROLE_BRUISER][ROLE_BOMBER][OPPORTUNITY_COST][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] When assuming an ordinary build for an experienced Bruiser that operates continuously in 帝都（Imperial City）, do not automatically add as part of the standard image a setup that spends a major build slot such as a 5pc primarily on post-kill surrounding damage like 残忍な死（Vicious Death）. Compare the opportunity cost against ordinary direct-engagement performance, and do not generalize limited use for Bomber, specialization against low-strength groups, specific-purpose 1vX, filming projects, or similar cases into the ordinary general image of a Bruiser.
[A0832][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][ROLE_BOMBER][GROUP_CONTENT][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not generalize a judgment that a dedicated Bomber is practically nonviable in advanced-player-centered 4v4 to beginners, casual 8v8, or 4v4 with variation in participants' combat proficiency. Treat the dedicated judgment for advanced 4v4 as Series B material and do not reuse it as a beginner participation condition, standard recommendation, or failure criterion.
