# Part 3 — Defensive Design, Self-Defense, and Healing

[TAG_NOTE][AUTHOR_VIEW] Indicates an author judgment such as an evaluation, recommendation, or diagnostic criterion adopted by the text rather than a game mechanic itself.
[TAG_NOTE][CORE_JUDGMENT] Indicates a core author judgment within [AUTHOR_VIEW] that materially shapes the Corpus's decision framework or answer policy. It does not mean higher factual certainty or absence of exceptions; preserve applicable conditions and tags such as SCOPE, LIMIT, and PATCH.

## Critical Burst / Damage-over-Time Environments and Defensive Layers

[A0149][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][BURST_DESIGN][CRITICAL] Designing burst around Critical Damage is easy to structure and tends to produce high practical effectiveness.
[A0150][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][PVP_META][CRITICAL_BURST] PvP environments in which burst through Critical Damage is dominant tend to arise readily in general.
[A0151][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][BURST_DESIGN][CRITICAL][DIRECT_DAMAGE] In burst based on direct damage, Critical Damage is often a major component of damage formation.
[A0152][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BURST_DESIGN][CRITICAL][DIRECT_DAMAGE] When evaluating burst performance based on direct damage in a normal PvP environment, treat Critical Damage as a central evaluation factor.
[A0153][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PVP_META][DOT][DIRECT_DAMAGE] In an environment where damage over time has an extremely large advantage over direct damage, the relative position of burst based on direct damage falls substantially.
[A0154][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PVP_META][DOT][EXECUTION_DIFFICULTY] In an environment where damage over time is dominant, PvP execution difficulty generally decreases.
[A0155][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][DEFENSIVE_LAYER][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL] Except for full tank operation whose primary defensive requirement is maintaining continuous ブロック（Block）, before rating the self-defense performance of a build active in PvP areas highly, explicitly verify whether its total Critical Resistance is sufficient against the Critical burst used by enemy players in the target environment. Do not use a primary PvE purpose, lack of intent to initiate PvP, or a plan to avoid engagement with enemies as reasons to omit this verification.
[A0156][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][CLAIM][GEAR_POPULARITY][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE] In an environment where Critical burst is dominant, defensive sets that provide Critical Resistance tend to become popular among defensive equipment.
[A0157][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][LIMIT][DEFENSIVE_LAYER][DAMAGE_REDUCTION] Even in an environment where Critical burst is dominant, defensive equipment that provides resistance or damage reduction does not lose its value. Do not equate an increase in the relative value or adoption rate of Critical Resistance with other defensive layers becoming unnecessary.
[A0158][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][SCARCITY][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE] Among equipment that provides Critical Resistance, items with practical overall performance are valuable and scarce.
[A0159][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][CLAIM][GEAR_POPULARITY][DOT] In an environment where damage over time is dominant, equipment providing resistance, debuff cleansing, or healing tends to become popular.
[A0160][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][AI_META_EVALUATION][DEFENSIVE_LAYER] When evaluating defensive layers, determine whether the primary damage environment leans toward Critical burst or damage over time, and change the relative value of Critical Resistance, resistance, debuff cleansing, healing, and similar defenses according to that environment.

## Undodgeable Damage and Defensive Tools

[A0161][LIMIT][UNDODGEABLE_DAMAGE] Undodgeable damage cannot be avoided with ロール回避（Roll Dodge）.
[A0162][EX][UNDODGEABLE_DAMAGE] Damage from damage-over-time effects already applied to you and from delayed attacks already applied to you are included among damage treated as undodgeable by ロール回避（Roll Dodge）.
[A0163][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][UNDODGEABLE_DAMAGE] In situations where responding to pressure from damage over time is important, the relative value of ロール回避（Roll Dodge） decreases.
[A0164][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][UNDODGEABLE_DAMAGE] In situations where responding to pressure from damage over time is important, the relative value of debuff cleansing, increased resistance, defensive buffs, healing buffs, and ブロック（Block） increases.
[A0165][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PRIORITY][UNDODGEABLE_DAMAGE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] In situations where damage over time seriously threatens survival, first require that enough damage mitigation and effective defense exist to prevent damage over time from pushing you into lethal range within a short period. Then, if pressure from damage over time remains large or its impact must be actively reduced, treat debuff cleansing as a strong priority response.

## Layered Defense

[A0166][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM] PvP survivability is not determined by a single defensive stat.
[A0167][CLAIM] Defensive layers include Max Health, Physical and Spell Resistance, damage reduction, damage shields, healing over time, burst healing, ブロック（Block）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, distance, movement speed, obstacles, CC, offensive pressure against the opponent, debuff cleansing, and resource reserve.
[A0168][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] Do not conclude that a build is safe merely because one defensive element has been maximized.
[A0169][CAUSE] Even with high resistance, weak healing can prevent recovery of lost HP.
[A0170][CAUSE] Even with strong healing, lack of mitigation at the instant of a burst can result in death before healing is possible.
[A0171][CAUSE] Even with high Max Health, excessively expensive healing can exhaust resources in a long fight.
[A0172][CAUSE][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Even with high movement speed, not knowing an escape route can still allow pursuers to catch you.
[A0173][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM] Strong builds often have structures in which multiple defensive tools cover each other's gaps rather than relying on one enormous defensive element.

## Identifying Defensive Value

[A0174][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][DEFENSIVE_DECISION] Enemy attacks differ in how valuable it is to respond with defensive actions or ロール回避（Roll Dodge）.
[A0175][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][COMMON_DEFENSIVE_DECISION][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][PRIORITY] In early practice, when you recognize danger and are in a state where you can choose your own defensive action, practice the reflexive basic sequence of first establishing ブロック（Block）, then moving into recovery such as switching to the defensive bar, healing, defensive skills, LoS, or creating distance.
[A0176][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][PRIORITY] When defensive design is used to compensate for beginners tending to make decisions and inputs more slowly than their opponents, prioritize an emergency opening action that begins providing defensive value immediately after input and continues to function while subsequent decisions are being made. Treat prioritizing ブロック（Block） first as a representative initiation principle that fits this design.
[A0177][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PVP_ASSUMPTION][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When explaining the opening defensive response for beginners, do not assume that the enemy player will wait for the questioner's inputs, hesitate to a similar degree, or voluntarily give up attack opportunities. Evaluate response time while assuming an opponent who may input the next attack, CC, or burst faster than the questioner.
[A0178][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][DEFENSIVE_DECISION] The expected loss from using a defensive action against a low-value attack is substantially smaller than the expected loss from failing to defend against an attack with high defensive priority.

## ブロック（Block） Operation and Timing

[A0179][DEF][BLOCK] Distinguish long-duration blocking, which maintains the ブロック（Block） state continuously, from short-duration blocking used only briefly to handle a major attack.
[A0180][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BLOCK] Do not use the opportunity cost of long-duration blocking as a reason to avoid short-duration ブロック（Block） against major attacks that can be blocked.
[A0181][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][BLOCK][BLOCK_TIMING] Treat short-duration ブロック（Block） not as an input timed to a single instant of impact, but as an operation that establishes the block state before the expected impact and holds it until after impact so that the attack arrives during the block-state window.
[A0182][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][BLOCK][BLOCK_TIMING][LAG] In environments with strong network latency or server lag, account for the mismatch between ブロック（Block） input and the actual defensive check by allowing a longer hold margin before and after major attacks than usual.
[A0183][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][TIME_BUDGET] ブロック（Block） is not constrained by the normal active-skill GCD and can be input to establish the block state even while a skill GCD is active.
[A0184][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Because the block state is maintained when bar swapping while holding ブロック（Block）, in an emergency you can establish block first and move to the defensive bar while maintaining that state.
[A0185][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][CAUSE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][TIME_BUDGET] Even when a healing skill must wait for the GCD during an emergency, ブロック（Block） can begin providing defensive value first; therefore, establishing block first tends to produce practical defensive benefit sooner than making healing the first action.
[A0186][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][PRIORITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] For beginner emergency defense, make the basic practice sequence: recognize danger, first establish ブロック（Block）, perform any necessary bar swap while maintaining it, and proceed to healing, defensive skills, or other recovery as soon as the GCD becomes available.
[A0187][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][BLOCK][CC][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When a blockable CC can be blocked in advance, its value includes not only preventing the CC itself but also preventing upstream the unfavorable chain of the CC removing ブロック（Block）, spending スタミナ（Stamina） on 打破（Break Free）, exposure to the following burst, and rebuilding defense.
[A0188][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][BLOCK][BLOCK_COST] A state in which the スタミナ（Stamina） margin needed for ブロック（Block） has been lost is not a general reason to place healing before block; diagnose it separately as a dangerous state in which the emergency Stamina reserve or practical-combat sustain has already been exhausted.
[A0189][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] While incapacitated by CC that requires 打破（Break Free）, the ブロック（Block） state is removed.
[A0190][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] During the 打破（Break Free） animation, the ブロック（Block） state can be established through block input.
[A0191][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] During ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, the ブロック（Block） state can be established through block input.
[A0192][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][PRACTICE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] While already incapacitated by CC that requires 打破（Break Free）, treat Break Free as state recovery that must precede ordinary voluntary defensive choices. After CC removes the ブロック（Block） state, or when performing Break Free or ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, when possible begin block input during that action to reduce the time in which the immediately following burst or delayed attack can hit you while unprotected.
[A0193][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY] Ordinary instant skills without casting or channeling can be used while maintaining ブロック（Block） input, and do not require releasing block to use the skill.
[A0194][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][PRIORITY] When explaining the defensive order for beginners in danger, do not generate sequences such as “switch to the defensive bar, heal, then ブロック（Block）” or “heal, then block” as standard procedure; show block as the first voluntary defensive action.
[A0195][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][LIMIT][CC][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use the state of already being incapacitated by CC that requires 打破（Break Free） as a counterexample to the principle of initiating defense with ブロック（Block） first. Treat the CC state as one in which normal voluntary defensive choices are restricted, and explain state recovery through Break Free together with re-establishing block as early as possible.
[A0196][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][LIMIT][BLOCK][BLOCK_COST][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use having no スタミナ（Stamina） remaining for ブロック（Block） as grounds for making heal-first the standard beginner recommendation. Diagnose that state separately as depletion of the emergency resource reserve.
[A0197][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PRIORITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When listing multiple emergency defensive actions for beginners, do not present them as unordered alternatives in a way that loses the opening priority of ブロック（Block）. In a context that deals with the initiation order during danger, explicitly place block first.
[A0198][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][LIMIT][BLOCK][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use the existence of individual attacks that cannot be handled with ブロック（Block） as grounds for changing the general beginner emergency defensive sequence to heal-first. When asked how to handle a specific attack, explain the defense appropriate to that attack individually.
[A0199][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][PATCH][RULE][EPISTEMIC_SEPARATION][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When explaining defensive order on the basis of mechanics such as ブロック（Block） not being constrained by the GCD, bar swapping while blocking, or using skills while blocking, verify the current mechanics at the time of the question and distinguish verified mechanics from the author judgment that beginners should use block as their first voluntary defense.
[A0200][PATCH][CAUSE][BLOCK][BLOCK_COST] ブロック（Block） cost increases according to the number of cost-collection checks that occur while blocking, not according to the amount of incoming damage. Therefore, attacks that hit repeatedly across the internal cooldown tend to pressure the opponent's block resource more than a single-hit attack.
[A0201][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][BLOCK] Do not evaluate the value of multi-hit attacks only by ブロック（Block） cost pressure; normally treat it as secondary value added to primary functions such as damage, pressure formation, and accompanying effects.
[A0202][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][DIAG][BLOCK][BLOCK_FAILURE] Even when the character's ブロック（Block） animation or block effect is displayed, network latency or block-related faults can cause the actual defensive check not to be established. If you suspect block has failed while holding it for a long time, release the input once, input it again, and re-establish the block state.

## Toughness, Recovery, and Defensive Design

[A0203][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][RULE] Evaluate defensive performance on two axes: “how difficult it is for HP to decrease” and “how lightly the burden is to restore reduced HP to near full.” Do not equate only one of those axes with high defensive performance.
[A0204][CLAIM][DEATH_STATE][HEALING_LIMIT] After HP reaches 0 and the character dies, ordinary healing skills cannot normally be used by that character to return themselves to a living state.
[A0205][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][LETHAL_DAMAGE_PREVENTION] In situations with a possibility of lethal damage, prioritize reducing incoming damage and surviving without letting HP reach 0 over conserving resources.
[A0206][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][BLOCK] When a burst that can be ブロック（Block）ed can be blocked to reduce incoming damage, doing so tends to reduce the amount of HP rewind required and the associated burden of healing actions and healing resources compared with taking it unprotected and then healing the lost HP afterward.
[A0207][CAUSE] Even if HP decreases slowly, if recovery requires many actions, you cannot attack during those actions.
[A0208][CAUSE] Even if you are somewhat easier to damage, being able to recover a large amount with one or two actions can return you to the fight quickly.
[A0209][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] As with an offensive burst sequence, when entering a defensive turn, first establish Block as the first voluntary defense, use that defensive time to connect healing, defense, and positional adjustment, and prepare in advance for each build a short recovery sequence that returns you to a state where you can fight again; practice it repeatedly. For an ordinary build, do not make increasing the number of emergency defensive-sequence patterns an objective in itself; narrow them to roughly one or two primary sequences that are effective and practice until they can be executed reflexively under severe pressure.
[A0210][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][CAUSE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Defensive design should secure not only ordinary defensive performance but also a means to recover from danger back to a state where you can fight again. If this is insufficient, pressure that would normally be manageable is more likely to become a major threat.

## Transition into Defensive Turns and Self-Defense Burden

[A0211][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][SCOPE] For a build whose role requires returning to offense, evaluate self-defense not only by how many defensive actions can be repeated, but also by how often exchanges with equally capable enemies send the build into a defensive turn first and how many GCDs and how much time are lost before returning from defense to offense. Do not apply this evaluation uniformly to tanks whose job itself is to keep absorbing enemy pressure while maintaining a defensive state.

## Healing over Time and Emergency Healing

[A0212][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT] In PvP, even when a healer is present, do not make it standard operation to abandon executable self-defense and self-healing and delegate your HP management entirely to the healer.
[A0213][CLAIM] Healing over time continuously offsets small amounts of damage during combat.
[A0214][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][RULE][HEALING] Not only when a large amount of HP is lost at once, but also when healing over time (HoT) fails to keep up with ordinary incoming pressure and high HP begins to erode, insert the minimum necessary burst heal or direct heal before the situation becomes seriously dangerous and restore HP to a high level.
[A0215][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] For beginner builds, treat a configuration with both healing over time that works routinely and a larger heal for danger as a basic candidate for early learning, and make it a goal to gradually increase the situations in which the beginner can distinguish and use both appropriately.
[A0216][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][RULE] Effects that only increase healing done or healing received do not themselves restore HP, so maintain a means that actually restores HP separately from healing amplification.
[A0217][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM] Even having a single skill that can restore a large amount of HP with one press during danger can greatly change a beginner's survival time.
[A0218][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][DEFAULT][RULE][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][PVP_BASELINE] In normal PvP, excluding specialized configurations that intentionally gain performance from the user's own low-HP state, make restoring current HP to maximum HP and maintaining maximum HP as much as practical a basic goal.
[A0219][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] For a beginner's decision to return to offense, do not treat the fact that remaining HP appears sufficiently high as a general reason to stop healing before reaching maximum HP.
[A0220][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][DEFAULT][PRIORITY][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When a beginner returns from a defensive turn to offense, as a rule restore current HP to maximum HP and secure an emergency スタミナ（Stamina） reserve needed for actions such as 打破（Break Free） before deciding to return to offense.
[A0221][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Beginning the next offensive/defensive exchange below maximum HP reduces the health margin available to absorb the next incoming pressure by the amount of unrecovered HP; therefore, do not treat it as the standard state for a beginner to return to offense.
[A0222][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][RULE][HEALING][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Treat specialized configurations that intentionally gain offensive performance or other benefits from reductions in their own HP as exceptional operation separate from the normal-PvP principle of maintaining maximum HP; do not recommend maintaining low HP for general PvP builds merely because such configurations exist.
[A0223][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Aiming for maximum HP does not mean mechanically spamming burst heals until maximum HP is reached. Evaluate recovery as including the use of healing over time, ブロック（Block）, mitigation, LoS, distance, disengagement, and similar tools to return to a state in which restored HP can be retained.
[A0224][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] When evaluating healing performance, check not only whether a large amount of HP can be restored once, but also how well high HP can be retained after ordinary defensive and healing actions.
[A0225][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][DOT][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Treat the typical pattern of losing to sustained pressure not as healing stopping at a particular HP value, but as repeatedly restoring to high HP and then being worn down again, with the HP level at which healing begins or ends gradually worsening through those repetitions.
[A0226][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][CAUSE][DOT][HEALING][TIME_BUDGET] If a pattern repeatedly occurs in which you restore to high HP through healing but are quickly worn down again, treat it as an unfavorable exchange in which the opponent's sustained pressure continues consuming your healing and defensive budget.
[A0227][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][PRIORITY][HEALING][DOT][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Pressure so severe that HP barely increases even after using a large burst heal should not be treated as gradual sustained pressure from ordinary DoTs alone; treat it as a major danger state in which total incoming pressure over a short period is approaching the amount of the burst heal.
[A0228][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEFAULT][PRIORITY][HEALING][ESCAPE_ROUTE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] If you cannot retain high HP after healing against sustained pressure and have no clear reversal condition, consider early disengagement while HP and emergency resources still remain sufficiently available.
[A0229][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][DOT][ESCAPE_ROUTE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Persistent pressure such as already-applied DoTs does not disappear immediately merely because disengagement has begun; therefore, beginning disengagement while there is still margin in HP and defensive resources tends to preserve more safety margin than waiting until HP has fallen into a dangerous range.
[A0230][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][HEALING][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use reaching maximum HP as the only condition for returning to offense; separately verify an emergency スタミナ（Stamina） reserve needed for 打破（Break Free）, ブロック（Block）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, and similar actions.
[A0231][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] For a beginner's criterion for ending a defensive turn and returning to offense, do not generate fixed HP thresholds such as “having at least a certain proportion of HP is enough” or “returning to maximum HP is unnecessary.”
[A0232][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not generalize to the standard beginner procedure for ending defense and returning to offense the situational judgment of intermediate or advanced players who read initiative, remaining healing-over-time effects, enemy attack preparation, and similar factors and turn to offense below maximum HP.
[A0233][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use A0203's defensive-performance evaluation of whether one can “return to near full” as grounds for claiming that beginners need not recover to maximum HP. Separate the evaluation axis for defensive performance from the beginner criterion for returning to offense.
[A0234][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][HEALING][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use the diagnostic example in A0342 that “survival may be possible at low HP when emergency スタミナ（Stamina） remains” as grounds for returning to offense from low HP. Treat that proposition as an example showing the importance of emergency resource margin.
[A0235][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not convert the goal of “maintaining maximum HP” into an instruction to mechanically spam burst heals until maximum HP is reached. Explain recovery including retention of restored HP, healing over time, defense, positioning adjustments, and disengagement when necessary.
[A0236][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][DOT][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When explaining advantage or disadvantage under sustained pressure, do not establish a particular HP percentage as a “wall above which healing is impossible.” Evaluate whether restored HP can be retained and whether the subsequent HP trend improves or worsens.
[A0237][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][LIMIT][DOT][EPISTEMIC_SEPARATION] Do not generalize from the opponent's sustained pressure being superior in the current exchange to the enemy build being superior to your build in every capability and every situation. At the same time, do not obscure the fact that the current exchange is unfavorable by listing general strengths.
[A0238][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][HEALING][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] When explaining specialized equipment or skills that gain benefits at low HP, or special operation such as Ganker / Bomber play, do not generalize that operation to HP management in normal PvP; separate it as an intentional performance tradeoff.

## Evaluation of Defensive Equipment

[A0239][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][HEALING_GEAR] Evaluate healing equipment not only by the amount healed but also by whether the healing effect occurs at the required timing.
[A0240][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][RESISTANCE_GEAR] Evaluate resistance equipment not only by the amount of resistance gained but also by whether the resistance increase occurs at the required timing.
[A0241][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][HEALING_GEAR] When evaluating healing equipment, consider not only the displayed effect but also the effective cooldown.
[A0242][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][HEALING_GEAR][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] A PvP healer should first establish baseline healing through skill rotation; evaluate healing equipment not as a substitute for baseline healing but as supplemental healing or added value such as buffs and debuffs.
[A0243][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PRIORITY][LIMIT][BEGINNER_HEALER][HEALING_GEAR][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] For beginner healer builds intended to operate without belonging to a large group, including PUG situations, prioritize healer equipment whose effects are not limited only to group members. Do not recommend support equipment whose effect targets only group members as equipment for the purpose of support in that use case.
[A0244][AUTHOR_VIEW][ADVANTAGE][LIMIT][SHIELD_GEAR] Equipment that grants shields is less likely than healing equipment to be wasted through overhealing, but it does not directly heal and rewind HP that has already been lost.
[A0245][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][GOAL][DEFENSIVE_GEAR] Use healing, resistance, and shield equipment not to replace active self-defense actions, but to support that self-defense, extend the time before death, and support active HP rewind.

## Shield Stacking, GCD Value, and Damage Calculation

[A0246][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][SHIELD_SKILL] The primary purpose of shield stacking in PvP is not to extend the displayed duration of a shield, but to layer multiple separate shield durability values and increase the amount of damage and number of offensive actions required before the enemy can reach actual HP.
[A0247][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][SHIELD_SKILL] When under continuous pressure from an equal or stronger opponent, normally assume that ordinary shields other than exceptions such as アルティメット（Ultimate） will disappear from durability depletion before their displayed duration expires; do not treat displayed duration as guaranteed safe time.
[A0248][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][SHIELD_SKILL] Even for a shield granted by アルティメット（Ultimate）, do not group it together with ordinary shields; evaluate the skill effect and shield amount individually.
[A0249][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][SHIELD] When evaluating the defensive value of a shield, look not only at absorption amount but also at whether the shield can require an additional enemy attack GCD or how much HP debt it can prevent from arising during the 1 GCD you spend on another action.
[A0250][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][SHIELD] Do not treat a shield clearly smaller than the general 1-GCD damage of an equal opponent as a shield that negates one enemy GCD.
[A0251][AUTHOR_VIEW][ADVANTAGE][SHIELD] Even a shield clearly smaller than the general 1-GCD damage of an equal opponent can have value as supplemental defense that reduces HP debt when attached to an action serving another purpose.
[A0252][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][SHIELD_SKILL] When multiple shields are used simultaneously and each shield has different additional effects when damaged or destroyed, which shield absorbs damage first also affects offensive or reflection effects; therefore, treat cast order itself as a tactic.
[A0253][PATCH][CLAIM][SHIELD] Defensive mitigation from ブロック（Block） does not apply to damage dealt to shields.
[A0254][PATCH][CLAIM][SHIELD] Critical Damage can occur against shields.
[A0255][PATCH][CLAIM][SHIELD] Resistance and damage reduction from buffs apply to damage dealt to shields.
[A0256][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][SHIELD][RULE] Because shield damage-calculation mechanics depend on the patch environment, when treating the application of ブロック（Block） mitigation, Criticals, resistance, or incoming-damage reduction precisely, verify the current mechanics in official patch notes at the time of the question.
[A0257][CLAIM][SHIELD] The shield damage-calculation mechanics in which ブロック（Block） mitigation does not apply, Criticals can occur, and resistance and incoming-damage reduction do apply have been maintained for a long period.
[A0258][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][SHIELD] In PvP, shields alone may not always absorb everything; operate with the expectation that damage can reach health.

## Indirect Self-Defense Value of Sustain

[A0259][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][SCOPE] The ability to repeat defensive actions for a long time through high sustain can support longer survival time, but do not infer from that alone that the build has strong offensive return capability or high combat initiative. Whether ending the defensive turn and returning to offense or disengagement is a required condition for a high rating depends on the build's role and win condition.
[A0260][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][CAUSE][LIMIT][DIAG][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][TIME_BUDGET] In a configuration with abundant sustain but insufficient per-use defense or healing against enemy pressure in the target environment, do not judge survivability sufficient merely because large sustain allows many defensive actions to be repeated. If processing enemy pressure requires spending most GCDs on defense and prevents transition to offense, CC, movement, or recovery, the marginal value of additional sustain falls sharply; suspect insufficient effective performance per defensive action.
[A0261][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] When diagnosing a self-defense build, separately examine “how many times defensive actions can be used” and “how many seconds or GCDs of safety or counterattack opportunity are gained from one defensive action.” If only the former is high and the latter is low, treat the problem as insufficient efficiency per defensive action rather than insufficient sustain.

## Effective Defense and Sustain-Dependence Structure

[A0262][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][LIMIT][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL][PVP_BASELINE] Evaluate build survivability not by the presence or count of defensive elements, but by whether the combined effective defense provides sufficient damage mitigation, health margin, time to heal, and room to recover against burst and sustained pressure realistically encountered in the target PvP environment. Do not treat merely possessing defensive skills, resistance buffs, healing, shields, or similar tools normally found in general PvP builds as evidence of sufficient self-defense or above-standard toughness.
[A0263][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][LIMIT][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL] Before rating sustain as a survivability strength, require verification that sufficient damage mitigation and effective defense are established against incoming damage in the target PvP environment. Do not use high recovery values, Max Resources, resource-restoration capability, or the number of times escape actions can be repeated as evidence substituting for insufficient damage mitigation, health margin, Critical Resistance, or similar direct defenses. Do not rate a build with insufficient direct defense as highly survivable merely because it has many resources.
[A0264][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][LIMIT][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] In a configuration that dies to enemy burst or sustained pressure before it can spend enough resources on defense, healing, disengagement, or similar actions, unused resources remaining at death do not function as survivability that could prevent that death.
[A0265][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Do not evaluate sustain only by the size of recovery values, Max Resources, or restoration amounts; compare supply against the actual resource demand generated by maintaining offense, defense, healing, 打破（Break Free）, movement, and similar actions under enemy pressure in the target environment. Even with high supply, do not rate practical sustain as high when demand is still greater.
[A0266][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][RESOURCE_DEPENDENCE] The more a build concentrates most survival tools into one or a small number of actions consuming a specific resource, the higher the practical sustain level required for that resource becomes. Do not highly rate a build with strong dependence on a specific resource as highly survivable unless it is confirmed that the required amount of that resource can be maintained reliably.
[A0267][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][RESOURCE_DEPENDENCE] When multiple survival tools depend strongly on the same resource, do not evaluate them as fully independent defensive layers. If using one causes the shared resource required by another to become insufficient, evaluate the common resource dependence as a single point of vulnerability.
[A0268][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][SUSTAIN][RESOURCE_DEPENDENCE] If regeneration or supply of the resource on which the primary defense depends is reduced or suspended while defending, moving, or in a specific state, do not treat high normal-state recovery values directly as crisis-state practical sustain.
[A0269][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][SUSTAIN][RESOURCE_DEPENDENCE] When an action relied upon for survival loses sustain efficiency with repeated use over a short period because of cost escalation, worsening supply conditions, or other reasons, do not estimate sustainable duration from the first-use cost or displayed recovery value alone.
[A0270][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] For a build that requires extremely high sustain, before rating that sustain as a strength, diagnose whether the required amount itself has become excessive because of insufficient defensive strength, high-frequency reuse of defensive actions, dependence on a single resource, or similar causes.
[A0271][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][DIAG][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][OPPORTUNITY_COST] When insufficient defensive strength increases the required frequency of defensive actions, which increases sustain investment needed to maintain them, which in turn further reduces the performance budget available for direct defense, treat this cycle as negative feedback in defensive design.
[A0272][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][OPPORTUNITY_COST][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] If extremely high sustain actually makes a particular defensive design function, do not deny that functionality itself. However, identify the resistance, damage reduction, Max Health, healing, damage, equipment slots, and other performance budget lost to obtain that sustain, and evaluate it as opportunity cost of the build as a whole.
[A0273][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][RESOURCE_DEPENDENCE] Do not judge allocating a large amount of performance budget to a specific resource as a flaw by itself. If effective defense required by the target environment is otherwise sufficiently established, the resource investment reliably supports multiple effective actions, and other important resources or performance remain at practical levels, evaluate the specialization individually as a functioning design.

## Defensive Margin for Beginners

[A0274][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Do not make configurations that greatly increase only resources, or only resources and damage, without sufficiently supplementing survivability a standard recommendation for entry-level beginners. Do not make beginner survival depend on continuously maintaining high precision in early enemy detection, danger prediction, optimal positioning and escape-route selection, accurate resource management, and appropriate defensive inputs within short windows. Secure defensive margin that can absorb a certain amount of error when judgment or operation is delayed. Do not treat a low-defense configuration that survives only when ideal early detection, avoidance, and escape succeed as having sufficient survivability for beginners.
[A0275][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL][MOVEMENT_SPEED][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][ESCAPE_ROUTE] In builds for entry-level beginners, do not permit insufficient effective defense against the target PvP environment merely because the build has high mobility or escape capability. Survival through mobility depends on when danger is detected, positioning, terrain and route, remaining resources, enemy pursuit capability, and similar factors; separately evaluate defensive margin that can absorb some error when judgment or operation is delayed or when damage is taken before escape begins.

## Defensive Investment, Effective Values, and Offense-Defense Transition

[A0276][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][SUSTAIN] When a primary defensive skill is central to a build, distinguish investment that increases the absorption, healing, or effective durability of one use of that skill from resource investment that only increases how many times it can be reused; verify that increasing only the latter has not left the defensive value of each use insufficient.
[A0277][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][TIME_BUDGET] For a defensive effect that is strong only immediately after activation and then becomes substantially weaker, do not treat the displayed total duration as safe time at the same strength. Evaluate how long the primary defensive strength is maintained and how many GCDs that one use allows before transitioning to the next attack, heal, CC, or similar action.
[A0278][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][SUSTAIN][TIME_BUDGET] Investment in resistance, incoming-damage reduction, shield amount, and similar defenses can save resources and GCDs by reducing the number of heals or shield recasts required. Therefore, when adjusting sustain, compare not only increases in recovery but also reductions in resource demand produced by stronger defense.
[A0279][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][MARGINAL_EFFECT_EVALUATION] When investment in a stat simultaneously increases not only unshielded durability but also shields or healing that scale with that stat, or the effective value of other defensive layers, evaluate the combined increments. Do not compare each defensive layer only as an independent investment.
[A0280][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] A configuration that survives sufficiently on the back bar but sharply loses resistance, mitigation, healing, or similar defense immediately upon switching to the offensive bar and is forced back to the back bar at once by continuing enemy pressure has difficulty creating an effective offensive turn. Evaluate build durability not only on the defensive bar but also in the state after transitioning to the offensive bar.

## Scope of Enemy-Threat Evaluation

[A0281][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][AI_META_EVALUATION] When evaluating the threat level of enemy classes, equipment, skills, or similar elements, do not represent them only by average or typically weak configurations. Include strong configurations that can realistically function and be encountered in the target environment, and do not generalize weakness in one specific role into denial of threats the same element can present in another practical role.

## Reinforced Trait on 重装鎧（Heavy Armor） Chest

[A0282][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][EVAL][HEAVY_ARMOR][REINFORCED_TRAIT][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Evaluate the value of the 強化（Reinforced） trait on a 重装鎧（Heavy Armor） chest piece not only by the trait percentage, but also by the absolute resistance increase gained because the percentage modifier applies to the increased armor value of the high-base-armor chest piece after quality improvement.

## Situational Awareness and Anti-Stealth

[A0283][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][HEURISTIC][SITUATIONAL_AWARENESS] Build situational awareness in PvP not by staring at the surroundings for long periods and stopping combat processing, but by using concepts of situational awareness, visual scanning, and attention switching to make it a habit of brief repeated scans compatible with combat, skill, and resource checks.
[A0284][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][LIMIT][STEALTH_COUNTER] After stealth has been revealed or removed, current re-stealth conditions, reuse restrictions, continuing reveal effects, or similar mechanics may prevent an immediate return to stealth or invisibility; do not assume that an enemy once detected can instantly return to full stealth.
[A0285][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][RULE][STEALTH_COUNTER] Anti-stealth effects include effects that continue revealing targets who enter stealth or invisibility and effects that prevent activation of stealth or invisibility itself for a period; do not treat the two as the same anti-stealth effect.
[A0286][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][STEALTH_COUNTER] 光輝く灯明（Radiant Magelight） is particularly valuable for beginners as a strong defensive option against stealth gankers because, while slotted, it can prevent Slam Stun originating from stealth attacks. Do not, however, treat it as an effect that also nullifies inherent CC belonging to a skill itself when that skill is used from stealth.
[A0287][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][RULE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][STEALTH_COUNTER] When using the 暴きの閃光（Revealing Flare） line for anti-stealth purposes, evaluate it less as a tool for searching for an enemy hiding somewhere and more as area denial that creates a zone difficult for enemies to approach or pass through while remaining stealthed; for beginners, treat 残存する閃光（Lingering Flare）, which can create a persistent area, as a strong candidate.

## Critical Resistance, Enemy Assumptions, and Defensive Modifications

[A0288][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] When evaluating Critical Resistance, evaluate whether the effective total from all sources is sufficient against Critical Damage in the target PvP environment, not merely whether any source exists. Do not treat merely having a small amount of Critical Resistance as evidence that sufficient defense against Critical burst has been established.
[A0289][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] For entry-level beginners active in PvP areas, do not make it a standard recommendation to shift armor traits heavily toward performance other than Critical Resistance without first confirming from data that sufficient Critical Resistance has been secured. In particular, do not make survival depend on anticipating enemy attacks in advance and defending appropriately.
[A0290][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY][OPPORTUNITY_COST] Do not automatically reduce Critical Resistance previously obtained from armor traits or similar sources merely because Critical Resistance is also obtained from an equipment set or another effect. Shift that performance budget to other purposes only when data confirms that total Critical Resistance after the reduction remains sufficient for the target use case.
[A0291][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PVP_ASSUMPTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] When evaluating self-defense performance of a build active in PvP areas, do not implicitly assume favorable enemy conditions such as enemy players not attacking, having low pursuit capability, having no more mobility than you, or being unable to perform effective attacks or CC from range. Include enemy capabilities realistically encountered in the target environment.
[A0292][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][PVP_ASSUMPTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Except where participation count is explicitly limited by the system, such as in a duel, do not evaluate self-defense performance in PvP content under the assumption that there is always only one enemy. Evaluate survivability including the possibility of receiving pressure from multiple enemies within a short period.
[A0293][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][DIAG][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL] For a build whose original effective defense is substantially insufficient for the target PvP environment, do not judge the problem solved merely because one defensive element was added. Re-evaluate the entire modified build including resistance, mitigation, health, Critical Resistance, healing, shields, sustain, and available uptime; if effective defense remains insufficient, do not rate the modification highly.
[A0294][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL] Do not treat the number of added defensive elements as the amount of increased defensive performance. One skill, one equipment effect, one armor trait, and similar elements differ in effective mitigation, healing, defendable time, activation condition, uptime, and opportunity cost; do not judge them as equivalent self-defense improvements merely because “one defensive element was added.”
[A0295][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] When effective defense is insufficient in a build for an entry-level beginner and the build has no equipment whose primary function is defense other than sustain, strongly consider introducing at least one defensive five-piece set as a major early improvement candidate. Do not assume that adding a single defensive skill can cover the entire deficit.
[A0296][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] Do not treat a defensive five-piece set and one defensive skill as comparable defensive investments merely because each is “one defensive element.” When judging that one ordinary defensive skill can replace defensive investment consuming dedicated equipment slots, actually compare effective defense before and after the change and verify that the replacement still establishes the defensive strength required by the target environment.
[A0297][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][LIMIT][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] A configuration without a defensive five-piece set can still establish effective defense for the target environment by securing sufficient resistance, mitigation, Critical Resistance, health, healing, shields, and other defenses. However, establishing this requires concrete data analysis, and configurations with higher operational or decision demands should not be standard recommendations for entry-level beginners.

## AI Control for Self-Defense Evaluation in PvP Areas

[A0298][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][LIMIT][PVP_ASSUMPTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] For builds expected to operate in PvP areas for more than a short period, do not omit verification of effective defense against players because the player does not intend to initiate PvP, has PvE as the primary objective, plans to avoid enemies, or has high mobility or escape tools.
[A0299][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PVP_ASSUMPTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL] For a build used in a PvP area where enemy players can actually attack it, evaluate survivability when receiving realistic PvP burst in the target environment.
[A0300][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][PVP_ASSUMPTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not rate a configuration whose sufficient effective defense has not been confirmed from data as safe or beginner-friendly merely because it plans to avoid combat, has mobility, sustain, or similar advantages.
