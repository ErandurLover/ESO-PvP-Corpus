# Part 1 — Fundamental Principles and Combat Model of ESO PvP

## Basic Premises

[A0057][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][BASIC_PREMISE] In ESO PvP, first ask what the character's combat power is for before focusing on the combat power itself.
[A0060][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][BASIC_PREMISE] ESO PvP is not a game of comparing character specifications alone; even with the same configuration, practical results vary with execution, decisions, and circumstances.
## Preparing Decision Principles in Advance

[A0061][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][SCOPE][COMMON_DECISION][HEURISTIC] For recurring real-time decisions in ESO PvP, do not try to compare every condition from scratch and prove the situational optimum each time. Prepare in advance decision principles to use under normal conditions for frequently occurring situations.
## Action Opportunities and Offensive/Defensive Turns

[A0069][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY] Treat the idea that “PvP is more a contest over opportunities to act than a contest over raw attack power” as a highest-priority principle.
[A0070][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] The more time you spend attacking and the more time the opponent is forced to spend healing or defending, the more favorable the fight becomes.
[A0071][CAUSE][CLAIM] If the cycle continues in which you spend an action healing → the opponent gains freer attack time → you take more damage → you are forced to heal again → the opponent gains still more attack opportunities, the opponent maintains initiative.
[A0073][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] During combat, recognize which side currently holds attacking initiative.
[A0074][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE] Survive or break the opponent's offensive turn to seize your own offensive turn, then maintain the offensive turn you seized so the opponent cannot simply withstand it.
[A0077][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY] Damage has the function of forcing the opponent into healing or defense and thereby reducing their freedom of action; this function is important in PvP.

# Part 2 — Offensive Design and Pressure Formation

## CC

[A0088][DEF][SCOPE] In this Corpus, CC（Crowd Control） refers to effects that incapacitate the opponent and are subject to 打破（Break Free）. 移動不能（Immobilize） and 減速（Snare） are not included in CC.
[A0093][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] When attacking, create enough pressure that the opponent judges that prioritizing self-defense over counterattacking is necessary to avoid death.
[A0094][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][RULE] Opponents also often initiate combos or rushes from CC, so anticipate this and either survive it through defensive actions or forcibly interrupt it with CC.
[A0095][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] Through attack practice, beginners should aim to gradually increase the situations in which they can move from “use a major attack because I want the kill” toward thinking “land CC and concentrate strong attacks into the short control window”; they do not need to create an appropriate CC opening every time from the start.
[A0096][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] The main uses of CC are to begin your offense, interrupt the opponent's offense, interfere with healing immediately before a kill, and interfere with escape immediately before a kill.
## Burst

[A0104][DEF] Burst means concentrating a large amount of damage into a short time window.
[A0105][LIMIT] Even a single large hit may fail to secure a kill if the opponent can heal after each individual hit.
[A0106][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] For burst, overlap delayed attacks, damage over time, debuffs, CC, strong regular attacks, アルティメット（Ultimate）, follow-up attacks, and executes within a short time window.
[A0110][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY] What matters in burst is not merely using individually strong abilities in sequence, but creating multiple simultaneous problems that the opponent cannot easily resolve with a single heal.
[A0113][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][COND][CAUSE] Do not forcibly commit everything when burst conditions are not ready. Representative poor conditions include the opponent being completely healthy, your buffs being down, important debuffs not yet applied, insufficient resources, or CC being unavailable; even committing major resources in such a state is unlikely to produce results.
[A0117][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE] If even your strongest-class combo cannot reduce the opponent's health enough, a win condition based on killing that opponent with a burst combo is unlikely to function; do not fixate on a burst kill and switch to another win condition.
[A0118][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][ALTERNATIVE] When a burst kill is unlikely to function, alternative win conditions include gradually pushing the opponent down with sustained damage pressure, attacking their resources by forcing inefficient combat actions, or abandoning the fight early and disengaging.
[A0119][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][PRESSURE_VS_FINISH] When you fail to secure a kill, distinguish whether “you applied enough pressure to force defense, healing, or disengagement but lacked the final finishing tool” or “the pressure itself was insufficient, leaving the opponent able to continue attacking, applying CC, and adjusting position.”
## Post-Burst Damage Trough and Pressure Between Kill Windows

[A0121][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BURST_DESIGN][PRESSURE_VS_FINISH] In a build whose primary finishing tools depend on アルティメット（Ultimate）, cooldowns, stacking conditions, or similar mechanics with long preparation times, evaluate not only the strength of the kill window but also whether ordinary pressure between windows can prevent the opponent from fully stabilizing. If the opponent can fully heal and fully prepare every time, merely surviving until the next kill window should not be counted as progress toward a kill.

[A0155][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][DEFENSIVE_LAYER][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL] Except for full tank operation whose primary defensive requirement is maintaining continuous ブロック（Block）, before rating the self-defense performance of a build active in PvP areas highly, explicitly verify whether its total Critical Resistance is sufficient against the Critical burst used by enemy players in the target environment. ## Layered Defense

[A0166][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM] PvP survivability is not determined by a single defensive stat.
[A0167][CLAIM] Defensive layers include Max Health, Physical and Spell Resistance, damage reduction, damage shields, healing over time, burst healing, ブロック（Block）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, distance, movement speed, obstacles, CC, offensive pressure against the opponent, debuff cleansing, and resource reserve.
[A0168][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] Do not conclude that a build is safe merely because one defensive element has been maximized.
## Identifying Defensive Value

[A0175][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][COMMON_DEFENSIVE_DECISION][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][PRIORITY] In early practice, when you recognize danger and are in a state where you can choose your own defensive action, practice the reflexive basic sequence of first establishing ブロック（Block）, then moving into recovery such as switching to the defensive bar, healing, defensive skills, LoS, or creating distance.
[A0177][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PVP_ASSUMPTION][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When explaining the opening defensive response for beginners, do not assume that the enemy player will wait for the questioner's inputs, hesitate to a similar degree, or voluntarily give up attack opportunities. Evaluate response time while assuming an opponent who may input the next attack, CC, or burst faster than the questioner.
## ブロック（Block） Operation and Timing

[A0179][DEF][BLOCK] Distinguish long-duration blocking, which maintains the ブロック（Block） state continuously, from short-duration blocking used only briefly to handle a major attack.
[A0180][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BLOCK] Do not use the opportunity cost of long-duration blocking as a reason to avoid short-duration ブロック（Block） against major attacks that can be blocked.
[A0183][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][TIME_BUDGET] ブロック（Block） is not constrained by the normal active-skill GCD and can be input to establish the block state even while a skill GCD is active.
[A0184][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Because the block state is maintained when bar swapping while holding ブロック（Block）, in an emergency you can establish block first and move to the defensive bar while maintaining that state.
[A0186][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][PRIORITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] For beginner emergency defense, make the basic practice sequence: recognize danger, first establish ブロック（Block）, perform any necessary bar swap while maintaining it, and proceed to healing, defensive skills, or other recovery as soon as the GCD becomes available.
[A0187][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][BLOCK][CC][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When a blockable CC can be blocked in advance, its value includes not only preventing the CC itself but also preventing upstream the unfavorable chain of the CC removing ブロック（Block）, spending スタミナ（Stamina） on 打破（Break Free）, exposure to the following burst, and rebuilding defense.
[A0188][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][BLOCK][BLOCK_COST] A state in which the スタミナ（Stamina） margin needed for ブロック（Block） has been lost is not a general reason to place healing before block; diagnose it separately as a dangerous state in which the emergency Stamina reserve or practical-combat sustain has already been exhausted.
[A0194][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][PRIORITY] When explaining the defensive order for beginners in danger, do not generate sequences such as “switch to the defensive bar, heal, then ブロック（Block）” or “heal, then block” as standard procedure; show block as the first voluntary defensive action.
[A0195][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][LIMIT][CC][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use the state of already being incapacitated by CC that requires 打破（Break Free） as a counterexample to the principle of initiating defense with ブロック（Block） first. Treat the CC state as one in which normal voluntary defensive choices are restricted, and explain state recovery through Break Free together with re-establishing block as early as possible.
[A0196][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][LIMIT][BLOCK][BLOCK_COST][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use having no スタミナ（Stamina） remaining for ブロック（Block） as grounds for making heal-first the standard beginner recommendation. Diagnose that state separately as depletion of the emergency resource reserve.
[A0197][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PRIORITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When listing multiple emergency defensive actions for beginners, do not present them as unordered alternatives in a way that loses the opening priority of ブロック（Block）. In a context that deals with the initiation order during danger, explicitly place block first.
## Toughness, Recovery, and Defensive Design

[A0203][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][RULE] Evaluate defensive performance on two axes: “how difficult it is for HP to decrease” and “how lightly the burden is to restore reduced HP to near full.” Do not equate only one of those axes with high defensive performance.
[A0205][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][LETHAL_DAMAGE_PREVENTION] In situations with a possibility of lethal damage, prioritize reducing incoming damage and surviving without letting HP reach 0 over conserving resources.
[A0206][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][BLOCK] When a burst that can be ブロック（Block）ed can be blocked to reduce incoming damage, doing so tends to reduce the amount of HP rewind required and the associated burden of healing actions and healing resources compared with taking it unprotected and then healing the lost HP afterward.
[A0209][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] As with an offensive burst sequence, when entering a defensive turn, first establish Block as the first voluntary defense, use that defensive time to connect healing, defense, and positional adjustment, and prepare in advance for each build a short recovery sequence that returns you to a state where you can fight again; practice it repeatedly. For an ordinary build, do not make increasing the number of emergency defensive-sequence patterns an objective in itself; narrow them to roughly one or two primary sequences that are effective and practice until they can be executed reflexively under severe pressure.
[A0210][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][CAUSE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Defensive design should secure not only ordinary defensive performance but also a means to recover from danger back to a state where you can fight again. If this is insufficient, pressure that would normally be manageable is more likely to become a major threat.

## Healing over Time and Emergency Healing

[A0212][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT] In PvP, even when a healer is present, do not make it standard operation to abandon executable self-defense and self-healing and delegate your HP management entirely to the healer.
[A0215][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] For beginner builds, treat a configuration with both healing over time that works routinely and a larger heal for danger as a basic candidate for early learning, and make it a goal to gradually increase the situations in which the beginner can distinguish and use both appropriately.
[A0218][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][DEFAULT][RULE][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][PVP_BASELINE] In normal PvP, excluding specialized configurations that intentionally gain performance from the user's own low-HP state, make restoring current HP to maximum HP and maintaining maximum HP as much as practical a basic goal.
[A0220][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][DEFAULT][PRIORITY][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When a beginner returns from a defensive turn to offense, as a rule restore current HP to maximum HP and secure an emergency スタミナ（Stamina） reserve needed for actions such as 打破（Break Free） before deciding to return to offense.
[A0223][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Aiming for maximum HP does not mean mechanically spamming burst heals until maximum HP is reached. Evaluate recovery as including the use of healing over time, ブロック（Block）, mitigation, LoS, distance, disengagement, and similar tools to return to a state in which restored HP can be retained.
[A0225][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][DOT][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Treat the typical pattern of losing to sustained pressure not as healing stopping at a particular HP value, but as repeatedly restoring to high HP and then being worn down again, with the HP level at which healing begins or ends gradually worsening through those repetitions.
[A0228][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEFAULT][PRIORITY][HEALING][ESCAPE_ROUTE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] If you cannot retain high HP after healing against sustained pressure and have no clear reversal condition, consider early disengagement while HP and emergency resources still remain sufficiently available.
[A0231][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] For a beginner's criterion for ending a defensive turn and returning to offense, do not generate fixed HP thresholds such as “having at least a certain proportion of HP is enough” or “returning to maximum HP is unnecessary.”
