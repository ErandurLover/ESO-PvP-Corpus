## B6 Competitive Skirmisher Operation in Capped Small-Group PvP

[B0111][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][SCOPE][LIMIT][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] The B6 section below is not a definition of the general Skirmisher role tendency covered in Series A, but an advanced operation for cases where advanced players competitively optimize part of that role in capped small-group PvP content for effective group contribution. It assumes simultaneous handling of rapid intervention across multiple locations, frequent battlefield changes, ally rescue, and continued offense and defense; do not extend B6's ranged-oriented suitability, class suitability, evaluation of ボルトエスケープ（Bolt Escape）／雷光（Streak）, rescue, aggro management, or similar requirements to solo play, uncapped large-scale combat, general or casual Skirmisher operation, or standard beginner requirements.

### B6.2 Competitive Skirmisher: Range and Intervention Speed in Advanced Capped Small-Group PvP

[B0129][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][SCOPE][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][RANGED_INTERVENTION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When optimizing competitive Skirmisher play in the advanced tier of capped small-group PvP defined in B0111, suitability is strongly biased toward ranged-centered builds rather than melee-centered builds. This does not mean melee builds are logically incapable of performing the role; rather, in this environment where intervention speed and frequency are continuously demanded, being able to begin effective combat participation from range creates a large suitability difference. Do not extend this bias into general Skirmisher or beginner build requirements.
[B0130][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][DEF][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][RANGED_INTERVENTION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In the advanced competitive use of this section, ranged-centered suitability does not merely mean slotting one or more long-range skills; it emphasizes being able, before fully closing with a distant fight, to begin effective attacks, CC, support, or similar participation immediately or within a short time under low preparation requirements and repeat that intervention at high frequency.
[B0131][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][RANGED_INTERVENTION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Ranged abilities requiring delayed activation, strict prerequisites, long reuse restrictions, special activation states, or similar conditions may be high-performance in ordinary ダメージディーラー（DD） builds, but have reduced value as rapid-response ranged intervention tools for the advanced competitive skirmisher of this section, whose intervention timing is externally demanded by battlefield conditions.
[B0132][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CAUSE][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][RANGED_INTERVENTION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When the same travel time is required in the advanced competitive use of this section, a melee-centered setup that cannot begin attacking, CC, supporting, or similar participation until it reaches effective range tends to begin intervention later than a setup that can start effective intervention from range.
[B0133][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In competitive combat among advanced players in the capped small-group PvP of this section, even a difference of a few seconds in intervention may greatly affect the local offensive-defense outcome, so treat time until intervention begins as a high-value tactical resource.
[B0134][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][CLAIM][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Even in capped small-group PvP, meaningful engagements are not necessarily always concentrated at only one location.
[B0135][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][CAUSE][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][RANGED_INTERVENTION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In advanced competitive operation that repeatedly changes battlefields as in this section, differences in the time required to begin one intervention accumulate into differences in the number of possible interventions and effective participation time, so range differences affect intervention frequency as well as intervention speed.
[B0136][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][CLAIM][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][RANGED_INTERVENTION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Ranged intervention by the advanced competitive Skirmisher of this section is not intended merely to land one attack before arrival; it includes producing continued pressure, CC, support, or similar effects while approaching.
[B0137][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][CLAIM][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][RANGED_INTERVENTION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In the advanced competitive use of this section, a setup that can, after beginning ranged intervention, either remain at distance and participate or close in as needed for full engagement or rescue has greater freedom of intervention.
[B0138][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][LIMIT][SCOPE][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][RANGED_INTERVENTION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] From the range evaluation in this section, do not conclude that general Skirmishers or beginners should use ranged-centered DD builds, that ranged builds are superior to melee builds in all PvP, that melee builds cannot function as Skirmishers, that ソーサラー（Sorcerer） or ボルトエスケープ（Bolt Escape）／雷光（Streak） is a general requirement for Skirmishers, or that possessing one long-range skill is equivalent to being a ranged-centered build. Limit the evaluation to the B0111 competitive Skirmisher operation involving capped, small-group, advanced-tier play, group contribution, and frequent battlefield changes.

### B6.3 Competitive Skirmisher: Ally Rescue and Battlefield Selection

[B0139][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][DIAG][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In the advanced competitive Skirmisher operation of B0111, roaming at range while prioritizing only personal safety and failing to make necessary group contributions is a failure.
[B0140][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][DIAG][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In the advanced competitive Skirmisher operation of B0111, if personal kills are prioritized while interventions with greater group value are repeatedly missed, the operation is unlikely to receive a high advanced-level evaluation.
[B0141][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] The advanced competitive Skirmisher of B0111 should prioritize intervention at locations with high team value rather than only personal safety or personal kills, and treat resolving crises affecting allies that can be reached as one of the primary duties.
[B0142][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] The advanced competitive Skirmisher of B0111 is not a role that only rescues allies; it also intervenes in combat for kill purposes.
[B0143][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In the advanced competitive operation of B0111, adding additional kill pressure to a near-death enemy and helping secure the kill is also legitimate team contribution.
[B0144][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][DEFAULT][HEURISTIC][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In the frequent B0111 advanced competitive choice between “an enemy who is almost dead” and “an ally who is almost dead,” when both exist simultaneously and there is no additional information sufficient to break the principle, prioritize rescuing the ally as the default.
[B0145][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][CAUSE][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Under normal conditions supporting the default of ally rescue in B0111 advanced competitive operation, judge that a near-death ally tends to have more difficulty recovering alone from the crisis than the remaining allies have finishing a near-death enemy.
[B0146][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][CAUSE][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In B0111 advanced competitive operation, successfully rescuing a near-death ally and preventing loss of allied combat strength has large tactical value for maintaining subsequent local combat power, and is one reason for making ally rescue the default.
[B0147][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][CAUSE][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] On the side finishing a near-death enemy in B0111 advanced competitive operation, sufficient allied combat strength may already be committed, and the marginal value of the Skirmisher's additional intervention may become small.
[B0148][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][LIMIT][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In B0111 advanced competitive operation, even if the kill on a near-death enemy is not completed immediately, the tactical pressure already applied to that enemy is not necessarily all lost at once.
[B0149][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][LIMIT][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][HEURISTIC][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Propositions giving reasons to support ally rescue in B0111 advanced competitive operation explain practical reasons for adopting the default principle in ordinary situations under the stated limited conditions; do not treat them as propositions proving a general behavioral norm for Skirmishers or that the quantitative expected value of rescue exceeds killing the enemy in every situation.

[B0159][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CAUSE][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] If the team collapses in B0111 advanced competitive operation, the individual Skirmisher also loses the chance to win.
[B0160][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][DIAG][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In B0111 advanced competitive Skirmisher operation, continually imposing excessive danger on allies as a result of prioritizing personal safety is a failure.

### B6A 雷光（Streak） Operation in High-MMR 4v4

[B0170][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][HIGH_MMR_4V4][GROUP_CONTENT] In high-MMR 4v4, shields, ブロック（Block）, 打破（Break Free）, healing, and ロール回避（Roll Dodge） become more important than offensive skills.
[B0171][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][HIGH_MMR_4V4][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Under the high-MMR 4v4 limited conditions of B0169, 雷光（Streak） becomes particularly important for establishing competitive distribution of combat power and return to combat.
[B0172][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][HIGH_MMR_4V4][GROUP_CONTENT] In high-MMR 4v4, assume proficiency with the fundamental elements other than 雷光（Streak） to the point that they can be executed without conscious attention.
[B0173][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][SCOPE][REQUIREMENT][STREAK_USAGE_CONDITION][STREAK][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Apply the 雷光（Streak） judgments below only to builds that can use ソーサラー（Sorcerer）'s 嵐の召喚（Storm Calling） and have Streak slotted; in the B0169 high-MMR 4v4 advanced competitive operation, being able to use Streak appropriately according to the situation becomes an important skill. [B0174][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][LIMIT][RULE][STREAK_RESOURCE][GROUP_CONTENT] 雷光（Streak） consumes マジカ（Magicka）, and builds without abundant Magicka have a limited number of consecutive uses, so determine use frequency while considering the current amount remaining and the Magicka that should be reserved for subsequent combat actions.
[B0175][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][LIMIT][CAUSE][EVAL][UNDER_FOCUS][STREAK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][ESCAPE_ROUTE] When under focus, 雷光（Streak） moves a large distance and can be used repeatedly, so chaining it solely to break enemy pursuit can cause only you to over-disengage from the main battlefield. Because the pursuers cannot participate in the main battlefield, maintaining that number of pursuers can prevent your allies from suffering a numerical disadvantage.
[B0177][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][ALT][ENEMY_LURING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][ESCAPE_ROUTE] After drawing deeply pursuing enemies for a certain period, there is an option to leave those enemies behind and use 雷光（Streak） to return rapidly to the main battlefield.
