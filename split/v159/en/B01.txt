## B1 All Roles / Common Principles

[B0001][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][REQUIREMENT][ALL_ROLES] As a prerequisite for applying advanced-player requirements, require stable execution of the Series A foundational behaviors relevant to the role and use being evaluated. Do not require prior mastery of every beginner-oriented goal in Series A across every role and use.
[B0002][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ALL_ROLES][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Consider opportunity cost at the group level as well.
[B0003][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ALL_ROLES][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] At the group level as well, consider pressuring the opponent's free time.
[B0004][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ALL_ROLES][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] At the group level as well, consider the concept of offensive and defensive turns.
[B0005][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][ALL_ROLES] Every role other than Pure Bruiser can improve its capabilities through actual experience performing the frontal-combat role of a Pure Bruiser.
[B0006][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CAUSE][ROLE_BRUISER][ALL_ROLES] A Pure Bruiser repeatedly performs offensive, defensive, healing, CC, positional, and resource decisions at high frequency; decision failures tend to return quickly as pressure received, defensive turns, being killed, or similar outcomes; and the player's own decisions change the opponent's actions, whose responses must then be processed in another perception-action loop. For these reasons, training exposure to various techniques and decision-making tends to be highly dense.
[B0007][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][CAUSE][ALL_ROLES] Because other roles can sometimes produce results through role-specific success mechanisms while avoiding direct frontal offensive/defensive exchanges, they may receive less learning exposure to the high-density offensive/defensive load required of a bruiser. This does not mean those other roles have lower value; it means that some learning paths may not repeat the load required of a bruiser sufficiently.
[B0008][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][REQUIREMENT][ALL_ROLES] At the advanced-player level assumed by Series B, evaluate not whether a skill exists at all, but whether the quality of required execution and decisions stably exceeds the demands of actual combat.
[B0009][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][REQUIREMENT][ROLE_BRUISER][ALL_ROLES] If the skills required to process repeated frontal-combat load as a bruiser are insufficient, even a player with high skill in parts of another role may be hindered from reaching broad advanced PvP proficiency.
[B0010][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][REQUIREMENT][ALL_ROLES] It is not necessary to become the strongest Bruiser; what is required for advanced-level learning is to exceed a standard at which the offensive, defensive, and decision load demanded in actual Pure Bruiser play can be handled stably.
[B0011][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][ALL_ROLES] A learning path that continually avoids frontal combat makes it difficult to repeat sufficiently the offensive and defensive judgments needed for advanced PvP operation.
[B0012][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][ALL_ROLES] In learning toward the advanced-player level assumed by this Series B, train as a Bruiser.
[B0013][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][EVAL][PRACTICE][ALL_ROLES] When evaluating skill transfer, emphasize 課題忠実度（task fidelity）: not merely whether practice contains the same execution elements, but how much it preserves the same perception-action loop, decision frequency, time pressure, failure feedback, and offensive-defense load as actual combat.
[B0014][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][EVAL][ALL_ROLES][ROLE_BRUISER] When training personal-combat capability at an advanced-player level, place more weight on task fidelity that allows high-frequency repetition of the perception-action loop, time pressure, and offensive/defensive transitions in which one opponent or a small number of opponents continually react to you than on total playtime in large-scale シロディール（Cyrodiil） combat itself.
[B0015][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][CLAIM][ROLE_BRUISER][ALL_ROLES] The most direct method of reaching the advanced-player level required by this Series B is to repeatedly gain practical experience establishing frontal combat as a Pure Bruiser; paths that avoid Bruiser combat experience while trying to reach equivalent execution and decision quality are generally indirect and difficult.
[B0016][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][ROLE_BRUISER][ALL_ROLES] When trying to reach equivalent execution and decision quality while avoiding Bruiser combat experience, differences in combat sense and value judgments relative to experienced Bruiser players also tend to remain.

### B1A Use of Third Parties in PvPvE Environments

[B0017][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][TACTIC][THIRD_PARTY_ENVIRONMENT_USE] In a PvPvE environment, attacks from monsters or third parties may be used to change the activation timing of your on-damage-taken effects or on-shield-break effects into a state that enemy players alone cannot control.

## B2 Tank

[B0018][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][LIMIT][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] In capped advanced small-group direct combat, when enemies can rationally ignore a Pure Tank and target allies first, additional contribution through healing, buffs, debuffs, CC, positional obstruction, or similar effects gains value by imposing losses on the enemy for ignoring the Tank as well as by providing survival. Do not generalize this requirement as a uniform mandatory condition to different battlefields such as シロディール（Cyrodiil）.
[B0019][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][EVAL][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Evaluate the binding value of an advanced Tank not only by the number of enemies occupied but also by the threat and role value of those enemies. If the enemy's main force can freely attack allies while the Tank is fixed only on low-threat enemies or a small number of enemies, do not rate long survival alone as high combat-force binding.
[B0020][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CC][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Tank's CC can create short action windows or attack opportunities for allies, but if the Tank's own offensive pressure cannot continuously force defense or healing from the enemy, do not conclude merely from successfully applying CC that the Tank personally can maintain offensive initiative.
[B0021][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][ROLE_TANK][ROLE_BRUISER][BLOCK_USAGE][MOVEMENT_SPEED][GROUP_CONTENT][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] In optimized small-scale operation where mobility is a primary foundation of activity, do not assume a permanent-ブロック（Block） Pure Tank as the standard frontline slot; evaluate as a strong candidate a composition where a high-durability DD / Bruiser that does not habitually block partially performs Tank-like functions such as resistance to focus fire and maintaining the frontline.
[B0022][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][ROLE_TANK][HEALING][HEALING_GEAR][OPPORTUNITY_COST][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] If the HoT required by a Tank is difficult to secure sufficiently through ordinary offensive / healing scaling alone and build slots permit it, evaluate equipment that produces a HoT or similar persistent healing using a major Tank-side attribute such as maximum health as a supplemental candidate.
[B0023][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][ROLE_TANK][HEALING][HEALING_GEAR][OPPORTUNITY_COST][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] In advanced Tank healing design, prioritize the sequence of first establishing the necessary healing functions through skills, then supplementing shortages or efficiency with equipment. If excessive dependence on synergies among multiple pieces of equipment and skills makes battlefield changes or changing some equipment difficult, evaluate that reduction in adjustment freedom as an opportunity cost as well.
[B0024][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] A Tank with added value from healing, buffs, or debuffs becomes able to contribute to allies more easily without depending on location or conditions.
[B0025][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][ROLE_TANK][LIMIT][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Occupying rule objectives is a contribution a Tank can make relatively easily without sacrificing survival, but it requires success conditions and its effect may be limited.
[B0026][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][ROLE_TANK][LIMIT][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Physically interposing on an enemy line of fire is a contribution a Tank can make relatively easily without sacrificing survival, but it requires success conditions and its effect may be limited.
[B0027][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][ROLE_TANK] If the class line has a useful Snare skill, strongly prioritize using it.
[B0028][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][CLAIM][ROLE_TANK] Snare does not grant crowd-control immunity.
[B0029][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Being able to enter the enemy frontline first and also perform additional jobs is an element of a high-contribution Tank.
[B0030][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][DIAG][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] A Tank that dies frequently is a bad Tank even if the player intends to support allies.
[B0031][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_TANK][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When an advanced Tank obtains a pull through an equipment effect, evaluate simultaneously the value of reducing the GCD, resources, and execution required by an active skill and the opportunity cost of losing durability, healing, sustain, or other support that could have occupied that equipment slot.
[B0032][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_TANK][PURGE][OPPORTUNITY_COST] For an advanced Tank continuously receiving DoTs and debuffs from many players, check whether a cleanse skill with a limited number of removals can keep up in terms of GCD, resources, and cleansing speed; if not, evaluate mass-cleanse equipment as a strong candidate including the opportunity cost of the equipment slot.

## B3 Healer / Buffer / Debuffer Support


### Opportunity Cost of a Support Slot in Small-Group Direct Combat

[B0033][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In environments such as advanced-level 4v4 where each slot in a fixed small group carries large responsibility and direct-combat pressure and kills are the primary win condition, evaluate the value of using one Support slot by the difference in overall combat value versus replacing that slot with an equally skilled Bruiser.
[B0034][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][REQUIREMENT][ROLE_SUPPORT][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When using one Support slot in advanced-level small-group direct combat, use as the criterion for justifying that slot that the Support's total value is at least equal to the overall combat value of the equally skilled Bruiser lost by selecting the Support.
[B0035][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][ADVANTAGE_CREATION_CONVERSION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Do not evaluate a Support's total value only by its own direct damage or healing amount; include the difference produced through healing, buffs, debuffs, CC, resource support, and similar effects in reducing allies' self-defense GCDs, increasing their attackable time, maintaining pressure, forcing additional defensive actions from enemies, and converting those advantages into kill opportunities or numerical advantage.
[B0036][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][ROLE_SUPPORT][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In advanced-level small-group direct combat, even if a Support keeps allies alive, if that survival does not convert into sufficient attack opportunities, pressure, resource margin, CC advantage, kill opportunities, or similar results and therefore cannot cover the opportunity cost of losing one Bruiser, that Support cannot justify consuming one composition slot.

### Additional Support and Hybrid-Role Evaluation for Advanced Support

[B0037][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][ROLE_SUPPORT][ROLE_TANK][OPPORTUNITY_COST][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When a Healer / Tank incorporates additional support, if that support can be established sufficiently through equipment effects or effects attached to healing, defensive, or support actions already required by the core role, prioritize those over active support that consumes GCDs or resources needed for the core role, while also comparing the opportunity cost of the equipment slot.
[B0038][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][ROLE_TANK][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When evaluating a dedicated Healer / Tank as also performing a DD-type role, require that the build maintain its dedicated healing, defensive, and support functions while also establishing enough offensive pressure to force effective defensive or healing actions from the enemy. Do not treat merely adding attack skills or small amounts of damage as performing a DD-type role.
[B0039][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_TANK][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Evaluate a Tank / DD hybrid role including the assumed enemy level and combat environment. The same setup may have DD-equivalent kill pressure against low-durability or low-skill enemies, but if it cannot continuously force defense or healing from advanced opponents, evaluate the offensive component as additional Tank pressure.

### Advanced Healer: Healing Zone and Rescue Decisions

[B0040][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In group combat among advanced players, a Healer should prioritize continuously establishing a dense healing zone into which allies can retreat, recover, and re-enter, rather than chasing individual allies in an attempt to prevent every death, and should not collapse the entire healing zone to rescue one player.
[B0041][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In group combat among advanced players, treat a major value of healing as using persistent healing to absorb ordinary incoming damage on allied Bruisers, reducing the GCDs Bruisers must spend on self-healing and allowing them to maintain offensive pressure.
[B0042][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][PRIORITY][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][UNDER_FOCUS][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][ESCAPE_ROUTE] When an advanced Healer moves to survive while under focus, do not make the objective moving far from the main battlefield and stopping healing supply; prioritize movement routes that use obstacles, allies, and healing methods that function while moving so that personal survival and healing supply to allies can coexist. If death cannot be avoided while maintaining healing line of sight, accept a temporary reduction in healing supply, create sufficient LoS, and prioritize resuming healing after surviving.
[B0043][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][DIAG][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When deciding whether to leave the main healing zone to rescue a distant ally, evaluate whether the benefit of saving that ally exceeds the loss imposed on other allies by weakening the current healing zone.
[B0044][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][DIAG][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When an ally leaves the main healing zone, check not only the result that the target is far away, but also whether positioning and operation on the ally's side or on your side is the primary cause of the target being outside the healing zone.
[B0045][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][PRIORITY][RULE][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In individual rescue of a distant ally, normally prioritize maintaining the overall healing zone, and move to individual rescue only when there is a concrete reason to override that priority.
[B0046][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][DIAG][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Healer should not immediately attribute an ally's death to inadequate self-defense by that ally; check the Healer's own healing range, HoT maintenance, support downtime, delays in emergency healing, and similar factors to evaluate whether the death could have been prevented from the Healer's side. At the same time, do not uniformly attribute deaths that the Healer could not prevent to Healer responsibility.
[B0047][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][LIMIT][CAUSE][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Selecting where to place a healing zone requires skill, and obstacles can also be used by enemies for LoS; however, placing the healing zone over an obstacle that allies can circle around often yields benefits that exceed the disadvantage of the enemy also using the obstacle, because allies can break enemy line of fire and weaken sustained pressure without moving far from the healing zone, can re-engage after healing with only short movement, and can more easily form the main battlefield around a common location for retreat, recovery, and re-entry.

### Advanced Healer: Death Analysis and LoS

[B0048][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][EX][RULE][CLAIM][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] There are also situations where placing the healing zone in an open area is stronger, with BG objective terrain lacking nearby obstacles as an example. Even then, as a basic approach choose a position around the objective that is easy to circle, and being even somewhat closer to an obstacle makes the healing zone easier for allies to use.
[B0049][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CAUSE][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] If a Healer moves excessively without purpose, it can make allies uneasy and make enemies judge the Healer as weak, increasing the likelihood of focused attacks.
[B0050][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] As a basic approach, an advanced Healer should use obstacles enough to reduce incoming enemy lines of fire while maintaining healing supply to allies, without unnecessarily cutting healing line of sight to allies merely to LoS enemies targeting the Healer.
[B0051][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Healer should not begin all healing preparation only after enemy pressure rises; maintain assigned HoTs, important buffs, and similar effects during normal periods so that GCDs can immediately be allocated to emergency healing when incoming damage increases.
[B0052][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Healer should switch GCD priorities as the situation changes among normal maintenance, enemy burst, defensive recovery, allied offense, and similar phases, rather than repeating only a fixed healing rotation.

### Advanced Healer: Preparation, GCDs, and Resources

[B0053][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][FUNCTION_LOSS][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Healer build should not depend on a single powerful channeled heal or アルティメット（Ultimate） heal as its sole healing foundation; even when that heal is interrupted or unavailable, it should be able to continue supplying healing through ordinary skills and pre-established HoTs.
[B0054][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Before entering a powerful heal that restricts use of other skills for a long period, an advanced Healer should establish HoTs, necessary buffs, and similar effects in advance where possible, reducing the loss of the group's healing foundation caused by being unable to use ordinary healing and support GCDs during the channel.
[B0055][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][SUSTAIN][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced group Healer should treat unused personal resources as future group-rescue capability and maintain healing margin or a rapid resource-rebuilding method sufficient to handle unexpected increases in pressure.
[B0056][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][LIMIT][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][SUSTAIN][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] A design in which an advanced Healer intentionally uses a primary healing resource such as マジカ（Magicka） down to a low level should be considered viable only if there is another reliable asset that can quickly and practically rebuild the required amount in an emergency.

### Premade Support, Anti-Healer Play, and Cleansing

[B0057][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Healer should not adopt a normal operating design that intentionally exhausts スタミナ（Stamina）, and should preserve enough margin to perform at least emergency 打破（Break Free）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, or similar actions.
[B0058][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][RULE][GROUP_CC][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When a Support handles burst-initiation CC in premade combat among advanced players, synchronize the CC and start of burst in advance through voice communication or similar means so that allied damage overlaps the short restraint window before 打破（Break Free）.
[B0059][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When a premade needs a persistent debuff role, before reducing a Bruiser's attack GCDs, skill slots, or equipment slots, examine whether that job can be consolidated into the Healer / Support; if it can be performed sufficiently on the Support side, prioritize maintaining Bruiser pressure.
[B0060][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When building anti-Healer measures in group combat among advanced players, do not use CC alone as the primary method; evaluate synchronizing focus fire and healing-reduction debuffs with allied burst as the main method.
[B0061][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][LIMIT][ROLE_SUPPORT][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In small-scale / ball-group play where the group enters tightly into enemy groups and multiple allies simultaneously receive DoTs and debuffs, secure group cleansing at high priority; in small-scale play that avoids such contact, do not treat it as permanently mandatory.

## B4 Bruiser

[B0062][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][REQUIREMENT][ROLE_PURE_BRUISER] Set the minimum viable level for an advanced Pure Bruiser as being able, in frontal combat against an equally skilled Pure Bruiser, to establish at least an even pressure exchange rather than being sent one-sidedly into defensive turns continuously.
[B0063][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][ROLE_HYBRID_BRUISER] A hybrid-role Bruiser is generally inferior to a Pure Bruiser in direct offensive and defensive output.
[B0064][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_HYBRID_BRUISER] Evaluate a hybrid-role Bruiser by total power including the value produced by the role it combines with Bruiser.
[B0065][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][ROLE_HYBRID_BRUISER] A hybrid-role Bruiser must be able to continue applying pressure while enduring to at least a certain degree even in combat against a Pure Bruiser.
[B0066][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][REQUIREMENT][ROLE_BRUISER] An advanced Bruiser requires proficiency in offense, defense, healing, CC, and tactics.
[B0067][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][REQUIREMENT][ROLE_BRUISER] Evaluate an advanced Bruiser's proficiency not by whether each skill is merely possessed, but by whether execution and decision quality stably exceeds actual combat requirements even when those skills are demanded at high frequency in frontal combat against equally skilled opponents; only upon reaching that level does the player stand at the starting point of advanced Bruiser play.
[B0068][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][EVAL][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] One benchmark for checking advanced bruiser execution proficiency through supplemental PvE training is being able to clear veteran ヴァテシュラン洞穴（Vateshran Hollows） reliably and with comfortable margin while remaining on the PvP build.
[B0069][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][EVAL][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] When using veteran ヴァテシュラン洞穴（Vateshran Hollows） as practice with a PvP build, do not treat the clear itself as a direct measurement of PvP strength. Use it as a supplementary benchmark for the stability of combat processing: whether you can continue offense and defense while maintaining self-healing, resource management, position management, awareness of enemy positions, and judgment of the next required action.
[B0070][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][EVAL][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] In solo-arena practice with a PvP build, compatibility among the class, skills, equipment configuration, and PvE-specific mechanics can greatly change clear suitability; therefore, do not directly convert differences in clear speed, ability to meet DPS checks, or remaining margin into differences in PvP ability.
[B0071][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][CAUSE][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] Combat against an advanced PvP player is more difficult than ヴァテシュラン洞穴（Vateshran Hollows） because, among the primary reasons, the opposing player changes behavior in response to your offense, defense, position, resource state, and similar information and actively rearranges the timing of CC, pressure, burst, defense, disengagement, and other actions, creating a higher-load perception-decision-action loop and greater time pressure than fixed PvE.
[B0072][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][RULE][ROLE_BRUISER] For a Bruiser, treat pressure as the most important element; even when combining another role, do not lose pressure that continuously forces enemies into defense or healing.
[B0073][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_BRUISER][PRESSURE_VS_FINISH] Evaluate a Bruiser's threat on the premise that actual damage can force enemies into defense and healing, not on the premise that enemies mistakenly believe low damage is high damage. Even without securing a kill, forcing sufficient self-defense frequency has value as pressure.
[B0074][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][RANGED_BRUISER][RANGE_COST] If an advanced ranged Bruiser allocates performance budget to range and thereby has lower frontal exchange performance than a similar melee Bruiser, evaluate in Pure Bruiser use whether that deficit can be recovered through the benefits of range, such as pressure before contact, continued pursuit pressure, and safe attack time. If those range benefits cannot be recovered sufficiently in actual combat under the relevant conditions, the build tends to be disadvantaged as a Pure Bruiser.
[B0075][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][CAUSE][ROLE_BRUISER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Do not treat a Bruiser as a hero who charges alone into the enemy and solves everything; account for the burden imposed on nearby allies when entering alone.
[B0076][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][ROLE_BRUISER] As a basic rule, one player's combat power is treated as one player's worth.
[B0077][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][ROLE_BRUISER] Differences in combat power between individual players tend to be less extreme among advanced players.
[B0078][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][DIAG][CLAIM][ROLE_BRUISER] Even if one button can easily kill multiple players, do not treat that as evidence of overwhelming personal strength; the primary cause is that the opponents were weak. [B0079][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][RULE][ROLE_BRUISER] Because the system does not allow making your own specifications many times greater than an advanced player's, do not underestimate enemy combat power; allocate vigilance equivalent to one player's worth to each one-player enemy and build tactics on the assumption of that combat power.

### Advanced Bruiser: Defensive Decisions, Group Detection, and Practice

[B0080][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] An advanced Bruiser should not begin defending only after Health clearly enters a danger zone; predict near-future danger from enemy attack preparation, remaining defensive resources, position, CC state, and similar factors, and transition into a defensive sequence immediately before lethal pressure becomes established.
[B0081][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Advanced players may sometimes turn to offense below maximum HP by reading initiative, remaining healing over time, enemy attack preparation, remaining defensive resources, and similar factors. Treat this as a situational judgment that trades HP margin for time and attack opportunities, and do not generalize it into the standard beginner condition for returning to offense.
[B0082][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][COND][DOT][ROLE_BRUISER][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Even when the exchange under sustained pressure is unfavorable, if the opponent has low durability and there is a concrete kill window in which you will not be defeated first even after turning to offense while existing DoTs or similar pressure remain on you, evaluate counterattacking for a reversal as an option in addition to early disengagement.
[B0083][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] In advanced group combat, expect the player under pressure to detect their own developing danger early and transition to self-defense at a high standard; if allies detect the same danger in advance and preemptively provide healing, shields, CC, pressure diversion, or similar help, evaluate that as advanced additional group contribution. Do not demand the same detection precision from the target and surrounding allies.
[B0084][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] Practicing PvE such as solo arenas in a PvP build can be used as auxiliary training that is based not merely on attack practice but on predicting your own developing danger and successfully defending while also processing additional actions such as attacking, checking surroundings, checking behind, and adjusting position according to the situation.
[B0085][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] In solo-arena practice with a PvP build, repeatedly practice a self-defense sequence that, from the stage when danger is detected, combines disengagement, self-healing, defense, positioning adjustment, and similar actions as needed to return to a state where you can fight again. Make one practice objective progressing from searching for individual inputs only after the situation becomes urgent toward entering defense while there is still margin and executing the sequence stably in parallel with other situational decisions.
[B0086][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] In solo-arena practice with a PvP build, first establish the ability to continue combat without losing control of survival, healing, resources, position, and surrounding awareness. As processing capacity increases, incorporate additional operations such as burst into the same combat load and increase the frequency with which they can be executed while preserving the foundational processing.
[B0087][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][EVAL][PRACTICE][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not treat the ability to establish a self-defense sequence reliably in a solo arena with a PvP build, by itself, as proof that the same processing will succeed in advanced PvP. On the other hand, if self-healing, defense, disengagement, and similar recovery from increasing danger cannot be handled reliably even in a highly predictable solo-arena environment with lower decision load than player-versus-player combat, treat that as a foundational deficiency in self-defense execution to improve before moving on to fights against advanced players.
[B0088][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] Because fixed PvE behavior cannot sufficiently reproduce the perception-action loop in which an opposing player changes behavior in response to your decisions and you then process that response, interpersonal time pressure, and failure feedback, do not treat it as a substitute for actual advanced Bruiser combat experience.

## B5 Evaluation of Ranged DD and Hybrid Roles in Advanced Fixed Small-Group Combat


### B5.1 Ranged Pure Bruiser: Range Cost, Focus Fire, and Self-Defense Floor

[B0089][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][DIAG][RANGED_BRUISER][ROLE_PURE_BRUISER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][OPPORTUNITY_COST][UNDER_FOCUS] When evaluating a ranged Pure Bruiser in advanced fixed small-group combat, even if range reduces incoming damage to that player, if the resulting enemy focus shifts disproportionately to allies and the player's own pressure also fails to compensate for the frontal exchange performance actually lost for range, do not treat personal safety alone as sufficient group contribution.
[B0090][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][RANGED_BRUISER][ROLE_PURE_BRUISER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][UNDER_FOCUS][ESCAPE_ROUTE] When a ranged Pure Bruiser uses distance, LoS, and mobility for self-defense in advanced fixed small-group combat, maintain or quickly resume effective combat participation while consuming enemy time, movement, and attacks directed toward you, and do not remain away from the main battlefield so long that excessive numerical or focus-fire burden is pushed onto allies.
[B0091][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CAUSE][DIAG][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][UNDER_FOCUS][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] If a DD that sacrifices self-defense in advanced fixed small-group combat falls to a level where it cannot handle focused attacks even briefly, that weakness converts from merely reduced durability into enemy priority targeting, loss of continuous attack time, and numerical burden on allies through forced disengagement or death; therefore evaluate a minimum survival floor under focus independently.
[B0092][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][DEFAULT][RANGED_BRUISER][ROLE_PURE_BRUISER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][OPPORTUNITY_COST][ESCAPE_ROUTE] In advanced 4v4, for a ranged Pure Bruiser where the opportunity cost actually paid for range makes it difficult to secure both necessary pressure and frontal durability equivalent to a similar melee Bruiser, treat as a strong basic solution prioritizing required offensive pressure over perfectly reproducing frontal durability, while handling periods under pressure through enough self-defense to avoid immediate death plus mobility and LoS.

### B5.2 Hybrid Roles: Role Compression and Reevaluation of Range

[B0093][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][EVAL][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION][TIME_BUDGET][OPPORTUNITY_COST] In an advanced build, when multiple role functions that would normally require separate GCDs, resources, and preparation time can be obtained effectively from the same action, maintenance action, equipment effect, or similar source, evaluate as the value of role compression the additional GCDs, resources, and rebuilding time saved by that overlap and the uptime preserved for the primary role. Evaluate not merely possession of multiple effects, but how many separate payments are actually avoided in combat.
[B0094][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][RANGED_BRUISER][ALTERNATIVE_COMPARISON][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] If the cost of compensating for insufficient frontal exchange performance as a Pure Bruiser is high for an advanced ranged DD, treat redesign into a role or hybrid role where range converts directly into group value—such as Skirmisher, off-healing, or debuffing—as a strong candidate instead of only forcing Pure Bruiser performance upward.
[B0095][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][RANGED_BRUISER][ALTERNATIVE_COMPARISON][OPPORTUNITY_COST][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] When an advanced ranged DD reaches a deficiency in frontal-exchange performance in small-group combat, choose according to the goal and affordable opportunity cost among increasing personal-combat skill and frontal performance as a ranged bruiser, converting range into value such as skirmishing, off-healing, or debuffing, or limiting the use case as a large-scale-combat specialist.
[B0096][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CLAIM][ROLE_BRUISER][RANGED_BRUISER][RANGE_COST][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] In duels between equally skilled players, excluding extreme patch-specific performance disparities, lower-to-intermediate proficiency tends to let the spacing and reduced exposure time provided by range lower the execution burden of a ranged DD; as both players become more proficient at closing distance, defense, and offensive/defensive transitions, that surplus benefit decreases and the difference in frontal offense/defense paid for range tends to appear more strongly in the result.
[B0097][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][PATCH][COND][BURST_DESIGN][RANGE_COST][ALTERNATIVE_COMPARISON] A melee-focused skill configuration may allocate performance in a direction that makes high frontal damage or short-window burst easier to construct, but actual burst suitability varies greatly by class, available skills, subclass configuration, and the current patch, so verify it individually.
[B0098][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][EVAL][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When an advanced build has already secured primary-role performance to a high level, adding effective value from a secondary role while keeping the primary role at the level needed for the use may sometimes yield greater total group contribution from the same performance budget than marginally investing further into the same role.
[B0099][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][OPPORTUNITY_COST] When a DD also performs off-healing, debuffing, or similar work in advanced fixed small-group combat, do not require the same direct pressure as a Pure Bruiser; evaluate whether effective group value from the secondary role compensates for the direct pressure lost. If that recovery of value through the secondary role does not occur, do not justify low direct pressure merely because the build performs another role.
[B0100][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][CAUSE][RANGE_COST][RANGED_INTERVENTION][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][ROLE_SUPPORT][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] For a ranged Skirmisher or ranged off-Support, range can be converted into earlier intervention start, higher intervention frequency, sustained support, and time / positional costs the enemy must pay to approach and deal with that player, so range that was an opportunity cost in comparison with a Pure Bruiser can become a major group value in that role.
[B0101][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When evaluating hybrid roles such as “off-Healer,” “Debuffer,” or “Skirmisher” in advanced fixed small-group combat, do not determine required performance from the role name alone; individually evaluate the offensive pressure, support, positioning, focus-fire handling, and GCD / resource payments actually assigned to the build.

### B5.3 Generative AI: Preventing Misreading of Ranged DD and Hybrid Roles

[B0102][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][SCOPE][LIMIT][RANGED_BRUISER][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not generalize from propositions about the frontal-exchange disadvantage of advanced ranged bruisers into claims such as “ranged DDs in general are weak,” “ranged characters have a universal combat-power penalty,” or “melee is always superior to ranged.” Preserve the use case of pure bruiser and the condition that other performance is actually lost to obtain range, and evaluate suitability for other roles separately. However, do not use this anti-generalization rule as grounds to deny or weaken a confirmed frontal-exchange disadvantage of the ranged side in the same use case.
[B0103][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][RANGED_BRUISER][ROLE_PURE_BRUISER][RANGE_COST][ALTERNATIVE_COMPARISON] When comparing ranged-focused and melee-focused builds serving the same use case, if the ranged side's disadvantage in frontal exchanges due to the opportunity cost of range has been confirmed, do not dilute recognition of that weakness in the use case by adding general statements such as “ranged also has strengths” or “ranged is not weak.”
[B0104][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][RANGED_BRUISER][ROLE_PURE_BRUISER][RANGE_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When evaluating an advanced ranged pure bruiser in 4v4, separate the benefit of range that allows attacks before contact from the benefit of recreating distance or kiting by separately using mobility, LoS, or similar tools. Do not treat the existence of either benefit alone as having already solved insufficient frontal-exchange performance. Verify individually how much each is converted in actual combat into pressure, consumption of enemy time or attention, reduced ally burden, and similar value that recovers the opportunity cost paid.
[B0105][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][ALL_ROLES][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][UNDER_FOCUS] In advanced fixed small-group combat, do not evaluate low personal damage taken by itself as high defensive or survival contribution. If being ignored by the enemy causes attacks and attention to shift disproportionately onto allies, evaluate the player's personal safety separately from effective group contribution including the distribution of enemy focus.
[B0106][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When evaluating a DD / off-Support in advanced fixed small-group combat, do not disqualify it merely because its direct damage is lower than that of a Pure Bruiser; add the effective value produced by the secondary role to the evaluation. [B0107][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][DIAG][RULE][TRADEOFF][ALTERNATIVE_COMPARISON] When a clear weakness in an advanced build is identified, do not deny the weakness itself by listing generic strengths; identify the cause of the weakness, the uses in which it matters, and the room to exchange it for different performance, roles, or operating conditions before evaluating improvement options.
[B0108][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][LIMIT][ROLE_SUPPORT][RANGE_COST][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not infer from the high value that range can create for ranged Skirmishers or ranged off-Supports that melee support DD such as melee off-Healers or melee Debuffers are unsuitable or strictly inferior in advanced fixed small-group combat. [B0109][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][LIMIT][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION][OPPORTUNITY_COST][PATCH][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not generalize a setup in which specific skills, passives, equipment, or other elements compress offense and self-defense or other role functions with exceptionally high efficiency into evidence that ordinary ranged Bruisers can also easily ignore the opportunity cost between damage and self-defense. [B0110][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_JUDGMENT][AI][LIMIT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION] Treat numerical examples such as “90 versus 100” or “80 DD + 30 support” used in explanations of advanced small-group combat as relative models for explaining composition-slot value and opportunity cost, not as measurable common ESO scores or simple additive performance values.
