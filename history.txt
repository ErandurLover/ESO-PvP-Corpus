ESO PvP Corpus — Version History
Current version: v162


================================================================
v162 — Changes from v161
================================================================

Corpus size and proposition identity
- Consolidated or removed 22 low-marginal-value or absorbed Series A propositions.
- The retained content set contains 839 Series A propositions and 205 Series B propositions (1,044 total); Full additionally contains 64 numbered retrieval-control propositions, for 899 Series A and 209 Series B propositions (1,108 total).
- Reassigned the retained Series A content propositions as A0001-A0839 and Series B content propositions as B0001-B0205 in finalized Full retrieval order, and synchronized internal proposition references; Full-only retrieval-control IDs remain A0840-A0899 and B0206-B0209.

Demand-oriented classification and placement
- Reorganized Full around a hierarchical retrieval map that selects one causal domain, places answer-ready core conclusions before reasons and diagnostics, and checks an explicit exit before another domain is added.
- Divided large Full domains into narrow families and subcategories without duplicating or changing proposition text.
- Kept role, location, proficiency, named-object, and advanced-context overlays separate so adjacent but unused information is not retrieved mechanically.
- Separated universal fundamentals, proficiency and learning, role fundamentals, locations and modes, builds, and combat functions into independent retrieval domains.
- Collected beginner and entry-level propositions in A02, organized them by onboarding, foundational skills, role, and battlefield, and retained general build, combat-function, role, and location principles in their substantive domains.
- Moved beginner-specific complete-build copying, evaluation, and food-cost guidance from A03 into A02, and reorganized A03 as a design flow from purpose and diagnostic questions through bottlenecks, opportunity costs, general configuration, and limited-use design while preserving proposition text.
- Reorganized A00 into functional retrieval groups for Corpus scope, proficiency-level application, patch and evidence verification, evaluation materials, user goals, feasible alternatives, and recommendation scope.
- Moved Penetration and range/damage-scaling propositions from A04 into A03 build evaluation, retaining separate child headings under a shared offensive-performance evaluation group while preserving proposition text.
- Moved defensive-layer selection, effective-defense baselines, Critical Resistance assumptions, sustain, mobility, skill-slot compression, usability, and execution-cost propositions into A03 so they are retrieved during build design and evaluation, while preserving proposition text.
- Re-evaluated all 1,044 retained content propositions under a staged retrieval model and separated build evaluation, practical execution, role overlays, and context overlays without changing their IDs or text.
- Added numbered Full retrieval controls for leaf-level acquisition, explicit-axis completion, bidirectional symptom-to-design routing, role and location overlays, and narrow specific-object evaluation exits.
- Consolidated build-performance requirements in A03; narrowed A04-A06 to practical offense, defense and healing, and movement or disengagement respectively.
- Separated advanced role operation into B02 and advanced combat-context operation into B03.
- Added a Manifest staged retrieval path, a retrieval stop rule, and per-page PAGE_FLOW guidance so a model can retrieve one primary page and only the necessary overlays.
- Moved the sustain-dependent-defense propositions into the A03 defensive-performance branch so defensive-design retrieval includes the requirement that sustain cannot substitute for sufficient damage mitigation and effective defense.
- Clarified the negative feedback in which weak per-action defense consumes GCDs, reduces offensive pressure, increases incoming pressure and sustain demand, and further reduces the performance budget for direct defense and damage; also clarified the conditions under which very high sustain is a valid survival-focused specialization.
- Grouped non-beginner Tank, Healer/Support, Bruiser, Skirmisher, Ganker, and Bomber fundamentals under the role domain, while centralizing beginner-specific role material in A02 and keeping location-specific general material in the location domains.
- Separated Series B into advanced common principles, advanced role fundamentals, and context-specific or specialist operation while preserving the beginner-exclusion constraints.

Proposition consolidation and revision
- Corrected A0819 internal references so the relative-benefit statement points explicitly to A0818 and the supporting historical observations A0826-A0828.
- Integrated the 1vX sequence into one actionable process and revised LoS propositions around exposure reduction and reusable terrain value.
- Moved the finite skill-slot and one-second GCD premise into universal fundamentals and expressed both as finite combat resources.
- Reframed build design around purpose and required work, including the selection of sets as means rather than starting points.
- Consolidated group-pressure diversion, role-based self-evaluation, support-role focus and survival, and group-healing delivery methods.
- Expanded the small-exchange sequence so each proposition independently exposes its examples, resource effects, pressure effects, and improvement target.
- Expanded build usability evaluation to distinguish theoretical performance from practical operability and performance realization.
- Moved the PvP defensive baseline into the defensive-value section and consolidated defensive-priority opportunity cost and beginner opponent assumptions.

Retrieval documents
- Updated Full, Split, Core150, and Manifest structure to the v162 demand-oriented placement; Core150 retains the selected 150 proposition identities and follows the finalized Full order.
- Updated orthogonal routing rules, combination examples, demand cues, and contiguous single-range ID scopes in the Manifest and Split metadata; synchronized Split proposition placement to the finalized Full demand domains.
- Reordered Core150 under the current page and heading structure and replaced deleted selections with the revised purpose-and-job build premise and consolidated beginner defensive premise while retaining 150 propositions.

v161 — Changes from v160
================================================================

Corpus size and proposition identity
- Series A now contains 861 propositions and Series B 205 propositions, 1066 total.
- Proposition IDs after inserted propositions were renumbered so the A/B sequences and Split scopes remain continuous and semantically aligned.

Offensive sequence and practice guidance
- Added guidance for varying major attack/CC/Ultimate timing, prioritizing effective offensive sequences over pattern count, placing CC around peak-damage timing, maintaining ordinary and Ultimate-enhanced offensive forms, and stabilizing combo execution on a Target Dummy before adding PvP adaptation.
- Expanded close-range circling guidance to favor tight movement around the opponent while creating only the distance that has a concrete defensive or positional purpose.

Defense, healing, and Bruiser guidance
- Expanded defensive-sequence practice to keep emergency sequences few and reflexive, with an Author View guideline of roughly one or two primary emergency sequences for an ordinary build.
- Expanded healing guidance for inserting the minimum necessary burst/direct healing when HoT no longer maintains a high-HP state under ordinary pressure.
- Expanded advanced Bruiser guidance for predictive defensive-transition timing, primary-attack baseline damage, frontal exchange diagnosis, and evidence requirements when inferring opponent resource exhaustion.

GCD reasoning
- Added the one-second ordinary skill GCD baseline and an AI reasoning rule against assuming sub-second ordinary skill sequences or asymmetric GCD shortening without individually verified mechanical evidence.

Version-fixed corpus files
- The corpus remains version v161; Full, Split, Core150, and Manifest metadata were synchronized to the revised proposition IDs and Split ranges.

v160 — Changes from v159
================================================================

Corpus size and proposition identity
- Proposition IDs were preserved: 855 Series A propositions and 202 Series B propositions, 1057 total.
- No proposition was added or removed.

Japanese proposition text
- Added or normalized English identification at the first occurrence of an ESO term or selected ESO-specific abbreviation in 32 Japanese propositions so each proposition remains identifiable when retrieved alone.
- Updated IDs: A0137, A0139, A0176, A0186, A0187, A0280, A0282, A0444, A0476, A0480, A0502, A0535, A0590, A0691, A0692, A0693, A0732, A0775, A0777, A0779, A0780, A0782, A0786, A0801, A0806, A0841, A0842, A0844, B0048, B0115, B0186, B0187.
- The Japanese Shield Wall section heading was normalized to the standardized Shield Wall-family heading.

Standalone-retrieval wording
- Replaced section-relative wording with an explicit B0111 scope anchor in B0115, B0130, B0131, B0132, B0135, B0136, and B0137 so their intended advanced competitive Skirmisher scope remains identifiable when retrieved alone.
- Expanded BG as Battlegrounds and IC as Imperial City where those abbreviations occur in proposition text or bibliographic records that may be retrieved alone.

Role-tag normalization
- Replaced BOMBER with ROLE_BOMBER in 36 propositions.
- Replaced RANGED_BRUISER with ROLE_RANGED_BRUISER in 10 propositions.
- Role tags are standardized in principle with the ROLE_ prefix.

Shield-tag reorganization
- Consolidated the previous specialized shield tags into a smaller hierarchy.
- Final shield categories: SHIELD (9 propositions), SHIELD_SKILL (4), SHIELD_GEAR (1).
- Removed SHIELD_STACKING, SHIELD_EVALUATION, SHIELD_GCD_VALUE, SHIELD_ORDER, SHIELD_USAGE, NORMAL_SHIELD_DURATION, and SHIELD_BELOW_ONE_HIT.

Alternative/comparison-tag reorganization
- Consolidated ALTERNATIVE_COMPARISON, FEASIBLE_ALTERNATIVES, COUNTERFACTUAL_COMPARISON, CONTRASTIVE_SELECTION, and ALT.
- Final categories: ALTERNATIVE_REASONING (26 propositions) and ALTERNATIVE (6).

Meta-tag reorganization
- Replaced META_FIT and META_THREAT_FIT with AI_META_EVALUATION for AI reasoning about PvP meta conditions (6 propositions total).
- Replaced META_TREND with PVP_META for propositions that present PvP meta information itself (3 propositions).

Block-tag reorganization
- Added BLOCK as the parent tag to all 29 block-related propositions so cross-cutting Block reasoning can be retrieved as one category.
- Retained or introduced specialized subcategories only where independently useful: BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY (7), BLOCK_COST (4), BLOCK_TIMING (2), and BLOCK_FAILURE (1).
- Removed BLOCK_USAGE, BLOCK_DURING_ACTION, BLOCK_DISRUPTION, BLOCK_COST_PRESSURE, and BLOCK_VS_RECOVERY.
- Specialized Block tags are used together with BLOCK.

Author-judgment hierarchy
- Replaced AUTHOR_JUDGMENT with AUTHOR_VIEW.
- AUTHOR_VIEW is assigned to 905 propositions that express evaluations, recommendations, diagnostic criteria, operational standards, decision rules, or closely equivalent author judgments rather than game mechanics alone.
- CORE_JUDGMENT is assigned to 65 propositions representing central judgments in the Corpus decision framework or author judgments with especially strong distinctive character.
- CORE_JUDGMENT always co-occurs with AUTHOR_VIEW and does not mean higher factual certainty or absence of exceptions.

Proposition-type coverage
- Added a semantic proposition-type tag to 22 propositions that previously lacked one.
- Added tags comprised 11 CLAIM, 3 RULE, 3 DEF, 3 REQUIREMENT, and 2 EVAL assignments.

Series B document structure
- Added an independent level-1 heading for Series B in both Full corpora so Series B is not hierarchically nested under the final Series A section.
- Japanese heading: localized Series B — Advanced heading
- English heading: # Series B — Advanced

Version-fixed corpus files
- Updated the version-fixed directories, CORPUS_VERSION metadata, manifests, Core150 metadata, Split metadata, and canonical Corpus URLs from v159 to v160.
