# Part 0 — Application and Interpretation Rules for Generative AI

## Tag Interpretation

TAG_NOTE is not a dictionary enumerating every tag. It provides supplementary definitions for important tags whose interpretive constraints are difficult to convey from the proposition text alone, in order to prevent misapplication during RAG or partial retrieval.
[TAG_NOTE][AUTHOR_VIEW] Indicates an author judgment such as an evaluation, recommendation, or diagnostic criterion adopted by the text rather than a game mechanic itself.
[TAG_NOTE][CORE_JUDGMENT] Indicates a core author judgment within [AUTHOR_VIEW] that materially shapes the Corpus's decision framework or answer policy. It does not mean higher factual certainty or absence of exceptions; preserve applicable conditions and tags such as SCOPE, LIMIT, and PATCH.
[TAG_NOTE][PATCH] Indicates a patch-dependent matter that may require verification of current mechanics. It does not mean short-lived, low-confidence, or scheduled for change; treat it as something to re-check when the relevant mechanics change.
[TAG_NOTE][HEURISTIC] Indicates a practical combat decision principle adopted with consideration for speed of repeated decisions, cognitive load, and usefulness under normal conditions rather than complete optimization every time.
[TAG_NOTE][DEFAULT] Indicates the default judgment to prioritize under normal conditions when no additional information shows an exception.
[TAG_NOTE][OVERRIDE] Indicates that concrete additional information can override a default principle.
[TAG_NOTE][ADVANCED_ONLY] Indicates a proposition for advanced operation that assumes high proficiency.
[TAG_NOTE][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT] Indicates that the proposition must not be used as a beginner performance requirement, achievement condition, failure criterion, or standard recommendation.
[TAG_NOTE][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT] Indicates a goal or practice item that beginners are not required to have already achieved from the start.
[TAG_NOTE][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] Indicates a learning process in which partial success develops into more frequent success and gradual internalization.
[TAG_NOTE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER] Indicates an entry-level beginner whose primary task is first to survive for some time on the battlefield and become familiar with the feel of PvP and the most basic elements of practical combat control.
[TAG_NOTE][GROUP_CONTENT] Indicates content or operation in which the player participates as part of a group and interactions with allies are included in the evaluation.
[TAG_NOTE][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Indicates that effective contribution to the group as a whole, rather than individual freedom of action, is the evaluation axis.
[TAG_NOTE][RANGED_INTERVENTION] Indicates the ability to begin meaningful combat participation through attacks, CC, support, or similar actions before fully reaching a distant engagement or target. It does not mean general ranged damage, general ranged-combat capability, or merely possessing a long-range skill.
[TAG_NOTE][ROLE_SKIRMISHER] Indicates a skirmisher role tendency that uses high mobility to select or change combat locations and to engage, disengage, or re-enter fights. The tag itself does not mean a ranged-focused damage dealer, participation in group content, prioritization of group contribution, repeated rescue of allies across multiple locations, use of ソーサラー（Sorcerer） or ボルトエスケープ（Bolt Escape）／雷光（Streak）, or the advanced competitive operation described in Series B. When tags such as [GROUP_CONTENT], [GROUP_CONTRIBUTION], or [ADVANCED_ONLY] are present, limit the evaluation to the use case satisfying those added conditions. Also, do not infer merely from failure to satisfy the Series B advanced-operation conditions that the operation is not skirmisher play or must instead be bruiser play.
[TAG_NOTE][NATIVE_CLASS_SUITABILITY] Indicates that the evaluation concerns suitability for a use case when operated as a pure class without transplanted subclass skill lines. The tag itself does not imply high or low suitability, completeness, or superiority over subclass configurations.
[TAG_NOTE][SUBCLASS_SUITABILITY] Indicates that the evaluation concerns suitability for a use case in a configuration containing class skill lines transplanted through subclassing. The tag itself does not imply completeness, high suitability, or superiority over pure classes.
[TAG_NOTE][RESOURCE_DEPENDENCE] Indicates a structure in which several survival, offensive, or other functions depend heavily on the same primary resource, so depletion, reduced supply, or suspended supply can disable several functions at once. The tag itself does not mean resource specialization is inappropriate; evaluate demand, supply, availability during crisis, and opportunity cost.
[TAG_NOTE][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE] Indicates the amount of defense against Critical Damage. Evaluate whether the total effective amount is sufficient for the target PvP environment rather than merely whether a source exists, and do not assume ordinary defensive elements, sustain, or planned avoidance have already substituted for it.
[TAG_NOTE][PVP_ASSUMPTION] Indicates that PvP self-defense evaluation must not fix enemy count, willingness to attack, range, mobility, pursuit capability, or similar threat conditions in the user's favor; include threat conditions that can realistically be encountered in the target environment.
[TAG_NOTE][VENGEANCE] Indicates an evaluation target specific to the 復讐（Vengeance） environment, which has dedicated rules, abilities, loadouts, or similar systems different from normal campaigns. Do not automatically generalize strength, weakness, or learning suitability within this environment to normal PvP.
[TAG_NOTE][HIGH_MMR_4V4] Indicates that the evaluation scope is high-MMR 4v4 バトルグラウンド（Battlegrounds）.
[TAG_NOTE][REFERENCE_NOTE] Indicates supplementary reference information such as past mechanics, history, or limited observations that is retained separately from current general propositions. Do not automatically generalize the content of a reference note to the current normal environment.
[TAG_NOTE][REFERENCE_SOURCE] Indicates a bibliographic record for the evidence supporting a corresponding proposition or reference note; do not treat it as an independent proposition about game mechanics, evaluation, or recommendation. Preserve the correspondence with the ID shown as the “evidence target” in the text.

## Rules for Applying the Corpus to Generative AI

[A0001][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][INSTRUCTION][SCOPE] Use ESO PvP Corpus as material for strengthening the reasoning framework of generative AI when it analyzes, explains, or advises on The Elder Scrolls Online（ESO） PvP; do not treat it as the sole source for individual numerical values or current game mechanics themselves.
[A0002][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][INSTRUCTION] Organize ESO PvP decisions around five core principles: “what to do rather than merely what can be done,” “offense and defense are a contest over freedom of action,” “think in opportunity costs,” “interaction is the back-and-forth exchange of offensive and defensive turns,” and “understanding what cannot be done creates strength.”
[A0003][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][INSTRUCTION] When explaining ESO PvP Corpus, do not merely enumerate individual propositions; explain them in an order that makes the relationship between the five ESO PvP principles and each part clear.
[A0004][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][BUILD_SUBSYSTEM_EVALUATION] When explaining a class or build in detail, do not end the analysis by listing verified skills, equipment, passives, and similar elements individually; integrate how they connect in sequence, under what conditions, and over what time windows to create win conditions, recovery, resource loops, and failure conditions.
[A0005][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][SCOPE] Use Series A for general, foundational, broadly applicable judgments including beginners, stable single-axis decision principles, and progressive learning goals that beginners can notice and try. Use Series B for advanced operation that assumes high proficiency and involves simultaneous management of multiple conditions, precise GCD, resource, positioning, line-of-sight, and group opportunity-cost management; do not use Series B as beginner requirements, achievement conditions, failure criteria, or standard recommendations.
[A0006][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE] In beginner-oriented advice, prioritize the foundational principles, practice order, and executable improvements in Series A. Refer to Series B only when the user explicitly asks for advanced operation or when explaining advanced players' operating principles is necessary, and even then do not present those standards as requirements that must immediately be met.
[A0007][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][GOAL][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT] Do not treat goals or practice items presented for beginners in Series A as propositions requiring beginners to have already achieved them from the start; incomplete learning, failure, or forgetting to execute them is not by itself grounds for judging a beginner unqualified, unsuccessful, or lacking understanding.
[A0008][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PRACTICE][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] For beginner advice, do not demand the finished form immediately. Allow progressive acquisition in the sequence of noticing the target behavior, trying it, partially succeeding, increasing the success frequency, and eventually performing it without conscious effort; as a rule, limit current improvement targets to one or a small number of executable items.
[A0009][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][GOAL][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT] Do not expand words such as “basic,” “priority,” or “goal” in beginner-oriented propositions into obligations that must always succeed, participation qualifications, or minimum role-competency requirements.
[A0010][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][PATCH][RULE] When using patch-dependent information such as skills, equipment, or numerical values in advice, verify the current mechanics for the patch at the time of the question and combine them with the long-term decision principles of ESO PvP Corpus.
[A0011][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][PATCH][RULE] Treat opinions, mechanics, and builds from old dates or patches as information belonging to their original environment; do not automatically transplant them into the current environment.
[A0012][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PATCH] Treat `[PATCH]` as a tag indicating that a proposition may need re-verification if the relevant mechanics change; do not infer from the tag alone that the proposition is short-lived, highly volatile, or currently unreliable. Evaluate update resilience separately by checking the historical persistence of the relevant mechanic, frequency of change, and the range of propositions affected when it changes.
[A0013][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PATCH][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Verify current patch mechanics as prerequisite work for patch-dependent analysis, but unless the user is asking about change history, past mechanics, future changes themselves, or a change is important to understanding the current conclusion, do not make statements such as “the game is updated” or “the mechanics used to be different” the main result of the answer. Prioritize using verified current mechanics to analyze present win conditions, interactions, and opportunity costs.
[A0014][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PATCH][TEMPORAL_EVIDENCE_ALIGNMENT] Do not treat official patch notes or other records of mechanic changes as descriptions limited only to their publication date; until a later official change record is confirmed to have changed the same mechanic again, treat them as evidence that the post-change state continued.
[A0015][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PATCH][EVIDENCE][TEMPORAL_EVIDENCE_ALIGNMENT] When verifying current mechanics, do not ask merely whether material close to the current date exists for the mechanic; check whether any official record after the last confirmed official change changed that same mechanic again.
[A0016][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PATCH][EVIDENCE][CLAIM_EVIDENCE_FIT] Do not prefer material describing an old mechanic that was superseded by a later official change record over that later change record merely because the older material is still publicly available or has a newer update date.
[A0017][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][EVIDENCE] When using forum logs as evidence, do not generalize from a single player's statement alone; evaluate evidence strength by checking factors such as the speaker's characteristics and tendencies and the number of people who agree with the opinion.
[A0018][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][PRIORITY] In ESO PvP advice, prioritize what is actually executable under the patch environment, user conditions, and available resources at the time of the question.
[A0019][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][INSTRUCTION][PRIORITY] Match the vocabulary used by generative AI in explanations, advice, and generated writing to the user's language whenever practical. For official ESO terminology such as skill, equipment, and class names, use the official or established localized term when one exists in that language whenever possible; when none exists or localization would reduce meaning or identifiability, retain or add the original term as needed.

## Reasoning Discipline for Generative AI

[A0020][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][PREMISE_VERIFICATION] Even when a question presupposes an evaluation such as “X is strong,” “X is optimal,” or “X is mandatory,” do not adopt that evaluation automatically; first verify whether it is supported in the target environment, then explain the reasons.
[A0021][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PREMISE_VERIFICATION] Even when the questioner appears highly knowledgeable about the target game, build, or mechanics, do not increase the evidentiary weight of evaluations, causal relationships, or comparative conclusions they provide merely because of their knowledge. Test them by the same standard as other premises; support them when supported and correct them when unsupported.
[A0022][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][EPISTEMIC_SEPARATION] When analyzing ESO PvP, distinguish verified game mechanics, observed usage or behavior, inference drawn from them, and recommendations; do not present inferences or recommendations as verified mechanics or observed facts.
[A0023][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][CAUSAL_INFERENCE] The fact that an option is effective or widely used does not by itself establish why it is effective or popular; when causal evidence is insufficient, treat the cause as a hypothesis or as unresolved.
[A0024][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][EVIDENCE][CLAIM_EVIDENCE_FIT] Use a source as evidence only for the type and scope of claim it directly supports, and do not expand its evidentiary reach to a different type of claim without additional evidence.
[A0025][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][EVIDENCE][EPISTEMIC_SEPARATION] When official Japanese material, official English material, in-game displays, or other representations conflict on the same mechanic, do not declare one a mistranslation or error without evidence. Check whether current in-game tooltips, newer official material, or reproducible testing can resolve the conflict; treat unresolved numerical values, proc intervals, and similar details as uncertain.
[A0026][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][EVIDENCE][CLAIM_EVIDENCE_FIT] When evaluating general PvP performance or practicality, treat theoretically possible extreme configurations, temporary states, or extremely rare examples as evidence that those conditions can exist. Unless their representativeness or encounter frequency in the target environment is established, do not give them heavy weight as primary evidence affecting conclusions about normal conditions.
[A0027][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][FORECAST_EVIDENCE] When predicting future patches or meta changes, do not convert technical or logical possibility of change into evidence that the change is realistically likely. Weight probability according to concrete official announcements, continuing design direction, current trends, historical persistence, and similar evidence, and do not substantially reduce confidence in the current evaluation merely because reversal is theoretically possible without concrete evidence.
[A0028][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][RETRIEVAL_EVIDENCE] Do not treat failure to retrieve a relevant proposition through RAG or partial search, by itself, as evidence that the content is absent from the text; if absence must be used in a conclusion, search and verify a sufficiently broad relevant scope.
[A0029][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][PROC][COMPOUND_CLAIM] When a single sentence contains different kinds of claims such as mechanics, observations, causation, comparative evaluation, and recommendation, separate them as needed and verify each with corresponding evidence.
[A0030][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][UNDERDETERMINED_RANKING] When the available evidence and evaluation criteria are insufficient to distinguish rankings among candidates, do not invent an unsupported ranking; identify the candidates that cannot be distinguished and state what additional information or evaluation criterion would be required.
[A0031][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][POPULARITY_EVIDENCE] Adoption rate, repeated recommendations, and broad community agreement can be evidence that an option is generally recognized as useful, but do not by themselves prove mechanical superiority, universal optimality, or suitability for a specific use case.
[A0032][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][CORRECTION_PROPAGATION] If a fact or evaluative premise used in an earlier inference is corrected, re-evaluate downstream conclusions that materially depended on that premise; do not preserve a previous recommendation merely to maintain consistency with an earlier answer.
[A0033][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][PROC][EVALUATION_SCOPE] When evaluating a document, proposition set, build, or similar object, first confirm what it actually contains or claims and what evaluation criteria it uses. Do not later add use cases, data, or evaluation items it was not intended to contain and count their absence as a flaw or penalty. Distinguish evaluation of the object itself from evaluation of what can be done by combining it with external information.
[A0034][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] When presenting alternatives, verify that they are concretely feasible under the target environment, conditions, and resource constraints; do not add possibilities whose feasibility has not been verified merely to create formal balance between viewpoints.
[A0035][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] If a concrete alternative considered is realistically infeasible under the target conditions, do not preserve fictional or impractical possibilities. If only one feasible candidate remains, treat it as the only realistic option.
[A0036][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Good advice narrows the set of options worth considering by excluding candidates that are not worth adopting under the user's objective, conditions, and available resources. Do not use incomplete analysis, lack of information, or avoidance of firm conclusions themselves as reasons to expand the candidate set.
[A0037][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][LIMIT][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Do not expand the option set based only on formal caveats such as “that is overstated,” “not necessarily,” or “it might not be so.” If no concrete option can be shown to be both strong and adoptable under the target conditions, do not present the caveat as a practical alternative.
[A0038][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Advice that expands the option set is justified when it can identify at least one concrete candidate that is realistically adoptable under the target conditions and explain why it is strong for the user's objective using mechanics, data, observation, opportunity cost, or similar evidence.
[A0039][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][PRIORITY][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] In AI advice, prioritize calmly analyzing available data, conditions, and feasibility to maximize the user's practical benefit rather than steering the answer toward a particular direction; do not force real-world evaluation to fit a preferred narrative, symmetry, or hedging language.

## Respecting the Questioner's Goal

[A0040][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][PRIORITY][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE][USER_GOAL] When the questioner explicitly states a goal they want to achieve, the AI should treat achieving that goal as the primary target of advice rather than denying the goal itself or replacing it with a different goal, unless there is a strong reason the goal cannot be treated as the primary advice target, such as being impossible within the target system or incompatible with conditions the questioner has explicitly specified.
[A0041][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][EVALUATION_SCOPE][USER_GOAL] Distinguish respecting the questioner's goal from positively evaluating the difficulty, requirements, opportunity cost, feasibility, or performance efficiency of achieving it. Keep the stated goal as the primary advice target while explicitly explaining when achieving it is difficult, conditional, inefficient, or impossible under the current conditions, together with the relevant constraints and means.
[A0042][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][LIMIT][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE][USER_GOAL] An adjacent alternative goal may be suggested as a supplement, but do not treat it as a replacement for the original goal. If the questioner explicitly rejects that direction, do not repeatedly steer them toward the same alternative goal unless there is a material change in the goal, conditions, available resources, or new information that changes the judgment.
[A0043][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][AUTHOR_REPUTATION_EVIDENCE] A build author's fame, achievements, or community status do not directly prove the performance of the build; prioritize mechanics, composition, measured results, and reproducible observations when evaluating performance.
[A0044][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][INFERRED_TIER] When presenting tiers or rankings without quantitative data such as win rate or sufficient combat logs, explicitly identify them as estimated evaluations inferred from mechanics, composition, and observations rather than measured rankings.
[A0045][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][EVIDENCE][BALANCE_CHANGE_EVIDENCE] A later official nerf or reduction in applicability can be evidence that the changed element was regarded as problematic at the time, but it does not prove the overall optimality or ranking of every specific build that contained that element.
[A0046][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][TEMPORAL_EVIDENCE_ALIGNMENT] When analyzing a past match or build using screenshots or equipment information from a different date, unless the configuration is confirmed to be identical, treat that evidence as supplementary evidence of design tendencies or feasibility rather than exact proof of the configuration used at the earlier time.
[A0047][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][TEMPORAL_EVIDENCE_ALIGNMENT][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] When comparing stats from multiple character sheets or build images, first verify that front/back bar, active sets, weapon traits, buff state, combat state, and other relevant conditions are at the same comparison stage. Do not directly compare values captured under different bars or states as though they represented the same practical combat state.

## Comparison and Recommendation Discipline for Generative AI

[A0048][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Do not attribute an achieved outcome to an included element merely because a configuration containing that element succeeded. If the same objective can be achieved without it, value only the actual incremental difference created by the element, such as success rate, required GCDs, resource cost, input tolerance, or stability.
[A0049][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE][CLASS_OPPORTUNITY_COST] Distinguish being the strongest candidate under a specified class, equipment, skill, or other constraint from being highly competitive among all realistic candidates once that constraint is removed. Do not label an option that only satisfies the former as unconditionally “strong in PvP” or “worth investing in.”
[A0050][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][LIMIT][EPISTEMIC_SEPARATION] When comparing multiple candidates, do not treat assigning an equal number of strengths and weaknesses to each as fairness. Describe only differences supported under the same evidence and evaluation standards, and do not invent advantages or flaws for balance.
[A0051][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][PROC][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] When trying to use a class, equipment, skill, or other element that is disadvantaged in its original role, before defending it as strong in that role, examine whether a concrete adjacent role, battlefield, or configuration can improve competitiveness while making use of verified strengths; present it as an alternative only when a concrete reason for viability can be shown.
[A0052][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][AI_META_EVALUATION] Separate the candidate set used when evaluating enemy threats and counters from the candidate set used when recommending a configuration for the user. For enemy threats, include high-performance configurations realistically encountered in the target environment; for self-use recommendations, center the configurations the user can adopt and their opportunity costs.

# Part 1 — Fundamental Principles and Combat Model of ESO PvP

## Basic Premises

[A0053][PATCH][BASIC_PREMISE] Equipment, skills, numerical values, and relative strengths in ESO PvP change through updates; previously strong options can become weak, and previously overlooked options can become strong.
[A0054][CLAIM][BASIC_PREMISE] ESO PvP contains combat and decision principles that remain useful for long periods even when numerical values change in individual patches.
[A0055][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][BASIC_PREMISE] Among the ESO PvP principles that persist across patches, the foremost are how to attack and how to defend.
[A0056][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BASIC_PREMISE] As the foundation for thinking about offense and defense in ESO PvP, evaluate build design, movement, forcing your own actions through, restricting the opponent's actions, supporting allies, and generating resources.
[A0057][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][BASIC_PREMISE] In ESO PvP, first ask what the character's combat power is for before focusing on the combat power itself.
[A0058][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][BASIC_PREMISE] Treat the ability to connect build design, movement, forcing your own actions through, restricting the opponent's actions, supporting allies, and generating resources to a concrete purpose as one benchmark for becoming a competent ESO PvP player.
[A0059][CLAIM][CAUSE][BASIC_PREMISE] Even if a PvP beginner perfectly copies a completed build, insufficient understanding of how to operate movement, offense, defense, and resource management can cause them to lose to an advanced player using the same build.
[A0060][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][BASIC_PREMISE] ESO PvP is not a game of comparing character specifications alone; even with the same configuration, practical results vary with execution, decisions, and circumstances.

## Preparing Decision Principles in Advance

[A0061][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][SCOPE][COMMON_DECISION][HEURISTIC] For recurring real-time decisions in ESO PvP, do not try to compare every condition from scratch and prove the situational optimum each time. Prepare in advance decision principles to use under normal conditions for frequently occurring situations.
[A0062][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][COMMON_DECISION][DEFAULT] For frequent binary choices in ESO PvP, define in advance which option to prioritize by default under normal conditions when no additional information indicates an exception.
[A0063][CAUSE][COMMON_DECISION] Handling frequent decisions through pre-established principles reduces decision time and cognitive-resource consumption, allowing more time and attention for judgments that genuinely require context-dependent comparison.
[A0064][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][RULE][COMMON_DECISION][OVERRIDE] Pre-established decision principles are not absolute rules. Break them when the current situation contains concrete additional information sufficient to justify doing so, but organize exception handling in advance where practical so deciding whether to break the rule does not itself create unnecessary prolonged deliberation.
[A0065][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][COMMON_DECISION][HEURISTIC] Do not expand a default decision or combat heuristic into a universal claim that it is always optimal in every individual situation or that no exceptions exist.
[A0066][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][EVAL][COMMON_DECISION][HEURISTIC] Evaluate the validity of a combat heuristic by including its fit under normal conditions, reduction in decision time, reduction in cognitive load, losses when misapplied, and the burden required for exception handling.
[A0067][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][COMMON_DECISION][HEURISTIC] Do not judge a combat heuristic wrong merely because exceptions exist or because there is no quantitative data proving superiority across every possible situation.
[A0068][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][EPISTEMIC_SEPARATION][COMMON_DECISION] Treat decision principles without quantitative data not as measured success rates or expected values, but as combat heuristics based on author judgment whose confidence should be managed accordingly.

## Action Opportunities and Offensive/Defensive Turns

[A0069][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY] Treat the idea that “PvP is more a contest over opportunities to act than a contest over raw attack power” as a highest-priority principle.
[A0070][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] The more time you spend attacking and the more time the opponent is forced to spend healing or defending, the more favorable the fight becomes.
[A0071][CAUSE][CLAIM] If the cycle continues in which you spend an action healing → the opponent gains freer attack time → you take more damage → you are forced to heal again → the opponent gains still more attack opportunities, the opponent maintains initiative.
[A0072][DEF][LIMIT] “Offensive turn” is a metaphor for a phase in which your side holds attacking initiative, and “defensive turn” is a metaphor for a phase in which your side is required to respond defensively; these are not formal turn-based mechanics in the game system.
[A0073][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] During combat, recognize which side currently holds attacking initiative.
[A0074][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE] Survive or break the opponent's offensive turn to seize your own offensive turn, then maintain the offensive turn you seized so the opponent cannot simply withstand it.
[A0075][CAUSE] If you attack with enough threat to make the opponent feel endangered, the opponent must spend actions on healing.
[A0076][CAUSE] When the opponent spends an action healing, your own freedom of action increases at that moment.
[A0077][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY] Damage has the function of forcing the opponent into healing or defense and thereby reducing their freedom of action; this function is important in PvP.
[A0078][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG] Even if character-sheet offensive power is high, if practical damage rarely forces the opponent to heal, the attack does not create sufficient pressure.

## Small Exchanges

[A0079][CLAIM] Even with identical builds, one side can become continuously more strained than the other.
[A0080][CAUSE] Small differences—ブロック（Block） only the opponent's strong attacks, having your own strong attacks go unblocked, 打破（Break Free） promptly from the opponent's CC, or landing your own CC cleanly—can tilt the exchange in your favor.
[A0081][CAUSE] Excess healing, missed attacks, important buffs dropping, delayed return to the back bar, and excessive スタミナ（Stamina） spent on ロール回避（Roll Dodge） are representative sources of small disadvantages.
[A0082][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY] Among these small exchanges, strong ブロック（Block） management is one of the first important skills to highlight.
[A0083][CAUSE] When small disadvantages repeat and accumulate, one player can become continuously strained even with identical equipment.
[A0084][CLAIM] Even while maintaining HP near full, resources can gradually become strained.
[A0085][CAUSE] If you use healing skills more often than the opponent, you lose attack opportunities by that amount.
[A0086][CAUSE] Continually losing attack opportunities can gradually make the fight unfavorable.
[A0087][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE] When improving at PvP, do not focus only on successfully landing one major move; continuously improve the accumulation of small exchanges.

# Part 2 — Offensive Design and Pressure Formation

## CC

[A0088][DEF][SCOPE] In this Corpus, CC（Crowd Control） refers to effects that incapacitate the opponent and are subject to 打破（Break Free）. 移動不能（Immobilize） and 減速（Snare） are not included in CC.
[A0089][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY] In PvP, strong CC such as stuns and fears that stop the opponent's free actions is important.
[A0090][CLAIM] CC that creates a period in which the opponent can neither heal nor attack is easy to understand as a trigger for beginning offense.
[A0091][LIMIT] The period for which CC alone can secure initiative is short.
[A0092][CAUSE] Continuing offensive actions after CC can create the possibility of maintaining initiative for several seconds or longer.
[A0093][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] When attacking, create enough pressure that the opponent judges that prioritizing self-defense over counterattacking is necessary to avoid death.
[A0094][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][RULE] Opponents also often initiate combos or rushes from CC, so anticipate this and either survive it through defensive actions or forcibly interrupt it with CC.
[A0095][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] Through attack practice, beginners should aim to gradually increase the situations in which they can move from “use a major attack because I want the kill” toward thinking “land CC and concentrate strong attacks into the short control window”; they do not need to create an appropriate CC opening every time from the start.
[A0096][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] The main uses of CC are to begin your offense, interrupt the opponent's offense, interfere with healing immediately before a kill, and interfere with escape immediately before a kill.
[A0097][CLAIM] A player affected by CC can remove it with 打破（Break Free）.
[A0098][CLAIM] Immediately after using 打破（Break Free）, the player gains a buff granting CC immunity.
[A0099][CLAIM] Immediately after using some skills, potions, or poisons, the player may gain a buff granting CC immunity.
[A0100][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][GROUP_CC][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When an ally needs an important CC to initiate burst, avoid applying your own CC first in a way that causes the opponent to 打破（Break Free） or gain CC immunity and prevents the ally's important CC from landing.
[A0101][LIMIT] CC is not a universal tool that guarantees a kill.
[A0102][CAUSE] Forcing the opponent to 打破（Break Free） also forces them to spend スタミナ（Stamina）.
[A0103][CLAIM] CC therefore also applies pressure to the opponent's resources.

## Burst

[A0104][DEF] Burst means concentrating a large amount of damage into a short time window.
[A0105][LIMIT] Even a single large hit may fail to secure a kill if the opponent can heal after each individual hit.
[A0106][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] For burst, overlap delayed attacks, damage over time, debuffs, CC, strong regular attacks, アルティメット（Ultimate）, follow-up attacks, and executes within a short time window.
[A0107][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BURST_DESIGN] For primary attacks, CC, Ultimate, and similar tools whose counterplay becomes easier when their sequence is predictable, vary their timing within the limits that preserve the attack setup rather than using them from the same interval and the same preparatory pattern every time and allowing the opponent to resolve them with a fixed defensive routine.
[A0108][CAUSE][BURST][BLOCK] If CC that cannot be prevented by ブロック（Block） is timed immediately before a delayed attack lands, it can prevent the opponent from receiving that hit while in block and make it easier to land heavy damage.
[A0109][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][RULE][BURST_DESIGN] In the basic form of an offensive sequence, when the properties of the CC and combo allow it, make the first design priority to place CC immediately before or at the point where maximum damage concentrates, reducing the time in which the opponent can freely answer that peak damage with defense or healing. Treat CC at the opener, during follow-up, during execute phases, and similar placements as branches used when they have a concrete purpose and effective result.
[A0110][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY] What matters in burst is not merely using individually strong abilities in sequence, but creating multiple simultaneous problems that the opponent cannot easily resolve with a single heal.
[A0111][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC] For beginners, think of a burst sequence as preparation → CC → major attack → follow-up.
[A0112][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND] If the opponent escapes or heals after the burst, continue with further follow-up attacks.
[A0113][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][COND][CAUSE] Do not forcibly commit everything when burst conditions are not ready. Representative poor conditions include the opponent being completely healthy, your buffs being down, important debuffs not yet applied, insufficient resources, or CC being unavailable; even committing major resources in such a state is unlikely to produce results.
[A0114][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL] The final objective of a burst combo is to reduce the opponent's health to zero and secure the kill.
[A0115][AUTHOR_VIEW][REQUIREMENT] For a burst-combo kill to function as a win condition, at minimum your strongest-class combo must be capable of pushing the opponent close to death.
[A0116][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BURST_DESIGN] Do not make increasing the number of offensive-sequence patterns an objective in itself; first establish one strong basic combo that is a sufficient threat in real combat and can be executed reliably, then add effective branches that force the opponent to make different choices and predictions.
[A0117][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE] If even your strongest-class combo cannot reduce the opponent's health enough, a win condition based on killing that opponent with a burst combo is unlikely to function; do not fixate on a burst kill and switch to another win condition.
[A0118][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][ALTERNATIVE] When a burst kill is unlikely to function, alternative win conditions include gradually pushing the opponent down with sustained damage pressure, attacking their resources by forcing inefficient combat actions, or abandoning the fight early and disengaging.
[A0119][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][PRESSURE_VS_FINISH] When you fail to secure a kill, distinguish whether “you applied enough pressure to force defense, healing, or disengagement but lacked the final finishing tool” or “the pressure itself was insufficient, leaving the opponent able to continue attacking, applying CC, and adjusting position.”

## Post-Burst Damage Trough and Pressure Between Kill Windows

[A0120][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][BURST_DESIGN][PRESSURE_VS_FINISH] Evaluate the killing power of a burst build not only by the instantaneous damage of its maximum combo, but also by the アルティメット（Ultimate）, special resources, primary attacks, debuffs, and follow-up capability that remain after the combo and the time required to rebuild them. A configuration whose damage falls sharply immediately after the maximum combo is survived can have lower actual killing power than its displayed maximum burst suggests.
[A0121][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BURST_DESIGN][PRESSURE_VS_FINISH] In a build whose primary finishing tools depend on アルティメット（Ultimate）, cooldowns, stacking conditions, or similar mechanics with long preparation times, evaluate not only the strength of the kill window but also whether ordinary pressure between windows can prevent the opponent from fully stabilizing. If the opponent can fully heal and fully prepare every time, merely surviving until the next kill window should not be counted as progress toward a kill.
[A0122][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][BURST_DESIGN][PRESSURE_VS_FINISH] Prepare an ordinary offensive sequence that does not use Ultimate and an enhanced form that adds Ultimate to increase finishing power. The ordinary form should still create enough pressure to require meaningful defense or healing, rather than making Ultimate the sole prerequisite for the offensive sequence to function.

## Countering Kiting and Temporary Disengagement with DoTs

[A0123][AUTHOR_VIEW][ADVANTAGE][DOT] A build with strong DoTs tends to have a countermeasure against play that uses kiting or temporary disengagement even when its direct offensive tools are purely melee attacks.
[A0124][CAUSE][DOT] DoTs can leave part of the offensive pressure active during periods in which direct attack skills cannot be cast because the opponent is kiting or temporarily disengaging.
[A0125][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][DOT][TIME_BUDGET] When an established DoT, persistent attack, or similar effect continues dealing damage without depending on your subsequent active offensive GCDs, evaluate its value not only by total damage but also by its ability to preserve pressure on the opponent while you spend GCDs on healing, defense, buff maintenance, movement, or similar actions.
[A0126][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][DOT][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Layering DoTs on top of an active primary attack such as a spammable can increase sustained offensive pressure relative to repeating the primary attack alone.

## Offensive Stat: Penetration

[A0127][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][PRIORITY][PENETRATION] Do not fix the priority order of offensive stats. Check the current mechanics at the time of the question and the opponent's amount of resistance. Because Penetration can have high expected damage value as long as it does not exceed the opponent's resistance, evaluate it as a priority candidate for increasing damage.
[A0128][LIMIT][PENETRATION] Excess Penetration beyond the opponent's resistance contributes nothing to damage and is therefore completely wasted investment.
[A0129][LIMIT][CAUSE][PENETRATION] Except for healing that depends on damage dealt, investment in Penetration does not directly increase ordinary healing power, so it is basically an investment directed one-way toward offensive performance.
[A0130][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][PENETRATION] Penetration can contribute greatly to damage, but it also depends heavily on the opponent's resistance amount and the combat situation.
[A0131][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PENETRATION] If Penetration will not exceed the opponent's resistance and other offensive components are not required as conditions for the build to function, investment in Penetration is easily justified when the goal is to increase damage.
[A0132][COND][PENETRATION] When the opponent combines resistance with mitigation buffs, they can maintain substantial toughness.
[A0133][LIMIT][PENETRATION] When the opponent combines resistance with mitigation buffs, even securing sufficient Penetration does not remove the defensive effect of the mitigation buffs.
[A0134][CLAIM][PENETRATION] Because of ESO's buff design, the number of layers of mitigation that can be maintained continuously, and the strongly time-limited nature of some buffs with substantial mitigation values, it is not possible to discard resistance entirely and maintain sufficient damage reduction at all times using mitigation buffs alone.

## Conditional Offensive Performance and Defensive State

[A0135][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][COND][CAUSE][BUILD_GENERAL] When your offensive modifier requires a condition such as a health differential, an advantageous state, or a maintained resource, enemy attacks can weaken not only your HP but also the condition that enables that offensive modifier. Because this structure can create a feedback loop of increased enemy pressure → reduced counteroffensive performance → further increased enemy pressure, do not evaluate conditional damage only by its maximum value.
[A0136][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][COND][BUILD_GENERAL] In a configuration where damage dealt, Critical performance, or other offensive performance increases only while favorable combat states such as high self-health, a state advantage over the enemy, or maintenance of a specific buff are present, defensive investments such as Max Health, mitigation, or healing that make those states easier to maintain can also have indirect offensive value. In such cases, do not treat defensive investment as a simple one-way tradeoff against damage; include how much it increases the practical uptime of the offensive condition.

## Effective Evaluation of Percentage Damage and Additional Attacks

[A0137][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][MARGINAL_EFFECT_EVALUATION][DEFENSIVE_LAYER] Even when a damage effect is calculated from a fixed percentage of the target's Max Health as its base value, if that damage is subject to ordinary PvP mitigation, do not equate the displayed percentage with the percentage of the target's HP actually lost. Evaluate it by the effective damage after passing through PvP-specific modifiers, resistance, percentage mitigation, ブロック（Block）, and other defenses applicable to that damage.
[A0138][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][MARGINAL_EFFECT_EVALUATION][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Even when the actual damage of an additional attack is small, if it has value as a trigger for another effect requiring conditions such as “an attack was made” or “an attack hit,” separate its direct damage contribution from its trigger contribution and do not double-count the trigger value as damage from that additional attack.

## Range and Damage Scaling

[A0139][PATCH][DEF][RANGE_CLASSIFICATION] Damage used by players has melee and ranged classifications.
[A0140][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][RULE][RANGE_SCALING] Using the official adjustment examples in A0141 and A0143 as common references, when comparing attacks of the same general purpose at melee and ranged distances without special modifiers, use a scaling baseline of 1.0 for the melee side and 0.9 for the ranged side. If an individual attack has an explicit additional modifier or exceptional scaling, prioritize that individual mechanic.
[A0141][REFERENCE_NOTE][PATCH_HISTORY][CLAIM][RANGE_SCALING] In the official PC/Mac patch notes for Update 28, all ranged Light Attack and Medium Attack damage was reduced by 10% to bring them in line with “ranged damage standards,” while Heavy Attacks were described as already meeting those standards.
[A0142][REFERENCE_SOURCE][EVIDENCE][PATCH_HISTORY][RANGE_SCALING] Evidence target: A0141. ZOS / PC/Mac Patch Notes v6.2.5 - Markarth & Update 28: https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/550927/pc-mac-patch-notes-v6-2-5-markarth-update-28
[A0143][REFERENCE_NOTE][PATCH_HISTORY][CLAIM][RANGE_SCALING] In the official patch notes for Update 35 PTS v8.1.0, the total damage of Crystal Weapon was described as being reduced by 41.5% when changing from “200% of a melee spammable” to “130% of a ranged spammable.”
[A0144][REFERENCE_SOURCE][EVIDENCE][PATCH_HISTORY][RANGE_SCALING] Evidence target: A0143. ZOS / PTS Patch Notes v8.1.0 (Update 35): https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/611025/pts-patch-notes-v8-1-0
[A0145][PATCH][COND][RANGE_SCALING] If the scaling baseline in A0140 is still maintained in the current patch, then among same-type damage skills that receive no additional enhancement from special conditions, ranged skills are disadvantaged in base damage scaling relative to melee skills.
[A0146][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][OPPORTUNITY_COST][RANGE_COST] Range is itself combat performance: it creates benefits in starting attacks, maintaining attacks, and safety, while also carrying opportunity cost when other performance is paid to obtain that range. Therefore, do not evaluate melee and ranged comparisons by displayed damage alone.
[A0147][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][EVAL][RANGE_COST] If the current patch assigns lower damage scaling to the ranged side, then when comparing melee and ranged builds whose other major conditions are similar, evaluate that difference as performance budget allocated to range and include lower damage output on the ranged side as a premise.
[A0148][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][RANGE_COST] In a build that actually sacrifices other performance to obtain range, close-range combat where the range benefit cannot be used can prevent recovery of that opportunity cost and create an overall performance disadvantage.

# Part 3 — Defensive Design, Self-Defense, and Healing

## Critical Burst / Damage-over-Time Environments and Defensive Layers

[A0149][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][BURST_DESIGN][CRITICAL] Designing burst around Critical Damage is easy to structure and tends to produce high practical effectiveness.
[A0150][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][PVP_META][CRITICAL_BURST] PvP environments in which burst through Critical Damage is dominant tend to arise readily in general.
[A0151][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][BURST_DESIGN][CRITICAL][DIRECT_DAMAGE] In burst based on direct damage, Critical Damage is often a major component of damage formation.
[A0152][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BURST_DESIGN][CRITICAL][DIRECT_DAMAGE] When evaluating burst performance based on direct damage in a normal PvP environment, treat Critical Damage as a central evaluation factor.
[A0153][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PVP_META][DOT][DIRECT_DAMAGE] In an environment where damage over time has an extremely large advantage over direct damage, the relative position of burst based on direct damage falls substantially.
[A0154][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PVP_META][DOT][EXECUTION_DIFFICULTY] In an environment where damage over time is dominant, PvP execution difficulty generally decreases.
[A0155][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][DEFENSIVE_LAYER][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL] Except for full tank operation whose primary defensive requirement is maintaining continuous ブロック（Block）, before rating the self-defense performance of a build active in PvP areas highly, explicitly verify whether its total Critical Resistance is sufficient against the Critical burst used by enemy players in the target environment. Do not use a primary PvE purpose, lack of intent to initiate PvP, or a plan to avoid engagement with enemies as reasons to omit this verification.
[A0156][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][CLAIM][GEAR_POPULARITY][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE] In an environment where Critical burst is dominant, defensive sets that provide Critical Resistance tend to become popular among defensive equipment.
[A0157][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][LIMIT][DEFENSIVE_LAYER][DAMAGE_REDUCTION] Even in an environment where Critical burst is dominant, defensive equipment that provides resistance or damage reduction does not lose its value. Do not equate an increase in the relative value or adoption rate of Critical Resistance with other defensive layers becoming unnecessary.
[A0158][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][SCARCITY][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE] Among equipment that provides Critical Resistance, items with practical overall performance are valuable and scarce.
[A0159][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][CLAIM][GEAR_POPULARITY][DOT] In an environment where damage over time is dominant, equipment providing resistance, debuff cleansing, or healing tends to become popular.
[A0160][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][AI_META_EVALUATION][DEFENSIVE_LAYER] When evaluating defensive layers, determine whether the primary damage environment leans toward Critical burst or damage over time, and change the relative value of Critical Resistance, resistance, debuff cleansing, healing, and similar defenses according to that environment.

## Undodgeable Damage and Defensive Tools

[A0161][LIMIT][UNDODGEABLE_DAMAGE] Undodgeable damage cannot be avoided with ロール回避（Roll Dodge）.
[A0162][EX][UNDODGEABLE_DAMAGE] Damage from damage-over-time effects already applied to you and from delayed attacks already applied to you are included among damage treated as undodgeable by ロール回避（Roll Dodge）.
[A0163][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][UNDODGEABLE_DAMAGE] In situations where responding to pressure from damage over time is important, the relative value of ロール回避（Roll Dodge） decreases.
[A0164][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][UNDODGEABLE_DAMAGE] In situations where responding to pressure from damage over time is important, the relative value of debuff cleansing, increased resistance, defensive buffs, healing buffs, and ブロック（Block） increases.
[A0165][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PRIORITY][UNDODGEABLE_DAMAGE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] In situations where damage over time seriously threatens survival, first require that enough damage mitigation and effective defense exist to prevent damage over time from pushing you into lethal range within a short period. Then, if pressure from damage over time remains large or its impact must be actively reduced, treat debuff cleansing as a strong priority response.

## Layered Defense

[A0166][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM] PvP survivability is not determined by a single defensive stat.
[A0167][CLAIM] Defensive layers include Max Health, Physical and Spell Resistance, damage reduction, damage shields, healing over time, burst healing, ブロック（Block）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, distance, movement speed, obstacles, CC, offensive pressure against the opponent, debuff cleansing, and resource reserve.
[A0168][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] Do not conclude that a build is safe merely because one defensive element has been maximized.
[A0169][CAUSE] Even with high resistance, weak healing can prevent recovery of lost HP.
[A0170][CAUSE] Even with strong healing, lack of mitigation at the instant of a burst can result in death before healing is possible.
[A0171][CAUSE] Even with high Max Health, excessively expensive healing can exhaust resources in a long fight.
[A0172][CAUSE][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Even with high movement speed, not knowing an escape route can still allow pursuers to catch you.
[A0173][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM] Strong builds often have structures in which multiple defensive tools cover each other's gaps rather than relying on one enormous defensive element.

## Identifying Defensive Value

[A0174][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][DEFENSIVE_DECISION] Enemy attacks differ in how valuable it is to respond with defensive actions or ロール回避（Roll Dodge）.
[A0175][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][PRACTICE][COMMON_DEFENSIVE_DECISION][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][PRIORITY] In early practice, when you recognize danger and are in a state where you can choose your own defensive action, practice the reflexive basic sequence of first establishing ブロック（Block）, then moving into recovery such as switching to the defensive bar, healing, defensive skills, LoS, or creating distance.
[A0176][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][PRIORITY] When defensive design is used to compensate for beginners tending to make decisions and inputs more slowly than their opponents, prioritize an emergency opening action that begins providing defensive value immediately after input and continues to function while subsequent decisions are being made. Treat prioritizing ブロック（Block） first as a representative initiation principle that fits this design.
[A0177][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PVP_ASSUMPTION][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When explaining the opening defensive response for beginners, do not assume that the enemy player will wait for the questioner's inputs, hesitate to a similar degree, or voluntarily give up attack opportunities. Evaluate response time while assuming an opponent who may input the next attack, CC, or burst faster than the questioner.
[A0178][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][DEFENSIVE_DECISION] The expected loss from using a defensive action against a low-value attack is substantially smaller than the expected loss from failing to defend against an attack with high defensive priority.

## ブロック（Block） Operation and Timing

[A0179][DEF][BLOCK] Distinguish long-duration blocking, which maintains the ブロック（Block） state continuously, from short-duration blocking used only briefly to handle a major attack.
[A0180][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BLOCK] Do not use the opportunity cost of long-duration blocking as a reason to avoid short-duration ブロック（Block） against major attacks that can be blocked.
[A0181][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][BLOCK][BLOCK_TIMING] Treat short-duration ブロック（Block） not as an input timed to a single instant of impact, but as an operation that establishes the block state before the expected impact and holds it until after impact so that the attack arrives during the block-state window.
[A0182][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][BLOCK][BLOCK_TIMING][LAG] In environments with strong network latency or server lag, account for the mismatch between ブロック（Block） input and the actual defensive check by allowing a longer hold margin before and after major attacks than usual.
[A0183][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][TIME_BUDGET] ブロック（Block） is not constrained by the normal active-skill GCD and can be input to establish the block state even while a skill GCD is active.
[A0184][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Because the block state is maintained when bar swapping while holding ブロック（Block）, in an emergency you can establish block first and move to the defensive bar while maintaining that state.
[A0185][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][CAUSE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][TIME_BUDGET] Even when a healing skill must wait for the GCD during an emergency, ブロック（Block） can begin providing defensive value first; therefore, establishing block first tends to produce practical defensive benefit sooner than making healing the first action.
[A0186][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][PRIORITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] For beginner emergency defense, make the basic practice sequence: recognize danger, first establish ブロック（Block）, perform any necessary bar swap while maintaining it, and proceed to healing, defensive skills, or other recovery as soon as the GCD becomes available.
[A0187][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][BLOCK][CC][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When a blockable CC can be blocked in advance, its value includes not only preventing the CC itself but also preventing upstream the unfavorable chain of the CC removing ブロック（Block）, spending スタミナ（Stamina） on 打破（Break Free）, exposure to the following burst, and rebuilding defense.
[A0188][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][BLOCK][BLOCK_COST] A state in which the スタミナ（Stamina） margin needed for ブロック（Block） has been lost is not a general reason to place healing before block; diagnose it separately as a dangerous state in which the emergency Stamina reserve or practical-combat sustain has already been exhausted.
[A0189][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] While incapacitated by CC that requires 打破（Break Free）, the ブロック（Block） state is removed.
[A0190][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] During the 打破（Break Free） animation, the ブロック（Block） state can be established through block input.
[A0191][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] During ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, the ブロック（Block） state can be established through block input.
[A0192][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][PRACTICE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] While already incapacitated by CC that requires 打破（Break Free）, treat Break Free as state recovery that must precede ordinary voluntary defensive choices. After CC removes the ブロック（Block） state, or when performing Break Free or ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, when possible begin block input during that action to reduce the time in which the immediately following burst or delayed attack can hit you while unprotected.
[A0193][PATCH][CLAIM][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY] Ordinary instant skills without casting or channeling can be used while maintaining ブロック（Block） input, and do not require releasing block to use the skill.
[A0194][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE][PRIORITY] When explaining the defensive order for beginners in danger, do not generate sequences such as “switch to the defensive bar, heal, then ブロック（Block）” or “heal, then block” as standard procedure; show block as the first voluntary defensive action.
[A0195][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][LIMIT][CC][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use the state of already being incapacitated by CC that requires 打破（Break Free） as a counterexample to the principle of initiating defense with ブロック（Block） first. Treat the CC state as one in which normal voluntary defensive choices are restricted, and explain state recovery through Break Free together with re-establishing block as early as possible.
[A0196][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][LIMIT][BLOCK][BLOCK_COST][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use having no スタミナ（Stamina） remaining for ブロック（Block） as grounds for making heal-first the standard beginner recommendation. Diagnose that state separately as depletion of the emergency resource reserve.
[A0197][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PRIORITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When listing multiple emergency defensive actions for beginners, do not present them as unordered alternatives in a way that loses the opening priority of ブロック（Block）. In a context that deals with the initiation order during danger, explicitly place block first.
[A0198][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][LIMIT][BLOCK][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use the existence of individual attacks that cannot be handled with ブロック（Block） as grounds for changing the general beginner emergency defensive sequence to heal-first. When asked how to handle a specific attack, explain the defense appropriate to that attack individually.
[A0199][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][PATCH][RULE][EPISTEMIC_SEPARATION][BLOCK][BLOCK_ACTION_COMPATIBILITY][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When explaining defensive order on the basis of mechanics such as ブロック（Block） not being constrained by the GCD, bar swapping while blocking, or using skills while blocking, verify the current mechanics at the time of the question and distinguish verified mechanics from the author judgment that beginners should use block as their first voluntary defense.
[A0200][PATCH][CAUSE][BLOCK][BLOCK_COST] ブロック（Block） cost increases according to the number of cost-collection checks that occur while blocking, not according to the amount of incoming damage. Therefore, attacks that hit repeatedly across the internal cooldown tend to pressure the opponent's block resource more than a single-hit attack.
[A0201][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][BLOCK] Do not evaluate the value of multi-hit attacks only by ブロック（Block） cost pressure; normally treat it as secondary value added to primary functions such as damage, pressure formation, and accompanying effects.
[A0202][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][DIAG][BLOCK][BLOCK_FAILURE] Even when the character's ブロック（Block） animation or block effect is displayed, network latency or block-related faults can cause the actual defensive check not to be established. If you suspect block has failed while holding it for a long time, release the input once, input it again, and re-establish the block state.

## Toughness, Recovery, and Defensive Design

[A0203][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][RULE] Evaluate defensive performance on two axes: “how difficult it is for HP to decrease” and “how lightly the burden is to restore reduced HP to near full.” Do not equate only one of those axes with high defensive performance.
[A0204][CLAIM][DEATH_STATE][HEALING_LIMIT] After HP reaches 0 and the character dies, ordinary healing skills cannot normally be used by that character to return themselves to a living state.
[A0205][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][LETHAL_DAMAGE_PREVENTION] In situations with a possibility of lethal damage, prioritize reducing incoming damage and surviving without letting HP reach 0 over conserving resources.
[A0206][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][BLOCK] When a burst that can be ブロック（Block）ed can be blocked to reduce incoming damage, doing so tends to reduce the amount of HP rewind required and the associated burden of healing actions and healing resources compared with taking it unprotected and then healing the lost HP afterward.
[A0207][CAUSE] Even if HP decreases slowly, if recovery requires many actions, you cannot attack during those actions.
[A0208][CAUSE] Even if you are somewhat easier to damage, being able to recover a large amount with one or two actions can return you to the fight quickly.
[A0209][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] As with an offensive burst sequence, when entering a defensive turn, first establish Block as the first voluntary defense, use that defensive time to connect healing, defense, and positional adjustment, and prepare in advance for each build a short recovery sequence that returns you to a state where you can fight again; practice it repeatedly. For an ordinary build, do not make increasing the number of emergency defensive-sequence patterns an objective in itself; narrow them to roughly one or two primary sequences that are effective and practice until they can be executed reflexively under severe pressure.
[A0210][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][CAUSE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Defensive design should secure not only ordinary defensive performance but also a means to recover from danger back to a state where you can fight again. If this is insufficient, pressure that would normally be manageable is more likely to become a major threat.

## Transition into Defensive Turns and Self-Defense Burden

[A0211][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][SCOPE] For a build whose role requires returning to offense, evaluate self-defense not only by how many defensive actions can be repeated, but also by how often exchanges with equally capable enemies send the build into a defensive turn first and how many GCDs and how much time are lost before returning from defense to offense. Do not apply this evaluation uniformly to tanks whose job itself is to keep absorbing enemy pressure while maintaining a defensive state.

## Healing over Time and Emergency Healing

[A0212][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT] In PvP, even when a healer is present, do not make it standard operation to abandon executable self-defense and self-healing and delegate your HP management entirely to the healer.
[A0213][CLAIM] Healing over time continuously offsets small amounts of damage during combat.
[A0214][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][RULE][HEALING] Not only when a large amount of HP is lost at once, but also when healing over time (HoT) fails to keep up with ordinary incoming pressure and high HP begins to erode, insert the minimum necessary burst heal or direct heal before the situation becomes seriously dangerous and restore HP to a high level.
[A0215][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] For beginner builds, treat a configuration with both healing over time that works routinely and a larger heal for danger as a basic candidate for early learning, and make it a goal to gradually increase the situations in which the beginner can distinguish and use both appropriately.

[A0221][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Beginning the next offensive/defensive exchange below maximum HP reduces the health margin available to absorb the next incoming pressure by the amount of unrecovered HP; therefore, do not treat it as the standard state for a beginner to return to offense.
[A0222][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][RULE][HEALING][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Treat specialized configurations that intentionally gain offensive performance or other benefits from reductions in their own HP as exceptional operation separate from the normal-PvP principle of maintaining maximum HP; do not recommend maintaining low HP for general PvP builds merely because such configurations exist.
[A0223][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Aiming for maximum HP does not mean mechanically spamming burst heals until maximum HP is reached. [A0224][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] When evaluating healing performance, check not only whether a large amount of HP can be restored once, but also how well high HP can be retained after ordinary defensive and healing actions.
[A0225][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][DOT][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Treat the typical pattern of losing to sustained pressure not as healing stopping at a particular HP value, but as repeatedly restoring to high HP and then being worn down again, with the HP level at which healing begins or ends gradually worsening through those repetitions.
[A0226][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][CAUSE][DOT][HEALING][TIME_BUDGET] If a pattern repeatedly occurs in which you restore to high HP through healing but are quickly worn down again, treat it as an unfavorable exchange in which the opponent's sustained pressure continues consuming your healing and defensive budget.
[A0227][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][PRIORITY][HEALING][DOT][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Pressure so severe that HP barely increases even after using a large burst heal should not be treated as gradual sustained pressure from ordinary DoTs alone; treat it as a major danger state in which total incoming pressure over a short period is approaching the amount of the burst heal.
[A0228][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEFAULT][PRIORITY][HEALING][ESCAPE_ROUTE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] If you cannot retain high HP after healing against sustained pressure and have no clear reversal condition, consider early disengagement while HP and emergency resources still remain sufficiently available.
[A0229][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][DOT][ESCAPE_ROUTE][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Persistent pressure such as already-applied DoTs does not disappear immediately merely because disengagement has begun; therefore, beginning disengagement while there is still margin in HP and defensive resources tends to preserve more safety margin than waiting until HP has fallen into a dangerous range.
[A0230][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][HEALING][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use reaching maximum HP as the only condition for returning to offense; separately verify an emergency スタミナ（Stamina） reserve needed for 打破（Break Free）, ブロック（Block）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, and similar actions.
[A0231][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] For a beginner's criterion for ending a defensive turn and returning to offense, do not generate fixed HP thresholds such as “having at least a certain proportion of HP is enough” or “returning to maximum HP is unnecessary.”
[A0232][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not generalize to the standard beginner procedure for ending defense and returning to offense the situational judgment of intermediate or advanced players who read initiative, remaining healing-over-time effects, enemy attack preparation, and similar factors and turn to offense below maximum HP.
[A0233][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use A0203's defensive-performance evaluation of whether one can “return to near full” as grounds for claiming that beginners need not recover to maximum HP. Separate the evaluation axis for defensive performance from the beginner criterion for returning to offense.
[A0234][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][HEALING][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not use the diagnostic example in A0342 that “survival may be possible at low HP when emergency スタミナ（Stamina） remains” as grounds for returning to offense from low HP. Treat that proposition as an example showing the importance of emergency resource margin.
[A0235][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not convert the goal of “maintaining maximum HP” into an instruction to mechanically spam burst heals until maximum HP is reached. Explain recovery including retention of restored HP, healing over time, defense, positioning adjustments, and disengagement when necessary.
[A0236][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][DOT][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When explaining advantage or disadvantage under sustained pressure, do not establish a particular HP percentage as a “wall above which healing is impossible.” Evaluate whether restored HP can be retained and whether the subsequent HP trend improves or worsens.
[A0237][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][LIMIT][DOT][EPISTEMIC_SEPARATION] Do not generalize from the opponent's sustained pressure being superior in the current exchange to the enemy build being superior to your build in every capability and every situation. At the same time, do not obscure the fact that the current exchange is unfavorable by listing general strengths.
[A0238][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][HEALING][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] When explaining specialized equipment or skills that gain benefits at low HP, or special operation such as Ganker / Bomber play, do not generalize that operation to HP management in normal PvP; separate it as an intentional performance tradeoff.

## Evaluation of Defensive Equipment

[A0239][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][HEALING_GEAR] Evaluate healing equipment not only by the amount healed but also by whether the healing effect occurs at the required timing.
[A0240][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][RESISTANCE_GEAR] Evaluate resistance equipment not only by the amount of resistance gained but also by whether the resistance increase occurs at the required timing.
[A0241][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][HEALING_GEAR] When evaluating healing equipment, consider not only the displayed effect but also the effective cooldown.
[A0242][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][HEALING_GEAR][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] A PvP healer should first establish baseline healing through skill rotation; evaluate healing equipment not as a substitute for baseline healing but as supplemental healing or added value such as buffs and debuffs.
[A0243][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PRIORITY][LIMIT][BEGINNER_HEALER][HEALING_GEAR][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] For beginner healer builds intended to operate without belonging to a large group, including PUG situations, prioritize healer equipment whose effects are not limited only to group members. Do not recommend support equipment whose effect targets only group members as equipment for the purpose of support in that use case.
[A0244][AUTHOR_VIEW][ADVANTAGE][LIMIT][SHIELD_GEAR] Equipment that grants shields is less likely than healing equipment to be wasted through overhealing, but it does not directly heal and rewind HP that has already been lost.
[A0245][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][GOAL][DEFENSIVE_GEAR] Use healing, resistance, and shield equipment not to replace active self-defense actions, but to support that self-defense, extend the time before death, and support active HP rewind.

## Shield Stacking, GCD Value, and Damage Calculation

[A0246][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][SHIELD_SKILL] The primary purpose of shield stacking in PvP is not to extend the displayed duration of a shield, but to layer multiple separate shield durability values and increase the amount of damage and number of offensive actions required before the enemy can reach actual HP.
[A0247][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][SHIELD_SKILL] When under continuous pressure from an equal or stronger opponent, normally assume that ordinary shields other than exceptions such as アルティメット（Ultimate） will disappear from durability depletion before their displayed duration expires; do not treat displayed duration as guaranteed safe time.
[A0248][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][SHIELD_SKILL] Even for a shield granted by アルティメット（Ultimate）, do not group it together with ordinary shields; evaluate the skill effect and shield amount individually.
[A0249][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][SHIELD] When evaluating the defensive value of a shield, look not only at absorption amount but also at whether the shield can require an additional enemy attack GCD or how much HP debt it can prevent from arising during the 1 GCD you spend on another action.
[A0250][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][SHIELD] Do not treat a shield clearly smaller than the general 1-GCD damage of an equal opponent as a shield that negates one enemy GCD.
[A0251][AUTHOR_VIEW][ADVANTAGE][SHIELD] Even a shield clearly smaller than the general 1-GCD damage of an equal opponent can have value as supplemental defense that reduces HP debt when attached to an action serving another purpose.
[A0252][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][SHIELD_SKILL] When multiple shields are used simultaneously and each shield has different additional effects when damaged or destroyed, which shield absorbs damage first also affects offensive or reflection effects; therefore, treat cast order itself as a tactic.
[A0253][PATCH][CLAIM][SHIELD] Defensive mitigation from ブロック（Block） does not apply to damage dealt to shields.
[A0254][PATCH][CLAIM][SHIELD] Critical Damage can occur against shields.
[A0255][PATCH][CLAIM][SHIELD] Resistance and damage reduction from buffs apply to damage dealt to shields.
[A0256][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][SHIELD][RULE] Because shield damage-calculation mechanics depend on the patch environment, when treating the application of ブロック（Block） mitigation, Criticals, resistance, or incoming-damage reduction precisely, verify the current mechanics in official patch notes at the time of the question.
[A0257][CLAIM][SHIELD] The shield damage-calculation mechanics in which ブロック（Block） mitigation does not apply, Criticals can occur, and resistance and incoming-damage reduction do apply have been maintained for a long period.
[A0258][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][SHIELD] In PvP, shields alone may not always absorb everything; operate with the expectation that damage can reach health.

## Indirect Self-Defense Value of Sustain

[A0259][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][SCOPE] The ability to repeat defensive actions for a long time through high sustain can support longer survival time, but do not infer from that alone that the build has strong offensive return capability or high combat initiative. Whether ending the defensive turn and returning to offense or disengagement is a required condition for a high rating depends on the build's role and win condition.
[A0260][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][CAUSE][LIMIT][DIAG][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][TIME_BUDGET] In a configuration with abundant sustain but insufficient per-use defense or healing against enemy pressure in the target environment, do not judge survivability sufficient merely because large sustain allows many defensive actions to be repeated. If processing enemy pressure requires spending most GCDs on defense and prevents transition to offense, CC, movement, or recovery, the marginal value of additional sustain falls sharply; suspect insufficient effective performance per defensive action.
[A0261][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] When diagnosing a self-defense build, separately examine “how many times defensive actions can be used” and “how many seconds or GCDs of safety or counterattack opportunity are gained from one defensive action.” If only the former is high and the latter is low, treat the problem as insufficient efficiency per defensive action rather than insufficient sustain.

## Effective Defense and Sustain-Dependence Structure

[A0262][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][LIMIT][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL][PVP_BASELINE] Evaluate build survivability not by the presence or count of defensive elements, but by whether the combined effective defense provides sufficient damage mitigation, health margin, time to heal, and room to recover against burst and sustained pressure realistically encountered in the target PvP environment. Do not treat merely possessing defensive skills, resistance buffs, healing, shields, or similar tools normally found in general PvP builds as evidence of sufficient self-defense or above-standard toughness.
[A0263][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][LIMIT][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL] Before rating sustain as a survivability strength, require verification that sufficient damage mitigation and effective defense are established against incoming damage in the target PvP environment. Do not use high recovery values, Max Resources, resource-restoration capability, or the number of times escape actions can be repeated as evidence substituting for insufficient damage mitigation, health margin, Critical Resistance, or similar direct defenses. Do not rate a build with insufficient direct defense as highly survivable merely because it has many resources.
[A0264][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][LIMIT][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] In a configuration that dies to enemy burst or sustained pressure before it can spend enough resources on defense, healing, disengagement, or similar actions, unused resources remaining at death do not function as survivability that could prevent that death.
[A0265][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Do not evaluate sustain only by the size of recovery values, Max Resources, or restoration amounts; compare supply against the actual resource demand generated by maintaining offense, defense, healing, 打破（Break Free）, movement, and similar actions under enemy pressure in the target environment. Even with high supply, do not rate practical sustain as high when demand is still greater.
[A0266][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][RESOURCE_DEPENDENCE] The more a build concentrates most survival tools into one or a small number of actions consuming a specific resource, the higher the practical sustain level required for that resource becomes. Do not highly rate a build with strong dependence on a specific resource as highly survivable unless it is confirmed that the required amount of that resource can be maintained reliably.
[A0267][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][RESOURCE_DEPENDENCE] When multiple survival tools depend strongly on the same resource, do not evaluate them as fully independent defensive layers. If using one causes the shared resource required by another to become insufficient, evaluate the common resource dependence as a single point of vulnerability.
[A0268][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][SUSTAIN][RESOURCE_DEPENDENCE] If regeneration or supply of the resource on which the primary defense depends is reduced or suspended while defending, moving, or in a specific state, do not treat high normal-state recovery values directly as crisis-state practical sustain.
[A0269][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][SUSTAIN][RESOURCE_DEPENDENCE] When an action relied upon for survival loses sustain efficiency with repeated use over a short period because of cost escalation, worsening supply conditions, or other reasons, do not estimate sustainable duration from the first-use cost or displayed recovery value alone.
[A0270][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] For a build that requires extremely high sustain, before rating that sustain as a strength, diagnose whether the required amount itself has become excessive because of insufficient defensive strength, high-frequency reuse of defensive actions, dependence on a single resource, or similar causes.
[A0271][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][DIAG][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][OPPORTUNITY_COST] When insufficient defensive strength increases the required frequency of defensive actions, which increases sustain investment needed to maintain them, which in turn further reduces the performance budget available for direct defense, treat this cycle as negative feedback in defensive design.
[A0272][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][OPPORTUNITY_COST][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] If extremely high sustain actually makes a particular defensive design function, do not deny that functionality itself. However, identify the resistance, damage reduction, Max Health, healing, damage, equipment slots, and other performance budget lost to obtain that sustain, and evaluate it as opportunity cost of the build as a whole.
[A0273][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][RESOURCE_DEPENDENCE] Do not judge allocating a large amount of performance budget to a specific resource as a flaw by itself. If effective defense required by the target environment is otherwise sufficiently established, the resource investment reliably supports multiple effective actions, and other important resources or performance remain at practical levels, evaluate the specialization individually as a functioning design.

## Defensive Margin for Beginners

[A0274][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Do not make configurations that greatly increase only resources, or only resources and damage, without sufficiently supplementing survivability a standard recommendation for entry-level beginners. Do not make beginner survival depend on continuously maintaining high precision in early enemy detection, danger prediction, optimal positioning and escape-route selection, accurate resource management, and appropriate defensive inputs within short windows. Secure defensive margin that can absorb a certain amount of error when judgment or operation is delayed. Do not treat a low-defense configuration that survives only when ideal early detection, avoidance, and escape succeed as having sufficient survivability for beginners.
[A0275][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL][MOVEMENT_SPEED][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][ESCAPE_ROUTE] In builds for entry-level beginners, do not permit insufficient effective defense against the target PvP environment merely because the build has high mobility or escape capability. Survival through mobility depends on when danger is detected, positioning, terrain and route, remaining resources, enemy pursuit capability, and similar factors; separately evaluate defensive margin that can absorb some error when judgment or operation is delayed or when damage is taken before escape begins.

## Defensive Investment, Effective Values, and Offense-Defense Transition

[A0276][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][SUSTAIN] When a primary defensive skill is central to a build, distinguish investment that increases the absorption, healing, or effective durability of one use of that skill from resource investment that only increases how many times it can be reused; verify that increasing only the latter has not left the defensive value of each use insufficient.
[A0277][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][TIME_BUDGET] For a defensive effect that is strong only immediately after activation and then becomes substantially weaker, do not treat the displayed total duration as safe time at the same strength. Evaluate how long the primary defensive strength is maintained and how many GCDs that one use allows before transitioning to the next attack, heal, CC, or similar action.
[A0278][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][SUSTAIN][TIME_BUDGET] Investment in resistance, incoming-damage reduction, shield amount, and similar defenses can save resources and GCDs by reducing the number of heals or shield recasts required. Therefore, when adjusting sustain, compare not only increases in recovery but also reductions in resource demand produced by stronger defense.
[A0279][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][MARGINAL_EFFECT_EVALUATION] When investment in a stat simultaneously increases not only unshielded durability but also shields or healing that scale with that stat, or the effective value of other defensive layers, evaluate the combined increments. Do not compare each defensive layer only as an independent investment.
[A0280][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] A configuration that survives sufficiently on the back bar but sharply loses resistance, mitigation, healing, or similar defense immediately upon switching to the offensive bar and is forced back to the back bar at once by continuing enemy pressure has difficulty creating an effective offensive turn. Evaluate build durability not only on the defensive bar but also in the state after transitioning to the offensive bar.

## Scope of Enemy-Threat Evaluation

[A0281][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][AI_META_EVALUATION] When evaluating the threat level of enemy classes, equipment, skills, or similar elements, do not represent them only by average or typically weak configurations. Include strong configurations that can realistically function and be encountered in the target environment, and do not generalize weakness in one specific role into denial of threats the same element can present in another practical role.

## Reinforced Trait on 重装鎧（Heavy Armor） Chest

[A0282][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][EVAL][HEAVY_ARMOR][REINFORCED_TRAIT][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Evaluate the value of the 強化（Reinforced） trait on a 重装鎧（Heavy Armor） chest piece not only by the trait percentage, but also by the absolute resistance increase gained because the percentage modifier applies to the increased armor value of the high-base-armor chest piece after quality improvement.

## Situational Awareness and Anti-Stealth

[A0283][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][HEURISTIC][SITUATIONAL_AWARENESS] Build situational awareness in PvP not by staring at the surroundings for long periods and stopping combat processing, but by using concepts of situational awareness, visual scanning, and attention switching to make it a habit of brief repeated scans compatible with combat, skill, and resource checks.
[A0284][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][LIMIT][STEALTH_COUNTER] After stealth has been revealed or removed, current re-stealth conditions, reuse restrictions, continuing reveal effects, or similar mechanics may prevent an immediate return to stealth or invisibility; do not assume that an enemy once detected can instantly return to full stealth.
[A0285][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][RULE][STEALTH_COUNTER] Anti-stealth effects include effects that continue revealing targets who enter stealth or invisibility and effects that prevent activation of stealth or invisibility itself for a period; do not treat the two as the same anti-stealth effect.
[A0286][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][STEALTH_COUNTER] 光輝く灯明（Radiant Magelight） is particularly valuable for beginners as a strong defensive option against stealth gankers because, while slotted, it can prevent Slam Stun originating from stealth attacks. [A0287][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][RULE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][STEALTH_COUNTER] When using the 暴きの閃光（Revealing Flare） line for anti-stealth purposes, evaluate it less as a tool for searching for an enemy hiding somewhere and more as area denial that creates a zone difficult for enemies to approach or pass through while remaining stealthed; for beginners, treat 残存する閃光（Lingering Flare）, which can create a persistent area, as a strong candidate.

## Critical Resistance, Enemy Assumptions, and Defensive Modifications

[A0288][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] When evaluating Critical Resistance, evaluate whether the effective total from all sources is sufficient against Critical Damage in the target PvP environment, not merely whether any source exists. Do not treat merely having a small amount of Critical Resistance as evidence that sufficient defense against Critical burst has been established.
[A0289][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] For entry-level beginners active in PvP areas, do not make it a standard recommendation to shift armor traits heavily toward performance other than Critical Resistance without first confirming from data that sufficient Critical Resistance has been secured. In particular, do not make survival depend on anticipating enemy attacks in advance and defending appropriately.
[A0290][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][CRITICAL_RESISTANCE][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY][OPPORTUNITY_COST] Do not automatically reduce Critical Resistance previously obtained from armor traits or similar sources merely because Critical Resistance is also obtained from an equipment set or another effect. Shift that performance budget to other purposes only when data confirms that total Critical Resistance after the reduction remains sufficient for the target use case.
[A0291][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PVP_ASSUMPTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] When evaluating self-defense performance of a build active in PvP areas, do not implicitly assume favorable enemy conditions such as enemy players not attacking, having low pursuit capability, having no more mobility than you, or being unable to perform effective attacks or CC from range. Include enemy capabilities realistically encountered in the target environment.
[A0292][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][PVP_ASSUMPTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Except where participation count is explicitly limited by the system, such as in a duel, do not evaluate self-defense performance in PvP content under the assumption that there is always only one enemy. Evaluate survivability including the possibility of receiving pressure from multiple enemies within a short period.
[A0293][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][DIAG][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL] For a build whose original effective defense is substantially insufficient for the target PvP environment, do not judge the problem solved merely because one defensive element was added. Re-evaluate the entire modified build including resistance, mitigation, health, Critical Resistance, healing, shields, sustain, and available uptime; if effective defense remains insufficient, do not rate the modification highly.
[A0294][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL] Do not treat the number of added defensive elements as the amount of increased defensive performance. One skill, one equipment effect, one armor trait, and similar elements differ in effective mitigation, healing, defendable time, activation condition, uptime, and opportunity cost; do not judge them as equivalent self-defense improvements merely because “one defensive element was added.”
[A0295][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] When effective defense is insufficient in a build for an entry-level beginner and the build has no equipment whose primary function is defense other than sustain, strongly consider introducing at least one defensive five-piece set as a major early improvement candidate. Do not assume that adding a single defensive skill can cover the entire deficit.
[A0296][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] Do not treat a defensive five-piece set and one defensive skill as comparable defensive investments merely because each is “one defensive element.” When judging that one ordinary defensive skill can replace defensive investment consuming dedicated equipment slots, actually compare effective defense before and after the change and verify that the replacement still establishes the defensive strength required by the target environment.
[A0297][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][LIMIT][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] A configuration without a defensive five-piece set can still establish effective defense for the target environment by securing sufficient resistance, mitigation, Critical Resistance, health, healing, shields, and other defenses. However, establishing this requires concrete data analysis, and configurations with higher operational or decision demands should not be standard recommendations for entry-level beginners.

## AI Control for Self-Defense Evaluation in PvP Areas

[A0298][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][LIMIT][PVP_ASSUMPTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] For builds expected to operate in PvP areas for more than a short period, do not omit verification of effective defense against players because the player does not intend to initiate PvP, has PvE as the primary objective, plans to avoid enemies, or has high mobility or escape tools.
[A0299][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PVP_ASSUMPTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL] For a build used in a PvP area where enemy players can actually attack it, evaluate survivability when receiving realistic PvP burst in the target environment.
[A0300][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][PVP_ASSUMPTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][BUILD_GENERAL][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not rate a configuration whose sufficient effective defense has not been confirmed from data as safe or beginner-friendly merely because it plans to avoid combat, has mobility, sustain, or similar advantages.

# Part 4 — Movement, Positioning, and Disengagement

## LoS

[A0301][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY] LoS is a top-tier defensive asset.
[A0302][DEF] LoS（Line of Sight） is the concept of whether there is a direct line of vision between you and the enemy; a representative use is employing fixed objects to make it harder for the opponent to target you directly.
[A0303][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT] The purpose of using obstacles is not to become permanently invulnerable.
[A0304][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY] The primary purpose of using obstacles is to reduce the amount of time you are exposed to enemy attacks.
[A0305][EX] One example goal of using LoS is to reduce a situation where you would be attacked continuously for 10 seconds to roughly 5 seconds of actual exposure.
[A0306][CAUSE] Time created by LoS during which you are not being attacked can be used for healing, recovering resources, refreshing buffs, regrouping with allies, or preparing the next attack.
[A0307][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM] Simply reducing the amount of time you are being attacked can greatly change combat difficulty.

## Circling Obstacles

[A0308][CLAIM] After learning LoS, players tend to want to keep circling continuously around obstacles.
[A0309][CAUSE] If you begin a wide circle ahead of the opponent, the opponent can run the shorter inside route and catch up.
[A0310][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] When circling an obstacle, focus on shifting to the opposite side after the opponent moves.
[A0311][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE] In early obstacle-circling practice, do not require that the enemy can never catch you; treat even a partial reduction in the time the enemy can attack as an effective improvement.

## Circling on Open Ground

[A0312][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][FLAT_TERRAIN_COMBAT] Even on open ground without obstacles, consciously fight while moving around the opponent's flank or rear. When circling at close range, do not run widely without purpose; circle tightly near the opponent while remaining within your primary attack range, and create only as much distance as needed when there is a concrete need for defense, evasion, or stabilization.
[A0313][CAUSE][FLAT_TERRAIN_COMBAT] Skills that cannot activate unless the enemy is inside the crosshair can have their activation frequency reduced when the target moves outside the opponent's crosshair by circling around them.
[A0314][CAUSE][FLAT_TERRAIN_COMBAT] If circling prevents the enemy from keeping the target in the crosshair and reduces the activation frequency of targeted offensive skills, an effective reduction in incoming attack frequency can be expected.
[A0315][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][FLAT_TERRAIN_COMBAT] Circling play does not need to prevent every enemy attack from being activated.
[A0316][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][FLAT_TERRAIN_COMBAT] Even reducing only part of the opponent's attack frequency through circling can make a large defensive contribution.
[A0317][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][FLAT_TERRAIN_COMBAT] Because the opponent also moves to circle toward your flank or rear, aiming skill remains important even in obstacle-free melee combat.
[A0318][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][FLAT_TERRAIN_COMBAT] To make circling on open ground practical, secure a certain level of movement speed that does not interfere with positioning.

## Escape

[A0319][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Do not equate “running away” in PvP with defeat.
[A0320][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Leaving a dangerous situation to heal, recover resources, refresh buffs, regroup with allies, or move to favorable terrain is a normal combat action.
[A0321][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ESCAPE_ROUTE] When targeted by multiple enemies, do not absorb every attack head-on in open ground; use distance, obstacles, and CC to spread out pursuers, and survive with ブロック（Block） and ロール回避（Roll Dodge）.

## Movement Speed and Combat Initiative

[A0322][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][LIMIT][COND][MOVEMENT_SPEED] The faster side tends to control when combat starts and ends, while the slower side may be unable to begin combat unless the opponent chooses to attack; this problem is especially pronounced for melee builds.
[A0323][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][CAUSE][MOVEMENT_SPEED] When one side has a one-sided advantage in both movement speed and attack range, the faster, longer-ranged side can disengage freely, rest and reset, and re-engage at timing favorable to itself more easily.
[A0324][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][RULE][COND][MOVEMENT_SPEED] Do not make excessive investment in movement speed an objective by itself. Reducing speed enough to impair positioning risks losing control over engagement and disengagement, and this risk is especially large for melee builds.

## 1vX

[A0325][CLAIM] 1vX can visually appear to mean “killing multiple opponents while taking all of their attacks at once.”
[A0326][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] The foundation of stable survival in 1vX is to avoid fighting multiple opponents head-on at the same time.
[A0327][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] In 1vX, use obstacles to break line of sight.
[A0328][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] In 1vX, create distance.
[A0329][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] In 1vX, use movement speed to spread out the order in which pursuers reach you.
[A0330][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] In 1vX, use CC to stop one opponent.
[A0331][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] In 1vX, isolate one opponent who overextends in pursuit.
[A0332][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] In 1vX, identify opponents who are already damaged.
[A0333][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] In 1vX, disengage again when the situation becomes dangerous.
[A0334][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE] Combine obstacles, distance, movement speed, CC, isolation, target selection, and repeated disengagement to convert one-against-many into a sequence of brief situations closer to one-against-one.
[A0335][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM] Being fully trapped by two equally capable opponents in a place with no escape route and receiving simultaneous attacks from both is an extremely difficult situation.
[A0336][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][CLAIM] Even against multiple opponents, the lower their PvP preparation and skill, the lower the difficulty can become even when taking simultaneous attacks in a place with no escape route.
[A0337][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT] Do not treat an example of defeating multiple underprepared or low-skill opponents head-on in a place with no escape route as evidence that you can similarly win when trapped head-on by two equally capable opponents.
[A0338][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE] Do not make video-style 1vX a minimum condition for PvP improvement.
[A0339][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM] A non-negligible share of PvP videos include examples of defeating overwhelmingly weaker opponents to create an appearance of strength for spectacle or view count.
[A0340][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][CLAIM][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Stage early 1vX practice goals in the order: survive 5 seconds longer even when targeted by multiple enemies → return to the position of your allies → bring only one pursuer with you. Achieving these is sufficient progress.

# Part 5 — Resources, Time, and Execution

## Emergency Resource Margin

[A0341][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES] Even on builds that are not スタミナ（Stamina）-based, treat Stamina as a survival resource used for 打破（Break Free）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, ブロック（Block）, and スプリント（Sprint）.
[A0342][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][DIAG] There are situations where you can survive at half HP if you still have enough スタミナ（Stamina） to 打破（Break Free）, while even at high HP you can die suddenly to CC into burst if Stamina is empty. Therefore, danger can begin not when a resource reaches zero, but when the margin reserved for safety actions has been exhausted.

## Sustain

[A0343][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][RULE][SUSTAIN] Sustain is the ability to maintain resources required to continue fighting. Evaluate it not only by recovery values, but by how many practical cycles of offense, defense, healing, 打破（Break Free）, movement, and renewed offense can be continued. Sustain itself is not a defensive layer that directly reduces incoming damage or directly restores HP; treat it as an indirect combat resource that makes those actions sustainable.
[A0344][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND] Even if resources are sufficient for attacking alone, practical sustain is insufficient if a single 打破（Break Free） from CC causes depletion.
[A0345][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND] Even when recovery values appear low, a build may fight for a long time by dividing use across multiple resources, combining offense and healing in one action, or securing time to recover resources in a safe area.

## Sustain Adjustment

[A0346][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][PRACTICE][SUSTAIN][BUILD_GENERAL] If the primary resource remains substantially unused across multiple representative real fights while another clear bottleneck such as killing power remains, suspect that marginal investment in sustain may be excessive. Change small elements such as recovery enchantments one at a time toward other performance and re-evaluate. Stop reducing sustain at the point where you can no longer return to offense after 打破（Break Free）, healing, or disengagement.

## Conditional Sustain and Deferred Effects

[A0347][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][CONDITIONAL_SUSTAIN] Treat conditional resource recovery as an additional effect that improves the economy of combat actions that were already tactically correct.
[A0348][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][CONDITIONAL_SUSTAIN] Be cautious with designs that cannot maintain the normal combat cycle without conditional resource recovery.
[A0349][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][GROUP_EFFECT][GROUP_CONTENT] In group PvP, do not depend on a sustain design that cannot maintain the normal combat cycle if resource supply from allies such as healers stops; treat external supply as an added effect that creates margin on top of sustain already established independently.
[A0350][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][OPPONENT_DEPENDENT_SUSTAIN] If continuously required resource demand on your side is supported only by resource supply that strongly depends on the opponent's class, attack type, or action choices, sustain performance becomes highly unstable across opponents.
[A0351][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][CONDITIONAL_SUSTAIN] Evaluate conditional resource recovery not only by its nominal amount but also by whether the timing when the effect occurs naturally aligns with the timing when the resource tends to become insufficient.
[A0352][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][CAUSE][RESOURCE_RECOVERY] The effective amount of an immediate lump-sum resource restore is capped by the current deficit; any amount exceeding the maximum cannot be saved for later combat, so do not always treat the nominal restore amount as the effective value.
[A0353][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][DEFERRED_EFFECT] Do not evaluate resources or アルティメット（Ultimate） gained only in a later stage of a combat cycle as resources that enabled the earlier stage of that same cycle.
[A0354][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][DEFERRED_EFFECT] Evaluate resources or アルティメット（Ultimate） gained after an execute or kill primarily as effects that accelerate preparation for the next attack wave or the next target.
[A0355][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][STAGE_DEPENDENT_PERFORMANCE] Evaluate performance that provides value only in a later combat stage on the premise that the build can reliably reach that stage.
[A0356][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][PRE_EXECUTE_PHASE] If you cannot reduce the opponent to an executable state with sufficient frequency, improve pressure, burst, CC, or similar tools that get the opponent into execute range before further strengthening execute performance.

## GCD and Time Budget

[A0357][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][CLAIM][TIME_BUDGET] GCD (Global Cooldown) is the common interval between successive skill uses; for ordinary GCD-bound skills, treat it as one second unless an individually verified exception applies. In PvP, not only skill slots but also the number of actions available within a short period is finite and valuable.
[A0358][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][TIME_BUDGET][PREMISE_VERIFICATION] When analyzing ESO PvP skill order and time budgets, do not assume without individually verified mechanical evidence that one player can use GCD (Global Cooldown)-bound skills consecutively at intervals shorter than one second, such as 0.3 or 0.5 seconds, that only one side of a fight can shorten the ordinary GCD, or that a player can use many ordinary GCD-bound skills within one second.
[A0359][CAUSE] If every role must be prepared through a separate action, the opponent can act multiple times during that preparation.
[A0360][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][TIME_BUDGET] When one skill can perform multiple jobs such as offense + healing within the same GCD, it is more time-efficient than performing those jobs with separate skills, and this value is not visible from individual numerical performance alone.
[A0361][EX] Offense + healing, healing + defensive buff, shield + another buff, and offense + debuff are examples of one action performing multiple jobs.
[A0362][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][TIME_BUDGET] Evaluate the time efficiency of a skill or effect not only by the total number of GCDs required, but also by when in the combat cycle those GCDs must be paid.

## Finite-Time Resources and Processing Capacity

[A0363][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][OPPORTUNITY_COST][TIME_BUDGET] When consuming a combat resource with an expiration time, simultaneous-hold cap, or generation rate requires GCDs, the resource can be generated faster than practical processing capacity. In that case, do not make consuming every resource the goal; intentionally discard low-marginal-value resources to preserve high-value GCDs for offense, defense, healing, positioning, and similar actions.

## Skill-Slot Compression

[A0364][LIMIT] The number of skill slots available in ESO is finite.
[A0365][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] In build design, consider what job each skill slot is responsible for.
[A0366][DEF] The idea of assigning multiple jobs to one skill slot is called “skill-slot compression.”
[A0367][EX] Configurations that handle healing + defensive buff, offense + healing, movement + CC, offense + debuff, or buff + emergency healing in a single slot are examples of skill-slot compression.
[A0368][CAUSE] Giving one slot multiple functions allows additional offensive or defensive capability to be placed in other freed slots.
[A0369][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][RULE][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION] Do not rate something as role compression, high efficiency, or high performance merely because multiple effects are listed. Verify whether each effect is needed, whether the desired timing of the effects is compatible, whether overlap with existing tools is small, and whether total efficiency is higher than strong alternatives for the same slot.
[A0370][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][SAME_ROLE_SLOTS] When using multiple skills or pieces of equipment that perform the same primary role within one build, do not justify adoption merely because “both are useful.” Verify that each covers different tactical situations, ranges, timings, targets, costs, or similar dimensions, and that the second tool provides more value than the opportunity cost of losing that slot.
[A0371][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][DIAG][SKILL_SLOT_COMPRESSION] If freeing one slot merely moves its job into a valuable flexible slot on another multifunction skill, it is not necessarily a net gain in compression. Check not only the number of freed slots but also how many powerful effects the receiving side could otherwise have selected but lost.
[A0372][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][PARTIAL_SUBSTITUTION] Even when equipment substitutes for part of a competing skill's function, it may not substitute for the other important effects that skill simultaneously provided; do not judge the entire competing skill unnecessary merely because of partial functional overlap.

## Usability

[A0373][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT] Even a numerically strong skill cannot deliver that strength in practice if you forget to use it.
[A0374][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND] Even if back-bar placement is theoretically more efficient, if putting a skill on the back bar causes you to forget it exists, front-bar placement can be stronger in practical combat.
[A0375][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY] In PvP, treat usability itself as build performance.
[A0376][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] In skill-layout design, evaluate ease of pressing, ease of remembering, whether it causes hesitation in emergencies, whether it can be used immediately after bar swapping, and compatibility with your own control habits.
[A0377][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND] If fully copying an advanced-player layout leaves you unable to use required skills when needed, a layout with slightly lower theoretical output but reliable execution can be stronger in practice.

## Complexity and Execution Cost

[A0378][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][CLASS_OPPORTUNITY_COST][BUILD_GENERAL] When comparing performance between classes or builds, subtract as execution cost not only their maximum-state numbers but also monitoring, positional adjustment, timing management, additional GCDs, special-resource management, and other tasks required beyond ordinary PvP actions to reach and maintain that state. If two configurations provide similar effective performance, the configuration that can produce it across broader conditions with less additional management has a practical advantage by that difference itself.

# Part 6 — Build Design and Evaluation

## Job-First Build Design

[A0379][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG] Starting build design from “I want to wear a strong set” tends to produce an incoherent whole.
[A0380][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY] In build design, first decide “what does this character do?”
[A0381][EX] Character jobs include front-line fighting, ranged pressure, supporting allies through high-speed movement, healing, interfering with enemy healing, drawing enemy attention, and assassinating isolated targets.
[A0382][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE] Once the character's role is decided, required performance becomes easier to identify.
[A0383][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND] If the character is responsible for front-line fighting, it needs offensive performance plus defense, healing, and sustain sufficient to continue that offense.
[A0384][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND] If the character is responsible for ranged combat, it gains safety from distance but needs countermeasures for when melee opponents close the gap and enough pressure from range that it cannot simply be ignored.
[A0385][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] If the character is responsible for healing, it needs sufficient healing output and enough self-defense not to die even when targeted first.
[A0386][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BUILD_SUBSYSTEM_EVALUATION] For a build composed of multiple elements, evaluate the core interaction, supporting elements, and the completed configuration separately; do not automatically expand the strength of one part into the strength of the completed whole.
[A0387][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][BUILD_SUBSYSTEM_EVALUATION] Even when a build includes meta-level equipment or interaction as its core, if added elements create large opportunity costs, activation restrictions, or missing role functions, the completed build can rate lower than the core itself.

## Front Bar / Back Bar and Equipment Topology

[A0388][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][LIMIT][PRACTICE] In a general model that is easy for beginners to understand, the front bar handles offense and pressure while the back bar handles healing, buffs, defense, and recovery; this is not an absolute rule.
[A0389][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE] Remaining on the offensive bar while the opponent is attacking strongly tends to be low-probability play, while continuing to prepare on the back bar while the opponent is occupied with healing misses an offensive turn. Determine whether the current time is for attacking or defending and connect that judgment to the roles of the bars.
[A0390][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][EQUIPMENT_SLOT] In PvP, even when the same two equipment slots are used, opportunity cost can differ greatly depending on whether those slots are front-bar weapons or back-bar weapons; do not judge set adoption cost from slot count alone.
[A0391][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][FRONT_BAR_WEAPON] Because front-bar weapons are often used to complete a five-piece effect intended to be active during offensive turns, an アリーナ武器（Arena Weapon） occupying the front-bar weapon slots should be evaluated not merely as two equipment pieces but as competition with the front-bar five-piece-set structure.
[A0392][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] The actual opportunity cost of changing equipment is determined not only by the number of replaced slots, but by the entire combination of five-piece sets, モンスターセット（Monster Set）, 神話装備（Mythic）, one-piece sets, and similar structures disrupted by that change.
[A0393][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] When evaluating a two-bar equipment configuration, separately verify which 2- through 5-piece bonuses are actually active on the front and back bars. Do not count equipment bonuses inactive during offensive turns toward offense, or bonuses inactive during defensive turns toward defense.
[A0394][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][TRIGGERED_EQUIPMENT] When evaluating an equipment effect, distinguish conditions required to trigger the effect from conditions required to maintain the effect after activation.
[A0395][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][RAMP_UP_MAINTENANCE] For stacking or combat-duration effects, evaluate initial ramp-up on first engagement, maintenance during engagement, and rebuilding after full disengagement separately. If the effect can be maintained through normal combat actions, do not equate slow initial ramp-up with difficulty maintaining it.
[A0396][ADVANTAGE][BACK_BAR_EQUIPMENT] Equipment whose effect persists after activation even when the five-piece condition is no longer met can complete the five-piece set on only one weapon bar and carry some or all of the effect over after switching to the opposite bar.
[A0397][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] Even under the same nominal five-piece structure, do not treat a static five-piece effect that disappears when leaving the bar as topologically equal in value to a triggered effect that persists for a period after leaving the activation bar.

## Time-Investment Performance and Long Fights

[A0398][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL][TIME_BUDGET] For configurations that pay an action now and recover its value several seconds later through delayed attacks, position-dependent resources, stacking effects, reuse through follow-up actions, or similar mechanics, evaluate not only maximum-state performance but how often the same local fight can be maintained until that investment is recovered. If disengagement, target switching, or battlefield movement repeatedly resets combat before recovery, effective performance can fall sharply because only the initial investment is repeatedly paid.
[A0399][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][RAMP_UP_MAINTENANCE][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Do not equate merely being able to survive a long fight with being suited to long fights. If extending combat time also grows enemy DoTs, stacking effects, アルティメット（Ultimate）, resource loops, or similar advantages, compare both sides' time-dependent performance. Even when your win condition requires time, prolonging combat is not automatically beneficial if that same time strengthens the enemy more than it strengthens you.

## Stat Scaling and Shared Resources

[A0400][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] When comparing different stats such as Max Resources and Weapon/Spell Damage that contribute to the same skill's base scaling, do not treat them as equal merely by a base conversion ratio; compare their final contribution in the practical state after each passes through applicable percentage buffs, passives, equipment effects, and similar modifiers.
[A0401][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][OPPORTUNITY_COST][BUILD_GENERAL][TIME_BUDGET] When an offensive combo and defensive actions share the same stacks, special resource, or similar value, spending it on defense carries not only the defensive skill's GCD and ordinary resource cost, but also the time required to rebuild that special resource before returning to the next offensive combo; evaluate this rebuilding time as opportunity cost.

## Marginal Evaluation of Skill-Enhancing Equipment

[A0402][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][MARGINAL_EFFECT_EVALUATION][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Evaluate equipment that enhances a skill relative to using the same skill without that equipment. Do not double-count performance already provided by the base skill; evaluate only the additional work actually created by the equipment.
[A0403][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][PRIORITY][NATURAL_ACTION_TEST] Equipment triggered by actions you would tactically choose even without the effect is easier to justify. If activating the effect requires extra GCDs, resources, bar swaps, positioning, or similar actions solely for the proc, subtract them as explicit costs.
[A0404][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][SLOT_VALUE_VS_CAST_VALUE] A skill can be strong merely by being slotted without also having high value when actively cast, so distinguish the two when evaluating equipment that requires active casting.
[A0405][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][CAUSE][EFFECT_COVERAGE] If an effect applies only to some attacks, heals, or actions, do not treat it as performance across the entire build; consider the share of practical actions covered. Even a large effect on covered actions can make only a small effective contribution to the whole when coverage is low.
[A0406][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][JOINT_EFFECT_COVERAGE] When value depends on multiple effects being active simultaneously, do not estimate combined performance from each effect's individual utilization rate alone; evaluate how often and for what time windows they are simultaneously active when needed.
[A0407][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][TARGET_TIMING_CONTROL] When evaluating offensive enhancement, debuffs, or support effects, verify whether you can choose the required target and activation timing yourself. Effects whose target or timing depends heavily on opponent actions or accidental hits tend to have lower tactical value even with the same nominal performance.
[A0408][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][TARGET_LOCAL_EFFECT] For effects whose value rises or falls based on a particular enemy's HP, state, taking damage, or similar target-local conditions, verify whether the same value can be carried over when switching targets; if not, include effective-value loss from target switching in multi-target combat.

## Simultaneous Alignment of Multiple Position References

[A0409][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL][TARGET_TIMING_CONTROL] In configurations whose offensive performance is distributed across multiple different positional references such as the target, yourself, a ground resource, or a moving delayed attack, evaluate the positional-adjustment cost required to align them simultaneously during the required window. Compared with configurations centered on effects applied to and following one target, multiple components are more likely to collapse in sequence during mobile combat.

## Demand Timing of Conditional Effects

[A0410][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][TARGET_TIMING_CONTROL] Evaluate conditional effects not only by proc rate, but by whether the situations in which the condition is met naturally align with the situations in which the effect is strongly needed.

## Interactions Between Build Elements

[A0411][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL][SUSTAIN][OPPORTUNITY_COST] When rating a combination of multiple skills, equipment effects, and combat mechanics as high performance, do not simply add each element in isolation. Verify whether they can coexist as one practical combat cycle, including competition for shared resources, order of use, maintenance duration, reuse conditions, GCDs, and conditions under which one effect removes or interferes with another.
[A0412][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] When combining effects that require specific states such as invisibility, stealth, defensive state, or movement state with other automatic, periodic, offensive, or movement effects, verify that activation of one does not remove, interfere with, or disable reuse of the other's state before evaluating them as synergy.

## Opportunity Cost and Alternative Comparison

[A0413][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][LIMIT][RULE][DEF][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Choice slots such as equipment slots, ムンダス（Mundus）, traits, and CP are finite, so “it has a useful effect” is not sufficient reason for adoption. Evaluate skills, equipment, and passives by their difference from the strongest realistic alternatives competing for the same slot, and include losing the opportunity to use a stronger choice among the primary disadvantages of a weaker option.
[A0414][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][DIAG][RULE][BUILD_EVALUATION_DISCIPLINE] Evaluate build elements not by the number of tooltip effects or standalone performance, but by the slot's job and necessity, practical utilization rate, overlap with existing tools, activation cost, low-cost alternatives, what must be given up to adopt it, and marginal value relative to the strongest alternative for the same slot.
[A0415][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In groups, assigning different useful effects across multiple members can enable build design with higher slot efficiency than solo play.
[A0416][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][GROUP_EFFECT][GROUP_CONTENT] For effects whose conditions require ally positioning or cooperation, evaluate effective value including how often allies actually satisfy those conditions, not only the theoretical maximum effect.

## Identifying Adoption Reasons Through Comparison

[A0417][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] When explaining why an option should be adopted, do not treat a property shared by many realistic competitors as a sufficient reason unique to that option.
[A0418][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] A useful adoption reason identifies the required job and shows a relevant difference by which that option performs the job substantially better, more reliably, or at lower real cost than realistic competitors.
[A0419][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] If replacing the recommended option's name with several realistic competitors leaves the explanation almost unchanged, the explanation likely fails to identify the actual reason for selection sufficiently.

## General Combat Actions and Unique Value

[A0420][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Do not award ブロック（Block）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, 打破（Break Free）, LoS, bar swapping, or other general combat actions broadly available to compared builds as unique performance of a specific build. Evaluate as unique value only the incremental mitigation, healing, input tolerance, resource efficiency, or other differences that equipment, skills, class traits, or similar elements add when performing the same general action.

## Viability and Recommendation Scope

[A0421][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][VIABILITY_VS_RECOMMENDATION][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Treat “functions under specific conditions,” “is competitive for that use case,” and “deserves general standard recommendation” as separate evaluation stages. Do not infer general-use competitiveness or superiority over standard alternatives from examples that function only under limited conditions.
[A0422][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][PREFERENCE_VS_PERFORMANCE] Personal preference can be a reason to adopt an option that is less performance-efficient than strong alternatives for the same objective, but that preference does not change the comparative performance evaluation itself.
[A0423][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][CLASS_OPPORTUNITY_COST] When recommending a class-specific element for PvP, evaluate opportunity cost not only against competing options within the same class but also against the performance difference from choosing another class that can realistically perform the same role.
[A0424][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BEGINNER_CLASS_OPPORTUNITY_COST] Do not omit inter-class opportunity cost even for beginner recommendations; compare after adding the burden of proficiency, leveling, and equipment preparation required to switch to another class as a cost.
[A0425][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][CLASS_OPPORTUNITY_COST][BUILD_GENERAL] In an environment where class-derived skill lines or abilities can be transplanted into another base class, evaluate separately the original class's own suitability, the suitability of the transplantable skill line or ability itself, and the suitability of the completed configuration. When another configuration adopts that ability and gains high performance, this supports the value of the ability itself while weakening the basis for counting it as an exclusive advantage of the original class.

## Opportunity Cost of Transformation Form and Normal Form

[A0426][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][RULE][CLASS_OPPORTUNITY_COST][NATIVE_CLASS_SUITABILITY] When ウェアウルフ（Werewolf） form replaces the original class's active bars, do not infer that class's Werewolf suitability from the class's overall humanoid-form performance. Separately evaluate passives, class mastery, base modifiers, and similar benefits that remain active during transformation and the opportunity cost of major class assets that become unavailable because of the transformation.
[A0427][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ALTERNATIVE][OPPORTUNITY_COST] When evaluating a transformation form with a skill set, control system, or combat style different from the normal form, evaluate not only maximum performance while transformed but also the value of switching to a different win condition against combat conditions that are difficult for the normal form to handle. The more strongly the normal form depends on specific positioning, timing, preparation, complex resource loops, or similar conditions, the more a transformation form with less dependence on those conditions can have value not as a simple performance increase but as a tactical escape route around weaknesses.

## Counter Equipment and Opponent-Dependent Effects

[A0428][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][COUNTER_GEAR_EXPECTED_VALUE] Evaluate equipment that is especially strong against a specific attack type or class not only by its maximum effect against the ideal opponent, but approximately by “encounter rate of eligible opponents in the environment × effective proc frequency during a fight × value per proc.”
[A0429][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][AI_META_EVALUATION] When using counter equipment routinely, check not only the opponent it counters most strongly but also how dead the equipment becomes against high-frequency, high-threat opponents that should be prioritized in the current environment.

## Specialized Configurations and Target Selection

[A0430][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING][OPPORTUNITY_COST] A configuration that sacrifices general performance to specialize against a particular opponent or situation is not justified merely because it is effective against that target. Evaluate whether it creates a larger difference than strong realistic general-purpose alternatives in kill probability, time-to-process, GCD or resource pressure, battlefield advantage, or similar outcomes, and whether that difference exceeds the opportunity cost of lost general performance.
[A0431][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][OPPORTUNITY_COST] Do not select an attack target only because “my configuration works well against that opponent.” Also evaluate the time required to process that opponent, the freedom of action granted to other enemies during that time, and the battlefield value gained by killing or removing the target. Even when matchup characteristics make it easy to deal damage, a long investment into a target that takes too long to process and leaves other high-threat enemies free can be a losing trade.
[A0432][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][OPPONENT_FUELED_OFFENSE] Builds whose offense is fueled by the opponent's attacks, such as reflection or on-hit effects, tend to give the opponent denial options such as “do not attack,” “change the attack method,” “create distance,” or “target someone else.”
[A0433][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][COUNTERPLAY_COST] When the opponent can deny, avoid, or nullify an effect, do not evaluate it only by the fact that counterplay exists; include the real cost the opponent pays to counter it, such as lost attack opportunities, position, resources, range, or target choice.
[A0434][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][COUNTERPLAY_COST] If executing the countermeasure restricts actions that would otherwise be strong for the opponent, include the fact that the restriction was forced as practical combat value.
[A0435][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][COUNTER_LAYER_COVERAGE] Even for an effect that greatly invalidates a specific defensive layer, if the opponent's survivability is distributed across resistance, percentage mitigation, shields, healing, CC, mobility, LoS, and similar layers, verify the range of defensive layers the effect can actually influence and do not generalize it into “an answer to durable opponents in general.”
[A0436][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][OFFENSIVE_USE_OF_DEFENSIVE_STAT] Investing heavily in a stat with inherently high defensive value such as Max Health and making that same stat the primary basis of offense are separate decisions.
[A0437][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][OFFENSIVE_USE_OF_DEFENSIVE_STAT] Even when a stat is broadly useful as a defensive investment, converting it into offense through reflection or similar mechanics can greatly increase dependence of offensive performance on the opponent.

## Bottlenecks

[A0438][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND] Even with extremely high offensive power, if insufficient durability forces the build into a defensive turn before it can begin attacking, that damage is difficult to use.
[A0439][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT] Even a configuration with high offense and low durability can have use cases, such as assassination specialization, where low durability is accepted for the intended purpose.
[A0440][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND] Even with extremely strong healing, if you cannot damage the opponent at all and can do nothing but continue healing, that may not lead to a win.
[A0441][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][DIAG] A bottleneck is the narrowest part stopping the practical performance of the build as a whole; candidates include damage, healing, スタミナ（Stamina）, mobility, CC, execution load, and recovery after defense.
[A0442][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE] When adjusting a build, ask “what is stopping practical performance the most?” rather than “what can I add more of?”

## Weaknesses and Tradeoffs

[A0443][CLAIM] Players tend to want high damage, durability, healing, speed, range, melee performance, resources, and ease of operation all at once.
[A0444][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT] It is normally impossible to maximize damage, durability, healing, speed, range, melee performance, resources, and ease of operation all at once; compromises are required somewhere.
[A0445][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][OPPORTUNITY_COST][BUILD_GENERAL] When allocating additional performance to functions outside pure frontal offense and defense, such as range, mobility, ally healing, or debuffs, evaluate as opportunity cost the damage, durability, healing, skill slots, GCDs, resources, and other performance lost to obtain those functions.
[A0446][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][OPPORTUNITY_COST][RANGE_COST][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] When comparing a melee-focused and a ranged-focused build that serve the same primary purpose and the ranged side actually pays performance budget to obtain range, begin from the premise that the ranged-focused side is disadvantaged in pure frontal offense and defense.
[A0447][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][EVAL][OPPORTUNITY_COST][RANGE_COST][RANGED_INTERVENTION] The opportunity cost paid for range is recovered only to the extent that the range is actually converted into value such as earlier attack initiation, intervention frequency, continued participation from safer positions, continued support, time and positional costs imposed on the enemy to approach, or target access.
[A0448][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][RANGE_COST][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Evaluate separately the ability to make ranged attacks and the ability to maintain or recreate distance after contact or disengage from combat; do not infer kiting or disengagement capability from range alone.
[A0449][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][MOVEMENT_SPEED][ESCAPE_ROUTE][OPPORTUNITY_COST] Melee builds can also allocate performance budget to movement speed, pursuit, disengagement, and handling slows or immobilization; therefore, do not treat kiting, disengagement, or distance play as privileges exclusive to ranged builds.
[A0450][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL][RANGE_COST][MOVEMENT_SPEED][ESCAPE_ROUTE] When evaluating a build's distance-related performance, examine range, movement speed, pursuit capability, disengagement capability, ability to maintain or recreate distance, and frontal offense/defense as separate performance axes.
[A0451][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][CAUSE][RANGE_COST][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] In open-ground combat without a frontline or effective obstacles, time for maintaining distance is less likely to be supplied externally by allies or terrain; therefore, in comparisons between ranged-focused and melee-focused builds serving the same primary purpose, the melee-focused side's advantage in frontal offense and defense tends to become more exposed.
[A0452][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][RANGE_COST][GROUP_CONTENT] An allied frontline and terrain or LoS can be evaluated as factors that externally supply distance and time to the ranged side. On battlefields where that external support is lost, the effective benefit the same ranged build obtains from range may decrease.
[A0453][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][RANGE_COST][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Do not automatically infer mobility, distance-maintenance capability, kiting capability, or disengagement capability from the fact that a build has ranged attack capability.
[A0454][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][MOVEMENT_SPEED][ESCAPE_ROUTE][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Allow the melee side to invest performance into pursuit, disengagement, and distance management; do not generate comparisons that grant the ranged side both range and mobility/disengagement for free.
[A0455][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] Strong builds identify “weaknesses acceptable for the intended use” rather than aiming for “zero weaknesses.”
[A0456][AUTHOR_VIEW][EX] Examples of tradeoffs include a configuration with long range but less performance available for frontal exchanges, a configuration with high mobility and strong disengagement but less performance available for frontal offense and defense, a configuration strong in frontal combat but only moderate in pursuit, and a configuration strong in healing support but slow at securing solo kills. Do not equate range itself with ease of escape.
[A0457][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE] Understanding tradeoffs makes it less likely that simply entering an unfavorable situation will be misdiagnosed as the entire build being broken.
[A0458][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][SURPLUS_PERFORMANCE] When a performance dimension already substantially exceeds the level required for the use case, losing part of that performance can cause less practical harm than losing the same amount from a dimension that is already insufficient.
[A0459][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][TRADEOFF] When equipment or an effect reduces a strength of a class or build, do not immediately reject it because “it cancels the strength.” Compare whether the use case's required level is still met after the disadvantage is applied and what performance is gained in exchange.
[A0460][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][TRADEOFF] Do not treat a clear weakness that can be avoided or supplemented by changing the use case symmetrically with a clear strength that remains present across use cases. In use cases where the weakness can be accepted, a configuration with a sharp strength can be more competitive than a configuration that is average in every category but lacks a strength directly connected to its win condition.

## Initial Builds and Preparation for Entry-Level Beginners

[A0461][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL] For entry-level beginners, before wins or maximum damage, use the ability to survive for a period on the battlefield and become familiar with the feel of PvP and basic controls as the main evaluation axis of initial builds and learning.
[A0462][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL] As a rule, verify that builds for entry-level beginners function in the order of ① survivability, ② practical sustain, ③ damage and other performance.
[A0463][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][PRACTICE] Unless the entry-level beginner themselves wants immediate participation, do not make “just join first even if you die repeatedly” the standard advice; first present a build that can absorb beginner mistakes more easily and minimum operational preparation.
[A0464][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER] An entry-level beginner unfamiliar with both PvP and two-bar PvE operation should equip the PvP build and, in an open-world area or similar environment, execute the skills on each bar, healing, defense, 打破（Break Free）, equipment effects, and similar mechanics at least once before entering real PvP as initial preparation.
[A0465][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][PRACTICE][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not use simple operating steps or the fact that survival is possible with ideal judgment and positioning alone as evidence that a build or tactic is suitable for entry-level beginners. Evaluate survivability, practical sustain, tolerance for judgment and execution mistakes, and the margin to learn PvP fundamentals such as LoS, defensive transitions, CC, and switching between offense and defense during actual combat.
[A0466][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][PRACTICE] For entry-level beginners, prioritize methods that reduce danger while allowing continued combat participation and experience with offense, defense, LoS, regrouping with allies, and similar fundamentals over methods that secure survival by persistently avoiding meaningful combat participation.

## Sustain Adjustment for Entry-Level Beginners

[A0467][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL] For entry-level beginner builds based mainly on 重装鎧（Heavy Armor）, verify the practical sustain lost by relying heavily on Heavy Armor and, if necessary, compensate through monster masks, 神話装備（Mythic）, ムンダス（Mundus）, food, or similar sources. In the initial stage, prioritize recovery and resource maintenance over small increases in damage.
[A0468][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL] For sustain adjustment of entry-level beginners, as a rule consider ムンダス（Mundus） → food → equipment → jewelry enchantments in that order. If collection constraints prevent use of monster masks, 神話装備（Mythic）, or similar equipment, the equipment-adjustment stage may be skipped and jewelry enchantments considered next.
[A0469][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][BUILD_GENERAL] When adjusting sustain through jewelry enchantments, do not change multiple pieces at once. As a rule, change one piece at a time, test it in practical combat for a meaningful period, and add further adjustment while confirming the required amount.

## Beginner Build Copying and Evaluation

[A0470][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] When a PvP beginner is comfortable using another person's design, recommend using a completed beginner-oriented build that secures survivability and execution tolerance as the baseline for an entry-level beginner, and a completed experienced-player build suited to the intended use as the baseline for a more proficient beginner.
[A0471][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRACTICE][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] When using a completed build as a baseline, reproduce skills, food, potions, traits, equipment slots, and armor weights as faithfully as practical, experience the original build's intent first, and only then move to custom modifications.
[A0472][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][PRACTICE][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][BUILD_GENERAL] Do not treat inability to reproduce a completed build perfectly as making practice with that build impossible.
[A0473][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] Builds have intended use cases, so read the usage explanation and verify that your own use case matches the intended one.
[A0474][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][BUILD_GENERAL] Do not take exaggerated wording by a build author or promotional examples showing only favorable video clips at face value as evidence of the build's general performance.
[A0475][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][DIAG][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] Give weaknesses high priority in build descriptions; if they are not shown, consider them yourself, and do not use the build in use cases where known weaknesses become relevant.
[A0476][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] When using a build from a past patch, verify how well it functions in the current environment.

## Preference-Prioritized Choices and Performance Efficiency

[A0477][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][PROC][PRIORITY][PREFERENCE_BUILD] When adopting an element with lower performance efficiency than strong alternatives for the same objective because of personal preference, use a completed build of the same general type with sufficient performance efficiency or another high-efficiency candidate as the comparison baseline. As the standard workflow, introduce preference elements one at a time and verify viability and impact at each stage; when proposing concrete configuration changes, use this procedure as the first candidate.

## Food-Buff Substitution and Cost Effectiveness for Beginners

[A0478][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][FOOD] Even while a beginner is at the stage of faithfully copying a completed build, if the specified food has extremely poor cost effectiveness, it may exceptionally be substituted with a lower-cost food serving a similar role.
[A0479][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][CLAIM][SUBSTITUTE][FOOD] オーゾーガの熊の腰肉の燻製（Orzorga's Smoked Bear Haunch） can be substituted with 失政の宝石（Jewels of Misrule）.

[A0481][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][COST][FOOD] オーゾーガの熊の腰肉の燻製（Orzorga's Smoked Bear Haunch） and クロックワークシトラスフィレ（Clockwork Citrus Filet） use 完ぺきな魚卵（Perfect Roe） as an ingredient, making their cost effectiveness poor for routine beginner practice.
[A0482][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][FOOD] During beginner practice, a low-cost substitute food serving a role close to the specified food is sufficient.
[A0483][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][FOOD] In beginner practice, explore optimization of combat actions before fine-tuning differences in food performance.
[A0484][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][ADVANTAGE][FOOD] 魔法のシュガースカル（Bewitched Sugar Skulls） has high cost effectiveness in its ingredients and a strong food effect, making it easy for beginners to use.
[A0485][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][PRACTICE][FOOD] If a build a beginner wants to use specifies 魔法のシュガースカル（Bewitched Sugar Skulls）, consider using it as specified.

# Part 7 — Group Combat and Roles

## Roles

[A0486][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][CLAIM] PvP playstyles can be organized broadly into role tendencies such as tank, healer / buffer / debuffer, bruiser, skirmisher, ganker / bomber, and similar categories, and hybrid operation combining multiple tendencies is also common. These categories are not intended to classify every player exclusively into only one role.
[A0487][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When evaluating effective contribution to the group in group content, focus more on “what problem do I solve for the team?” than on the role name itself.
[A0488][DEF][SCOPE] DD（Damage Dealer） is a broad term for configurations or roles whose main function is dealing damage to enemies and does not correspond one-to-one with the PvP role categories distinguished in ESO PvP Corpus, such as bruiser, skirmisher, or ganker / bomber.
[A0489][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] A tank's value is not merely in refusing to die, but in making enemies feel they cannot ignore the tank, absorbing enemy numbers and attacks, and thereby creating local numerical advantage for allies.
[A0490][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_SUPPORT][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] The job of a healer / support is not only HP restoration, but also buffs, debuffs, CC, rescue, and pressure on the enemy healer.
[A0491][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_BRUISER] A bruiser continuously pressures opponents in front-line combat and therefore requires enough survivability to keep attacking.
[A0492][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_SKIRMISHER] A skirmisher is a mobile role tendency that uses high mobility to flexibly change combat locations and engagement states. Whether it operates solo or with a group, focuses on close or long range, and uses mobility for self-survival, offense, or ally support depends on the participating content and operational objective; being a ranged-focused DD is not itself a general requirement for a skirmisher.
[A0493][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When operating a skirmisher in group content with priority on effective group contribution, use mobility not merely as freedom to move wherever desired but as the ability to reach high-intervention-value locations quickly.
[A0494][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Evaluate the intervention performance of a group-contribution skirmisher not only by single instances of fast movement or sporadic hit-and-run attacks, but by the time required after moving to the needed battlefield to begin effective offense, CC, rescue, or pressure and by how frequently effective intervention can be repeated across multiple combat locations.
[A0495][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][ROLE_SKIRMISHER][ROLE_BRUISER][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not automatically conclude that an operation is not skirmisher play or must be bruiser play merely because it does not satisfy the conditions of a group-contribution skirmisher or the advanced competitive skirmisher conditions in Series B. Evaluate role tendency individually from the combat actions actually performed and the operational objective.
[A0496][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_GANKER] Gank means choosing the target, position, attack-start timing, or similar conditions and seeking a kill from a short offensive opportunity rather than maintaining a long ordinary front-line exchange. Stealth, invisibility, and melee attacks are not necessary conditions for a gank.
[A0497][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][STEALTH_MOBILITY_SEPARATION] Evaluate stealth and mobility separately: stealth hides information about position and intent from enemies, while mobility changes actual position rapidly. Do not equate high stealth with high mobility.
[A0498][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Evaluate suitability for a specific role by performance directly related to that role's objective, required actions, and success conditions. Do not reuse evaluations of solo general performance, another role, ganker performance, or similar categories as grounds for ranking suitability in the role unless that performance directly contributes to the role's requirements.

## Hybridization into Adjacent Roles

[A0499][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][BUILD_GENERAL] When a class or build is disadvantaged in direct competition for its primary role, do not make excessive investment to average out weaknesses the only improvement path; consider hybridizing into an adjacent role where competitiveness can be gained while preserving existing strengths.

## Creating and Converting Advantage in Group Combat

[A0500][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ADVANTAGE_CREATION_CONVERSION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In group PvP, evaluate separately the ability to lead allies into an advantageous state and the ability to maintain, expand, or convert an advantageous state already created by allies into results such as kills.
[A0501][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][ALLY_DEPENDENCE][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Showing high performance when allies have supplied pressure, CC, damage, or similar advantage does not mean the character can create the same advantageous state alone.
[A0502][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][SOLO_EVALUATION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Do not conclude that a build has low group-combat value merely because it has low ability to create advantage by itself if it can convert advantage created by allies into results with high efficiency.

## Spillover Value Beyond the Direct Matchup

[A0503][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][GROUP_EFFECT][TARGET_LOCAL_EFFECT][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Even when two players in group combat appear to be mutually locking each other down, if one can continue providing value to other allies or enemies through HoTs, area healing, buffs, debuffs, AoE, CC, or similar effects, do not treat them as a simple one-for-one cancellation; include the difference in value spilling outside the direct matchup while they are occupied.

## Aggro Value

[A0504][CLAIM][GROUP_CONTENT] Enemy offensive actions are finite in group combat.
[A0505][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] If you can survive while drawing enemy attention, that action itself can have team value.
[A0506][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][LIMIT][COMBAT_PARTICIPATION_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] As a future goal, beginners should become able to evaluate operation that reduces meaningful combat participation through stealth, waiting, or similar behavior in fixed-size group combat by subtracting the opportunity cost of offense, CC, support, focus distribution, and other contributions lost during that time and determining whether later intervention exceeds those losses; do not make this an initial-stage requirement.

## Escape That Draws Enemies and Return to the Front

[A0507][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In Battlegrounds (BG), merely not dying can be insufficient.
[A0508][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] The problem that “merely not dying is insufficient” is especially pronounced in highly competitive 4v4.
[A0509][EX][GROUP_CONTENT][ESCAPE_ROUTE] An example is a situation where two enemies chase you far away from the main battlefield.
[A0510][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][CLAIM][CAUSE][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][ESCAPE_ROUTE] While two enemies continue chasing you, those two cannot attack your allies, giving your side a temporary numerical advantage on the main battlefield. This numerical advantage has tactical value.
[A0511][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][LIMIT][CAUSE][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][ESCAPE_ROUTE] If the enemies stop chasing and return to the main battlefield while you alone remain far away, your allies must fight without you and the value gained by drawing the enemies away diminishes.
[A0512][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ESCAPE_ROUTE] Distinguish “escaping from attacks” from “disappearing from the battlefield.”
[A0513][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][ESCAPE_ROUTE] An ideal escape-and-return sequence is: evade the opponent's attacks → return near allies or a healer → if possible bring pursuers back toward your allies as well → stabilize and re-enter combat.
[A0514][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ESCAPE_ROUTE] The final destination of escape is not “the farthest safe place” but “a place where you can fight next.”

## Permanent Safe-Zone Positioning and Combat Participation for Entry-Level Beginners

[A0515][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][LIMIT][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In fixed-capacity small-group content, when an entry-level beginner personally prioritizes team victory and effective contribution, do not make it a standard recommendation to remain in positions difficult for enemies to attack, such as high ground near the spawn side, and continue only ranged attacks while persistently avoiding participation in normal combat.
[A0516][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][ENTRY_LEVEL_BEGINNER][PRACTICE] Relying only on ranged attacks from positions difficult for enemies to attack reduces opportunities to repeat defensive transitions under enemy pressure, LoS, resource management, CC, positional adjustment with allies, and similar fundamentals in real combat, making it limited as a basic learning method for entry-level beginners.

## Evaluating Kill Count

[A0517][CLAIM] Kill count is visually prominent on PvP result screens.
[A0518][CAUSE] Kill count depends on the final hit, allied composition, opponent skill, rules, match population, your role, focus target, and battlefield position.
[A0519][EX][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Contributions such as protecting a healer, occupying multiple enemies for a long period, breaking enemy healing, or rescuing a nearly dead ally are not fully reflected in kill count.
[A0520][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] In self-evaluation, review not only kill count but whether you fulfilled your role.
[A0521][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE] Reviewing role fulfillment in self-evaluation contributes more to improvement than looking only at kill count.
[A0522][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][LIMIT][KILL_CONTRIBUTION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] As a future goal, beginners should learn to evaluate contribution to a kill not only by whether they received the Killing Blow, but by comparing whether the opponent could have survived, healed, or disengaged without their intervention and by not counting the final hit on an opponent who would have been killed immediately even without them as major additional value; do not make this an initial-stage requirement.

## Tank Fundamentals

[A0523][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_TANK] In tank design, establish the foundation for sustained survival under the intended enemy pressure before increasing added value.
[A0524][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When a tank survives while continuously absorbing meaningful enemy attacks and attention and thereby reduces the offensive or defensive burden on allies, evaluate continuing to survive without ending the defensive turn itself as tank contribution.

## Branches in Tank Design and Operation

[A0525][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_TANK][BLOCK] When evaluating or designing a tank, distinguish a permanent-block type whose primary defense is continuous ブロック（Block） maintenance from an action/mobility type whose main survival tools include movement and actions outside block; do not apply identical defensive, sustain, LoS, and execution principles uniformly to both.
[A0526][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][SCOPE][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Performance required of a PvP tank changes with participant limits, combat scale, mobility, victory conditions, and how easily enemies can ignore the tank. Do not generalize tank requirements from fixed-size advanced small-team combat uniformly to different battlefields such as シロディール（Cyrodiil）.
[A0527][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_TANK][TIME_BUDGET] A tank does not need to consume GCDs constantly. When additional actions have no value, doing nothing and simply maintaining the required defensive state or ブロック（Block） is appropriate operation.
[A0528][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_TANK][ROLE_BRUISER] Distinguish a high-durability bruiser from a dedicated tank not merely by durability values, but by whether the primary purpose after securing survival is to return performance budget into personal offensive pressure or to convert it into support such as absorbing focus, protecting allies, buffs, debuffs, CC, and disruption.
[A0529][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_TANK] Even when a dedicated tank contributes offensively, prioritize not breaking required defense, ally protection, or support through those offensive actions.
[A0530][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_TANK][BLOCK][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] If a dedicated tank releases ブロック（Block） required for survival to add low-value attacks and thereby increases death risk or loss of its primary job, the tank's total contribution can fall even when the attack adds positive DPS. Actions usable while blocking should be prioritized for execution while maintaining the required block.
[A0531][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][SCOPE][OVERRIDE][ROLE_TANK][BEGINNER_TANK][BLOCK] For tanks whose primary defense is continuous ブロック（Block） maintenance, do not make LoS a standard primary defense requirement, especially for beginners; first establish block maintenance, healing, and sustain. For tanks that include movement outside block as a primary defense, treat obstacles and LoS as active survival tools.
[A0532][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_TANK][BLOCK][BLOCK_COST][SUSTAIN] For permanent ブロック（Block）-type Tanks, treat Block cost as a primary viability condition in addition to Block mitigation. If the Tank cannot maintain Block for the required duration, do not rate long-duration durability as established even if raw toughness is high.

## Tank Beginner Viability Order and Success Evaluation

[A0533][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][ROLE_TANK][BEGINNER_TANK][SUSTAIN] For beginner tanks, first secure minimum durability sufficient to survive initial pressure, then prepare minimum sustain support that allows continued defense and self-healing before the first real match, obtain enough survival time, and only then learn positioning, ブロック（Block） judgment, and additional support progressively as needed.
[A0534][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][GOAL][ROLE_TANK][BEGINNER_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] If an entry-stage tank can survive for a period while receiving continuous attacks from multiple enemies, evaluate that survival itself as success. Treat additional support such as CC, debuffs, or pulls as candidates for progressive learning according to content requirements and the player's execution margin, not as mandatory obligations immediately after survival becomes possible.
[A0535][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_TANK][BEGINNER_TANK][BLOCK][HEAVY_ARMOR][OPPORTUNITY_COST][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] For beginner permanent-block tanks, first establish performance of the primary defense, ブロック（Block）, and do not standardly recommend increasing the share of 中装鎧（Medium Armor） or changing armor traits solely to reduce ロール回避（Roll Dodge） cost when doing so substantially lowers block durability. Evaluate armor changes for other clear objectives separately.

## Tank Self-Healing

[A0536][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_TANK][HEALING][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] As a basic design principle, a high-Max-Health PvP tank should secure at least one burst self-heal scaling from Max Health that can independently rewind a large amount of its own lost HP.
[A0537][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_TANK][HEALING][TIME_BUDGET][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] In PvP tanks, do not rely only on burst self-healing; combine it with HoTs that continuously offset ordinary incoming damage. Repeated burst healing consumes not only resources but GCDs, movement, Heavy Attacks, and other action time, so HoTs reduce the burden of recovery.

## Tank Pulls

[A0538][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Evaluate tank pulls separately for two uses: gathering multiple enemies into a narrow area to make allied AoE attacks and CC easier to land, and moving an enemy far from its original position to disrupt line of sight, distance, formation, or positioning.

## Tank Resistance

[A0539][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][RULE][ROLE_TANK][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Do not judge PvP tank resistance excessive merely because the displayed value exceeds the point where ordinary resistance mitigation reaches its cap; evaluate based on effective resistance after enemy Penetration and resistance reduction. ## Shield Wall Line

[A0540][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][EVAL][ROLE_TANK][BLOCK][SUSTAIN][OPPORTUNITY_COST] Because the Shield Wall line automates and removes the cost of maintaining ブロック（Block） for a period, freeing the player from manual blocking, evaluate it for permanent-block tanks not only by survival time but also by the value of using that period to rebuild resources through Heavy Attacks or similar actions. [A0555][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][EVAL][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][OPPORTUNITY_COST] The 庇護（Guard） line can be evaluated as a strong support tool by which a high-durability player absorbs part of an ally's incoming damage. # Part 8 — Learning, Verification, and Practical Application

## Practical Results Vary Even with the Same Build

[A0577][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF] The build screen shows the upper limit of what a character “can do.”
[A0578][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE] Practical performance changes with buff maintenance, sustained attacks, timing of CC, ブロック（Block）, and ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, switching between front and back bars, use of obstacles, when healing begins, recognition of offensive turns, and remaining resources; even with the same build, the actual utilization rate of its performance changes.
[A0579][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][CAUSE][PRACTICE][BUILD_GENERAL] Even if a character has the performance to handle many use cases, if the player has not learned the execution and decisions required for each use case, only the portions of that versatility for which the player is proficient can be converted reliably into practical combat value.
[A0580][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][PRACTICE][GROUP_CONTENT][RANGE_COST] Continuing to attack as a back-line ranged DD in large-scale field and keep battles in シロディール（Cyrodiil） is a valid PvP job in itself, and repeated operation in that role can develop proficiency in attacking large groups, managing range, and maintaining position relative to the frontline.
[A0581][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][RANGE_COST] Achieving results as a back-line ranged DD in large-scale combat does not necessarily expose lack of proficiency in defense under focus, offensive/defensive transitions, one-on-one combos, and similar personal-combat skills; a player may obtain results in that role while deficiencies in personal-combat skill remain.
[A0582][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][RANGE_COST] To operate a general-purpose ranged DD effectively across both large-scale and small-group combat, become proficient in foundational personal-combat skills that can sustain offense, defense, healing, CC, and resource decisions against one enemy's worth of combat power even when the benefit of range is not working sufficiently.
[A0583][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][RANGE_COST] Candidate environments for practicing the personal-combat skills of a general-purpose ranged DD include duels, small-number direct fights in バトルグラウンド（Battlegrounds）, and local fights in 帝都（Imperial City）, where defense against an individual opponent, offensive/defensive transitions, combos, and resource exchanges can be repeated at higher density than in large-scale back-line combat.
[A0584][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][PRACTICE][BUILD_GENERAL] Copying the equipment and skill configuration of a skilled general-purpose ranged DD does not copy the player's combat judgments, combo precision, handling under focus, and other player skills developed in personal and small-group combat.
[A0585][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][PRACTICE][RANGE_COST][ROLE_BRUISER][OPPORTUNITY_COST] When a ranged DD moving from large-scale シロディール（Cyrodiil） combat into smaller バトルグラウンド（Battlegrounds） or similar content repeatedly struggles in direct fights against melee builds optimized for small-group combat, diagnose separately underdeveloped personal-combat skills and the opportunity cost in frontal offense/defense paid for range, while allowing that both may be causes at the same time.
[A0586][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][PRACTICE][RANGE_COST] Being able to achieve sufficient results through a preferred large-scale ranged role alone can reduce the perceived need to practice missing personal-combat skills and become a learning factor that causes the player to repeat already-strong skills while progress in the skills needed for broader versatility stagnates.
[A0587][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][DIAG][RULE][PRACTICE][RANGE_COST][OPPORTUNITY_COST] When a ranged DD with extensive experience in large-scale シロディール（Cyrodiil） combat struggles in small-group combat, do not immediately reduce the cause to either player skill alone or build performance alone; diagnose proficiency in personal-combat skills and the opportunity cost of range separately.
[A0588][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][LIMIT][PRACTICE][EPISTEMIC_SEPARATION] Do not treat long participation in large-scale combat as evidence of equal proficiency in personal combat or bruiser skills. At the same time, do not use deficiencies in small-group combat alone to undervalue skills developed in large-scale combat such as range management, group pressure, and frontline awareness.
[A0589][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][DIAG][RULE][PRACTICE][BUILD_GENERAL] If personal-combat skill is diagnosed as the primary bottleneck, before repeatedly changing equipment, present as an improvement candidate a practice environment that demands that skill at high density.
[A0590][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][PRACTICE][RANGE_COST][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] When advising on improvement of a general-purpose ranged DD, distinguish whether the goal is to increase frontal-combat capability as a ranged bruiser, convert range into other value such as skirmishing, off-healing, or debuffing, or maintain specialization for large-scale combat.
[A0591][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][PRACTICE][BUILD_GENERAL] Do not treat the versatility a character theoretically possesses as identical to the player's practical versatility; separately verify the execution and decision proficiency needed to use each function in actual combat.
[A0592][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][RANGE_COST] When the missing skill lies in the perception-action loop of personal combat, do not casually assume that “continuing more large-scale シロディール（Cyrodiil） combat will naturally teach it.” Prioritize high-task-fidelity practice that reproduces at high density the necessary decision frequency, time pressure, and offensive/defensive load.
[A0593][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG] If an opponent using the same equipment and skills alone seems much tougher or deals much higher damage, do not immediately attribute the cause to your overall weakness.
[A0594][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG] When the same equipment or skills produce different practical results, identify differences in utilization rate or timing one by one.

## Use Duels as a Laboratory

[A0595][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][PRACTICE] Battlegrounds (BG) and シロディール（Cyrodiil） contain simultaneous variables such as player count, terrain, allies, lag, and attacks from other directions, making conditions complex for testing a single element; duels with acquaintances are therefore useful for build adjustment and mechanic verification.
[A0596][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE] For build adjustment, comparative testing that changes only one element at a time is useful.
[A0597][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE] When learning an offensive combo, first repeat it against a Target Dummy until the input order, bar swaps, and timing can be completed reliably, then add mid-sequence changes and cancellations in PvP in response to opponent movement and defensive reactions. Do not treat stable real-player execution through the more variable PvP environment alone as a sound premise while the basic sequence still cannot be reproduced reliably against a dummy.
[A0598][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][BUILD_REGRESSION_CHECK] After changing a build multiple times, re-check not only the changed parts but also whether major functions that worked before the changes have been unintentionally lost.
[A0599][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][FUNCTION_LOSS] When damage, durability, or similar performance worsens for unknown reasons, check not only numerical insufficiency but also whether major roles such as buffs, debuffs, CC, healing, movement, or resource supply have dropped out of the configuration during adjustment.
[A0600][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG] In comparative testing, for defense measure HP loss, the number of actions required to restore HP, and resources required; for offense measure not only maximum damage but whether you forced the opponent to heal, how many times, and how many offensive opportunities you created before resource depletion.

## Display and Verification

[A0601][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][EX][RULE] Because PvP results can be misread from screen display or subjective feel alone, questions such as “Criticals do not occur,” “the effect does not apply,” or “only a specific skill is weak” should, when possible, be repeatedly checked through combat logs, recording tools, or duels; do not discard the entire build based on a single subjective impression.
[A0602][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND] If repeated practical use remains difficult, consider that the option may not fit you even if its theoretical performance is high.
[A0603][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE] Evaluate builds using both numerical values and usability.

## Prioritizing Causes of Death and Bottleneck Learning

[A0604][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][PRACTICE] When you die, do not end with the general summary “I am weak”; classify the death into one cause category.
[A0605][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][DEATH_REVIEW] When analyzing a death, if possible review the moments immediately before it from a recording instead of deciding the cause only from memory.
[A0606][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][DEATH_REVIEW] When reviewing a recording, inspect position, resources, buffs, defensive actions, and enemy attack tells before determining the death-cause category.
[A0607][EX] Example death-cause categories include failing to 打破（Break Free） CC, スタミナ（Stamina） depletion, delayed healing start, healing-over-time expiration, excessive front-bar time, failure to ブロック（Block） burst, being caught by multiple enemies on open ground, insufficient pressure, overpursuit away from allies, and excessive investment in an opponent you cannot kill.
[A0608][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][LIMIT] Choosing one death cause allows a concrete practice target to be set for the next match, while a diagnosis of “everything was bad” makes it difficult to decide what to fix next.
[A0609][CLAIM][DEATH_REVIEW] Multiple major factors commonly contribute to one death simultaneously; for beginners, delayed 打破（Break Free）, failed ブロック（Block）, delayed healing, unnecessary continued offense, positioning, insufficient resources, and similar problems often overlap in the same death.
[A0610][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][DEATH_REVIEW] Narrowing the death cause to one is not meant to claim that only that cause produced the death; it is a learning procedure for identifying the behavioral bottleneck expected to produce the largest immediate improvement and fixing the next practice target to one item.
[A0611][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][DIAG][DEATH_REVIEW] Even when multiple major mistakes are confirmed in one death, do not treat listing all of them as superior diagnosis; prioritize the one factor most likely to change the result or the current behavioral bottleneck as the next improvement target.
[A0612][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][PRACTICE][DEATH_REVIEW] After the chosen behavioral bottleneck improves, re-analyze subsequent deaths and move to the next dominant bottleneck. Treat repeated focus on one factor at a time as data collection that keeps it interpretable which improvement changed practical results.
[A0613][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM] The ability to understand concrete causes of defeat is a major learning asset that persists even when patches change.

## Beginner Practice Order

[A0614][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] In the first stage of practice, make the primary goal that when you notice you are about to die, you stop attacking, first establish ブロック（Block）, and then move into the defensive sequence. Success is not required every time from the start; increase the number of times you notice danger and can switch from block into defense.
[A0615][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 2, make increasing situations where you retain enough emergency スタミナ（Stamina） for one 打破（Break Free） or ロール回避（Roll Dodge） the primary goal. Continuous maintenance is not required from the start; recognize situations where you spent it all and improve in the next match.
[A0616][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 3, aim to use obstacles to reduce the time you continue taking attacks from multiple enemies on open ground. Do not require continuous complete avoidance; treat partial reduction of exposure time as progress.
[A0617][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 4, aim to increase situations where CC can be used as the signal to begin offense.
[A0618][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 5, aim to combine multiple attacks after CC; do not require reproducing a complete combo every time from the start.
[A0619][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 6, aim to try continuing offense when the opponent is forced into healing and reduce unnecessary offense when you are forced into healing.
[A0620][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][GROUP_CONTENT][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING][ESCAPE_ROUTE] In Practice Stage 7, aim to gradually increase situations where you check ally positions and can think about both the direction to escape and the direction back toward allies.
[A0621][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL][PRIORITY][PRACTICE][BEGINNER_ALL_ROLES][NOT_INITIAL_REQUIREMENT][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING] In Practice Stage 8, aim to find one bottleneck in your build or operation and improve problems one at a time rather than trying to fix many at once.
[A0622][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE] Once you can perform basic controls, resource management, and battlefield attention without consciously thinking through each of them, make observing the opponent's state the next learning target.
[A0623][AUTHOR_VIEW][GOAL] Ideally, make learning the major attack effects and operating feel of every class a goal.
[A0624][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE] If learning every class is difficult, at least learn the major attack effects and operating feel of high-threat classes in the current environment.
[A0625][CAUSE] Knowing the major attack effects and operating feel of an opponent's class greatly improves the accuracy of responses when facing that class.

## Practical Decision Flow

[A0626][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][COND][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] 1. Determine whether “I am about to die right now.” If yes, stop attacking, first establish ブロック（Block）, then proceed as needed to healing, switching to the defensive bar, LoS, creating distance, and similar recovery, and check remaining resources. If not, proceed to the next step.
[A0627][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][DIAG][COND] ② Determine “is the opponent ignoring my attacks and continuing to hit me?” If your attacks are being ignored, suspect insufficient pressure and review buffs, debuffs, Penetration, CC, and combo timing. If the opponent is healing or defending, there is a high chance your offensive turn has begun.
[A0628][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][COND] ③ Determine “are CC and burst ready?” If ready, engage at a moment when the opponent's defense becomes thin. If not, do not forcibly commit all major resources; maintain pressure while preparing.
[A0629][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][COND] ④ Determine “do I have enough スタミナ（Stamina） left for 打破（Break Free） and ロール回避（Roll Dodge）?” If not, do not overpursue; create distance, recover, and readjust. If yes, continue normal decision-making.
[A0630][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][COND] ⑤ Determine “am I being targeted by multiple enemies?” If yes, do not take every attack head-on; split pursuers using obstacles, distance, and CC, and counterattack when one becomes isolated. If not targeted by multiple enemies, continue normal offense and defense.
[A0631][AUTHOR_VIEW][PROC][COND][RULE] ⑥ Determine “has a long time passed without being able to kill the opponent?” If the fight is stalled, do not fixate on the same opponent; shift attention to another target, allies, or an objective, and treat disengaging as legitimate when the matchup is poor. If it is not stalled, continue the exchange.

## Build-Adjustment Questions

[A0632][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE] Q1. Confirm the character's role and choose one most important job rather than “do everything.”
[A0633][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][EX] Q2. Confirm how the build sends the opponent into a defensive turn. Candidates include single-hit damage, sustained pressure, delayed attacks, CC + burst, and focus with allies.
[A0634][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][EX] Q3. Confirm how the build ends its own defensive turn. Candidates include healing over time, burst healing, shields, LoS, fast disengagement, CC, and ブロック（Block）.
[A0635][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][PRIORITY] Q4. Confirm what resources are reserved for emergencies and maintain at least enough margin for 打破（Break Free） and ロール回避（Roll Dodge）.

## Definitions and Conditions for Gank, Ganker, and Bomber

[A0814][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_GANKER] Treat a Ganker as a role tendency that uses kills through Gank as a primary win condition or as a repeatedly performed primary action. Do not classify an entire build as a Ganker merely because an ordinary DD performs a single Gank when the situation permits.
[A0815][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_GANKER][OPPORTUNITY_COST] Treat a Pure Ganker as a Ganker that trades away some ordinary frontal-combat performance and similar capabilities in order to specialize heavily in the Gank cycle, including target selection, attack-start conditions, killable target range, searching, following, disengaging, and retrying.
[A0816][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][ROLE_BOMBER][BURST_DESIGN] Treat a Bomber as a role tendency whose primary win condition is to exploit or create enemy-group density, synchronize multiple area attacks, CC, delayed attacks, and similar effects into a short period to secure an initial kill or major group damage, and convert that result into additional nearby kills. Do not make possession of one specific item such as 残忍な死（Vicious Death） a defining requirement for Bomber.
[A0817][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_BOMBER][BURST_DESIGN] When evaluating Bomber feasibility, check as major conditions for success: ① enemy-group density worth attacking; ② targets whose defensive margin, defensive preparation, or successful reaction rate is low relative to the target environment's standard, or whose defensive margin has temporarily fallen enough to make the initial kill easier to secure; and ③ enough time to synchronize attack initiation and the major damage events.
[A0818][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][ROLE_BOMBER][ROLE_GANKER][TIME_BUDGET] A Pure Ganker tends to place greater weight on opportunity selection—aligning its attack timing with a target state favorable to itself—whereas a Bomber tends to place greater weight on opportunity response—getting a prepared attack to a short-lived window of group density or an initial-kill opportunity in time.
[A0819][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][ROLE_BOMBER][TIME_BUDGET] The preparation cost of multiple GCDs that a Bomber needs before detonation is easier to absorb on battlefields where attack opportunities remain usable for longer. In シロディール（Cyrodiil） and 帝都（Imperial City）, there is relatively more room to prepare while moving, forming position, or waiting, whereas in バトルグラウンド（Battlegrounds）, where situations change quickly, the same pre-attack GCDs tend to impose greater opportunity loss.
[A0820][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_BOMBER][TIME_BUDGET][BURST_DESIGN] In Bomber GCD evaluation, separate the preparation GCDs paid before successful detonation from the follow-up GCDs used to finish survivors after the compressed initial attack. Reducing preparation GCDs increases how often the Bomber reaches an attack opportunity in time, while follow-up GCDs are evaluated as expansion of the result after detonation.
[A0821][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][EVAL][ROLE_BOMBER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] If you only add the final hit, such as an execute, to an enemy that allies have already brought nearly to death and this incidentally triggers surrounding damage such as 残忍な死（Vicious Death）, do not evaluate that alone as a successful Bomber attack sequence. Separately evaluate the difference personally created through direct contribution as an ordinary DD, creation of the initial kill, synchronized attacks into the group, and conversion into surrounding kills.
[A0822][AUTHOR_VIEW][PATCH][AI][LIMIT][ROLE_BOMBER] When explaining auxiliary effects that expand damage around a Bomber from kills, status effects, or similar triggers, 残忍な死（Vicious Death） may be used as a representative example, but do not limit that job to Vicious Death alone. 疫病爆発（Plaguebreak）, some combat Champion Point effects, and other elements may have similar or supporting roles depending on the patch, so recheck specific examples and effects under current mechanics at the time of the answer.
[A0823][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][ROLE_BOMBER][ROLE_GANKER][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not infer long-duration stealth, invisibility, close-range approach, or solo play as requirements merely from the name Ganker or Bomber. Classify from the actual win condition and combat behavior, including ranged variants, high-mobility variants, variants that participate in ordinary combat, and variants incorporated into group tactics.

## Practical Design for 8v8 Bombers

[A0824][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEF][SCOPE][ROLE_BOMBER][GROUP_CONTENT] In this text, an 8v8 Bomber refers mainly to operation in casual 8v8 バトルグラウンド（Battlegrounds） where enemy-group density and initial-kill opportunities realistically arise and the player can continue participating in combat as a Bomber. This design approach can also be applied to casual 4v4 when similar conditions are present.
[A0825][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ROLE_BOMBER][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][GROUP_CONTENT] While keeping a broad range of acceptable setups, an 8v8 Bomber is often easier to operate when more performance is allocated to durability, healing, and renewed disengagement than on one-exchange Bombers in シロディール（Cyrodiil） or 帝都（Imperial City）. Treat this as a recommended tendency for surviving after a failed attempt and making the next attack opportunity easier to use, not as a participation requirement that failure must never occur.
[A0826][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][EVAL][ROLE_BOMBER][TIME_BUDGET][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] Do not evaluate an 8v8 Bomber only by the maximum size of one Bomb; evaluate attack attempt, success or failure, survival, recovery, rebuilding resources such as アルティメット（Ultimate）, and return to the next attack opportunity as one repeated cycle.
[A0827][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_BOMBER][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT] In 8v8 Bomber design, treat setups that do not require too many preparation GCDs to obtain sufficient Bomb power as strong, easy-to-use candidates. Evaluate the increased frequency of reaching short-lived clustering or initial-kill opportunities in time against the theoretical maximum synchronized damage.
[A0828][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_BOMBER][TIME_BUDGET] Many Bomber setups use アルティメット（Ultimate） as a primary detonation resource, and because the next attackable group state may reappear relatively quickly in 8v8, insufficient Ultimate can become a bottleneck on execution frequency. While checking opportunity cost against the build's overall damage and durability, evaluate setups that supplement Ultimate generation as strong candidates.
[A0829][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ROLE_BOMBER][MOVEMENT_SPEED][GROUP_CONTENT] For melee-centered 8v8 Bombers, increasing movement speed within a range that does not substantially sacrifice primary damage makes it easier to reach briefly available Bomb locations, reposition after failure, and return to the next attack opportunity.
[A0830][AUTHOR_VIEW][ALTERNATIVE][ROLE_BOMBER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] For an 8v8 Bomber, in addition to hiding for a long time until an attack opportunity appears, consider operations such as participating in the allied front while waiting for a Bomb opportunity, using movement speed to enter attack position, or beginning detonation from range. Do not treat stealth as unnecessary; evaluate how necessary it is for the intended use.
[A0831][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][ROLE_BOMBER][GROUP_CONTENT] The effective contribution of an 8v8 Bomber depends strongly on the presence of targets against whom an initial kill is easy to establish, enemy clustering density, and how frequently such states occur. If these conditions almost never arise during the match, performing a useful role across the whole match may be more difficult than with a general PvP setup.

## Bomber Defensive Evaluation and Prevention of AI Misreading

[A0832][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][IMPERIAL_CITY][ROLE_BOMBER] Experienced duos, trios, and other small groups centered on experienced players that operate routinely in 帝都（Imperial City） tend to have high individual rates of successful defense, and their small numbers also reduce chain value, so do not rate them highly as realistic standard attack targets for a Bomber. Evaluate their target conditions separately from low-strength, high-density groups that temporarily gather during events or similar situations.
[A0833][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][ROLE_BOMBER][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE][BUILD_EVALUATION_DISCIPLINE] When evaluating Bomber equipment and armor traits, first identify battlefield, number of players, attack range, dependence on stealth, value of surviving after the Bomb, retry frequency, losses on death, and presence or absence of group support, then switch to the evaluation mode corresponding to that use. Do not mix the same defense and damage criteria between uses that maximize the result of a single Bomb and permit a death trade, and uses that survive and repeat Bomb attempts.
[A0834][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][ROLE_BOMBER][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][EQUIPMENT_TOPOLOGY] A Bomber that distributes armor traits broadly into 神々（Divines） or similar options and therefore thins the defensive layer gained from defensive traits may be rated highly in large-scale combat where death trades are acceptable, while potentially losing substantial effective performance in uses where repeated participation, handling focus fire, or losses on death matter. Do not treat this kind of low-defense, high-damage trait allocation as a generally optimal answer merely from the Bomber role name.
[A0835][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][RULE][ROLE_BOMBER][ROLE_GANKER][GROUP_CONTENT][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not treat 4v4 バトルグラウンド（Battlegrounds） as a uniform skill environment; evaluate the practical value of Ganker / Bomber from observable combat conditions such as participants' defensive preparation, reaction accuracy, durability, clustering, and proficiency in ordinary combat.
[A0836][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][AI][RULE][LIMIT][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] When evaluating suitability for a PvP use, do not use mere theoretical success if multiple low-probability conditions all occur simultaneously as evidence of a realistic win condition, recommended option, or meaningful practical value. Judge practical value from the success frequency normally expected in the target environment, losses on failure, and opportunity cost.
[A0837][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][IMPERIAL_CITY][ROLE_BRUISER][ROLE_BOMBER][OPPORTUNITY_COST][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] When assuming an ordinary build for an experienced Bruiser that operates continuously in 帝都（Imperial City）, do not automatically add as part of the standard image a setup that spends a major build slot such as a 5pc primarily on post-kill surrounding damage like 残忍な死（Vicious Death）. Compare the opportunity cost against ordinary direct-engagement performance, and do not generalize limited use for Bomber, specialization against low-strength groups, specific-purpose 1vX, filming projects, or similar cases into the ordinary general image of a Bruiser.
[A0838][AUTHOR_VIEW][AI][LIMIT][ROLE_BOMBER][GROUP_CONTENT][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] Do not generalize a judgment that a dedicated Bomber is practically nonviable in advanced-player-centered 4v4 to beginners, casual 8v8, or 4v4 with variation in participants' combat proficiency. Treat the dedicated judgment for advanced 4v4 as Series B material and do not reuse it as a beginner participation condition, standard recommendation, or failure criterion.

## B1 All Roles / Common Principles

[B0001][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][REQUIREMENT][ALL_ROLES] As a prerequisite for applying advanced-player requirements, require stable execution of the Series A foundational behaviors relevant to the role and use being evaluated. Do not require prior mastery of every beginner-oriented goal in Series A across every role and use.
[B0002][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][ALL_ROLES][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Consider opportunity cost at the group level as well.
[B0003][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][ALL_ROLES][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] At the group level as well, consider pressuring the opponent's free time.
[B0004][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][ALL_ROLES][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] At the group level as well, consider the concept of offensive and defensive turns.
[B0005][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][ALL_ROLES] Every role other than Pure Bruiser can improve its capabilities through actual experience performing the frontal-combat role of a Pure Bruiser.
[B0006][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][ROLE_BRUISER][ALL_ROLES] A Pure Bruiser repeatedly performs offensive, defensive, healing, CC, positional, and resource decisions at high frequency; decision failures tend to return quickly as pressure received, defensive turns, being killed, or similar outcomes; and the player's own decisions change the opponent's actions, whose responses must then be processed in another perception-action loop. For these reasons, training exposure to various techniques and decision-making tends to be highly dense.
[B0007][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][CAUSE][ALL_ROLES] Because other roles can sometimes produce results through role-specific success mechanisms while avoiding direct frontal offensive/defensive exchanges, they may receive less learning exposure to the high-density offensive/defensive load required of a bruiser. This does not mean those other roles have lower value; it means that some learning paths may not repeat the load required of a bruiser sufficiently.
[B0008][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][REQUIREMENT][ALL_ROLES] At the advanced-player level assumed by Series B, evaluate not whether a skill exists at all, but whether the quality of required execution and decisions stably exceeds the demands of actual combat.
[B0009][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][REQUIREMENT][ROLE_BRUISER][ALL_ROLES] If the skills required to process repeated frontal-combat load as a bruiser are insufficient, even a player with high skill in parts of another role may be hindered from reaching broad advanced PvP proficiency.
[B0010][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][REQUIREMENT][ALL_ROLES] It is not necessary to become the strongest Bruiser; what is required for advanced-level learning is to exceed a standard at which the offensive, defensive, and decision load demanded in actual Pure Bruiser play can be handled stably.
[B0011][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][ALL_ROLES] A learning path that continually avoids frontal combat makes it difficult to repeat sufficiently the offensive and defensive judgments needed for advanced PvP operation.
[B0012][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][ALL_ROLES] In learning toward the advanced-player level assumed by this Series B, train as a Bruiser.
[B0013][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][EVAL][PRACTICE][ALL_ROLES] When evaluating skill transfer, emphasize 課題忠実度（task fidelity）: not merely whether practice contains the same execution elements, but how much it preserves the same perception-action loop, decision frequency, time pressure, failure feedback, and offensive-defense load as actual combat.
[B0014][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][EVAL][ALL_ROLES][ROLE_BRUISER] When training personal-combat capability at an advanced-player level, place more weight on task fidelity that allows high-frequency repetition of the perception-action loop, time pressure, and offensive/defensive transitions in which one opponent or a small number of opponents continually react to you than on total playtime in large-scale シロディール（Cyrodiil） combat itself.
[B0015][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][CLAIM][ROLE_BRUISER][ALL_ROLES] The most direct method of reaching the advanced-player level required by this Series B is to repeatedly gain practical experience establishing frontal combat as a Pure Bruiser; paths that avoid Bruiser combat experience while trying to reach equivalent execution and decision quality are generally indirect and difficult.
[B0016][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][ROLE_BRUISER][ALL_ROLES] When trying to reach equivalent execution and decision quality while avoiding Bruiser combat experience, differences in combat sense and value judgments relative to experienced Bruiser players also tend to remain.

### B1A Use of Third Parties in PvPvE Environments

[B0017][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][TACTIC][THIRD_PARTY_ENVIRONMENT_USE] In a PvPvE environment, attacks from monsters or third parties may be used to change the activation timing of your on-damage-taken effects or on-shield-break effects into a state that enemy players alone cannot control.

## B2 Tank

[B0018][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][LIMIT][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] In capped advanced small-group direct combat, when enemies can rationally ignore a Pure Tank and target allies first, additional contribution through healing, buffs, debuffs, CC, positional obstruction, or similar effects gains value by imposing losses on the enemy for ignoring the Tank as well as by providing survival. Do not generalize this requirement as a uniform mandatory condition to different battlefields such as シロディール（Cyrodiil）.
[B0019][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Evaluate the binding value of an advanced Tank not only by the number of enemies occupied but also by the threat and role value of those enemies. If the enemy's main force can freely attack allies while the Tank is fixed only on low-threat enemies or a small number of enemies, do not rate long survival alone as high combat-force binding.
[B0020][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CC][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Tank's CC can create short action windows or attack opportunities for allies, but if the Tank's own offensive pressure cannot continuously force defense or healing from the enemy, do not conclude merely from successfully applying CC that the Tank personally can maintain offensive initiative.
[B0021][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][ROLE_TANK][ROLE_BRUISER][BLOCK][MOVEMENT_SPEED][GROUP_CONTENT][RECOMMENDATION_SCOPE] In optimized small-scale operation where mobility is a primary foundation of activity, do not assume a permanent-ブロック（Block） Pure Tank as the standard frontline slot; evaluate as a strong candidate a composition where a high-durability DD / Bruiser that does not habitually block partially performs Tank-like functions such as resistance to focus fire and maintaining the frontline.
[B0022][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][ROLE_TANK][HEALING][HEALING_GEAR][OPPORTUNITY_COST][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] If the HoT required by a Tank is difficult to secure sufficiently through ordinary offensive / healing scaling alone and build slots permit it, evaluate equipment that produces a HoT or similar persistent healing using a major Tank-side attribute such as maximum health as a supplemental candidate.
[B0023][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_TANK][HEALING][HEALING_GEAR][OPPORTUNITY_COST][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] In advanced Tank healing design, prioritize the sequence of first establishing the necessary healing functions through skills, then supplementing shortages or efficiency with equipment. If excessive dependence on synergies among multiple pieces of equipment and skills makes battlefield changes or changing some equipment difficult, evaluate that reduction in adjustment freedom as an opportunity cost as well.
[B0024][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] A Tank with added value from healing, buffs, or debuffs becomes able to contribute to allies more easily without depending on location or conditions.
[B0025][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][ROLE_TANK][LIMIT][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Occupying rule objectives is a contribution a Tank can make relatively easily without sacrificing survival, but it requires success conditions and its effect may be limited.
[B0026][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][ROLE_TANK][LIMIT][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Physically interposing on an enemy line of fire is a contribution a Tank can make relatively easily without sacrificing survival, but it requires success conditions and its effect may be limited.
[B0027][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][ROLE_TANK] If the class line has a useful Snare skill, strongly prioritize using it.
[B0028][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][CLAIM][ROLE_TANK] Snare does not grant crowd-control immunity.
[B0029][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Being able to enter the enemy frontline first and also perform additional jobs is an element of a high-contribution Tank.
[B0030][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][ROLE_TANK][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] A Tank that dies frequently is a bad Tank even if the player intends to support allies.
[B0031][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_TANK][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When an advanced Tank obtains a pull through an equipment effect, evaluate simultaneously the value of reducing the GCD, resources, and execution required by an active skill and the opportunity cost of losing durability, healing, sustain, or other support that could have occupied that equipment slot.
[B0032][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_TANK][PURGE][OPPORTUNITY_COST] For an advanced Tank continuously receiving DoTs and debuffs from many players, check whether a cleanse skill with a limited number of removals can keep up in terms of GCD, resources, and cleansing speed; if not, evaluate mass-cleanse equipment as a strong candidate including the opportunity cost of the equipment slot.

## B3 Healer / Buffer / Debuffer Support


### Opportunity Cost of a Support Slot in Small-Group Direct Combat

[B0033][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][COND][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In environments such as advanced-level 4v4 where each slot in a fixed small group carries large responsibility and direct-combat pressure and kills are the primary win condition, evaluate the value of using one Support slot by the difference in overall combat value versus replacing that slot with an equally skilled Bruiser.
[B0034][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][REQUIREMENT][ROLE_SUPPORT][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When using one Support slot in advanced-level small-group direct combat, use as the criterion for justifying that slot that the Support's total value is at least equal to the overall combat value of the equally skilled Bruiser lost by selecting the Support.
[B0035][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][ADVANTAGE_CREATION_CONVERSION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Do not evaluate a Support's total value only by its own direct damage or healing amount; include the difference produced through healing, buffs, debuffs, CC, resource support, and similar effects in reducing allies' self-defense GCDs, increasing their attackable time, maintaining pressure, forcing additional defensive actions from enemies, and converting those advantages into kill opportunities or numerical advantage.
[B0036][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][ROLE_SUPPORT][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In advanced-level small-group direct combat, even if a Support keeps allies alive, if that survival does not convert into sufficient attack opportunities, pressure, resource margin, CC advantage, kill opportunities, or similar results and therefore cannot cover the opportunity cost of losing one Bruiser, that Support cannot justify consuming one composition slot.

### Additional Support and Hybrid-Role Evaluation for Advanced Support

[B0037][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_SUPPORT][ROLE_TANK][OPPORTUNITY_COST][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When a Healer / Tank incorporates additional support, if that support can be established sufficiently through equipment effects or effects attached to healing, defensive, or support actions already required by the core role, prioritize those over active support that consumes GCDs or resources needed for the core role, while also comparing the opportunity cost of the equipment slot.
[B0038][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][ROLE_TANK][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When evaluating a dedicated Healer / Tank as also performing a DD-type role, require that the build maintain its dedicated healing, defensive, and support functions while also establishing enough offensive pressure to force effective defensive or healing actions from the enemy. Do not treat merely adding attack skills or small amounts of damage as performing a DD-type role.
[B0039][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_TANK][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Evaluate a Tank / DD hybrid role including the assumed enemy level and combat environment. The same setup may have DD-equivalent kill pressure against low-durability or low-skill enemies, but if it cannot continuously force defense or healing from advanced opponents, evaluate the offensive component as additional Tank pressure.

### Advanced Healer: Healing Zone and Rescue Decisions

[B0040][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In group combat among advanced players, a Healer should prioritize continuously establishing a dense healing zone into which allies can retreat, recover, and re-enter, rather than chasing individual allies in an attempt to prevent every death, and should not collapse the entire healing zone to rescue one player.
[B0041][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In group combat among advanced players, treat a major value of healing as using persistent healing to absorb ordinary incoming damage on allied Bruisers, reducing the GCDs Bruisers must spend on self-healing and allowing them to maintain offensive pressure.
[B0042][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PRIORITY][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][UNDER_FOCUS][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][ESCAPE_ROUTE] When an advanced Healer moves to survive while under focus, do not make the objective moving far from the main battlefield and stopping healing supply; prioritize movement routes that use obstacles, allies, and healing methods that function while moving so that personal survival and healing supply to allies can coexist. If death cannot be avoided while maintaining healing line of sight, accept a temporary reduction in healing supply, create sufficient LoS, and prioritize resuming healing after surviving.
[B0043][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][DIAG][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When deciding whether to leave the main healing zone to rescue a distant ally, evaluate whether the benefit of saving that ally exceeds the loss imposed on other allies by weakening the current healing zone.
[B0044][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When an ally leaves the main healing zone, check not only the result that the target is far away, but also whether positioning and operation on the ally's side or on your side is the primary cause of the target being outside the healing zone.
[B0045][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PRIORITY][RULE][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In individual rescue of a distant ally, normally prioritize maintaining the overall healing zone, and move to individual rescue only when there is a concrete reason to override that priority.
[B0046][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Healer should not immediately attribute an ally's death to inadequate self-defense by that ally; check the Healer's own healing range, HoT maintenance, support downtime, delays in emergency healing, and similar factors to evaluate whether the death could have been prevented from the Healer's side. At the same time, do not uniformly attribute deaths that the Healer could not prevent to Healer responsibility.
[B0047][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][LIMIT][CAUSE][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Selecting where to place a healing zone requires skill, and obstacles can also be used by enemies for LoS; however, placing the healing zone over an obstacle that allies can circle around often yields benefits that exceed the disadvantage of the enemy also using the obstacle, because allies can break enemy line of fire and weaken sustained pressure without moving far from the healing zone, can re-engage after healing with only short movement, and can more easily form the main battlefield around a common location for retreat, recovery, and re-entry.

### Advanced Healer: Death Analysis and LoS

[B0048][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][EX][RULE][CLAIM][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] There are also situations where placing the healing zone in an open area is stronger, with Battlegrounds (BG) objective terrain lacking nearby obstacles as an example. Even then, as a basic approach choose a position around the objective that is easy to circle, and being even somewhat closer to an obstacle makes the healing zone easier for allies to use.
[B0049][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] If a Healer moves excessively without purpose, it can make allies uneasy and make enemies judge the Healer as weak, increasing the likelihood of focused attacks.
[B0050][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] As a basic approach, an advanced Healer should use obstacles enough to reduce incoming enemy lines of fire while maintaining healing supply to allies, without unnecessarily cutting healing line of sight to allies merely to LoS enemies targeting the Healer.
[B0051][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Healer should not begin all healing preparation only after enemy pressure rises; maintain assigned HoTs, important buffs, and similar effects during normal periods so that GCDs can immediately be allocated to emergency healing when incoming damage increases.
[B0052][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Healer should switch GCD priorities as the situation changes among normal maintenance, enemy burst, defensive recovery, allied offense, and similar phases, rather than repeating only a fixed healing rotation.

### Advanced Healer: Preparation, GCDs, and Resources

[B0053][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][FUNCTION_LOSS][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Healer build should not depend on a single powerful channeled heal or アルティメット（Ultimate） heal as its sole healing foundation; even when that heal is interrupted or unavailable, it should be able to continue supplying healing through ordinary skills and pre-established HoTs.
[B0054][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][TIME_BUDGET][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Before entering a powerful heal that restricts use of other skills for a long period, an advanced Healer should establish HoTs, necessary buffs, and similar effects in advance where possible, reducing the loss of the group's healing foundation caused by being unable to use ordinary healing and support GCDs during the channel.
[B0055][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][SUSTAIN][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced group Healer should treat unused personal resources as future group-rescue capability and maintain healing margin or a rapid resource-rebuilding method sufficient to handle unexpected increases in pressure.
[B0056][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][LIMIT][ROLE_SUPPORT][HEALING][SUSTAIN][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] A design in which an advanced Healer intentionally uses a primary healing resource such as マジカ（Magicka） down to a low level should be considered viable only if there is another reliable asset that can quickly and practically rebuild the required amount in an emergency.

### Premade Support, Anti-Healer Play, and Cleansing

[B0057][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][SUSTAIN][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] An advanced Healer should not adopt a normal operating design that intentionally exhausts スタミナ（Stamina）, and should preserve enough margin to perform at least emergency 打破（Break Free）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, or similar actions.
[B0058][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][RULE][GROUP_CC][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When a Support handles burst-initiation CC in premade combat among advanced players, synchronize the CC and start of burst in advance through voice communication or similar means so that allied damage overlaps the short restraint window before 打破（Break Free）.
[B0059][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_SUPPORT][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When a premade needs a persistent debuff role, before reducing a Bruiser's attack GCDs, skill slots, or equipment slots, examine whether that job can be consolidated into the Healer / Support; if it can be performed sufficiently on the Support side, prioritize maintaining Bruiser pressure.
[B0060][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][HEALING][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] When building anti-Healer measures in group combat among advanced players, do not use CC alone as the primary method; evaluate synchronizing focus fire and healing-reduction debuffs with allied burst as the main method.
[B0061][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][LIMIT][ROLE_SUPPORT][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] In small-scale / ball-group play where the group enters tightly into enemy groups and multiple allies simultaneously receive DoTs and debuffs, secure group cleansing at high priority; in small-scale play that avoids such contact, do not treat it as permanently mandatory.

## B4 Bruiser

[B0062][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][REQUIREMENT][ROLE_PURE_BRUISER] Set the minimum viable level for an advanced Pure Bruiser as being able, in frontal combat against an equally skilled Pure Bruiser, to establish at least an even pressure exchange rather than being sent one-sidedly into defensive turns continuously.
[B0063][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][ROLE_HYBRID_BRUISER] A hybrid-role Bruiser is generally inferior to a Pure Bruiser in direct offensive and defensive output.
[B0064][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_HYBRID_BRUISER] Evaluate a hybrid-role Bruiser by total power including the value produced by the role it combines with Bruiser.
[B0065][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][REQUIREMENT][ROLE_HYBRID_BRUISER] A hybrid-role Bruiser must be able to continue applying pressure while enduring to at least a certain degree even in combat against a Pure Bruiser.
[B0066][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][REQUIREMENT][ROLE_BRUISER] An advanced Bruiser requires proficiency in offense, defense, healing, CC, and tactics.
[B0067][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][REQUIREMENT][ROLE_BRUISER] Evaluate an advanced Bruiser's proficiency not by whether each skill is merely possessed, but by whether execution and decision quality stably exceeds actual combat requirements even when those skills are demanded at high frequency in frontal combat against equally skilled opponents; only upon reaching that level does the player stand at the starting point of advanced Bruiser play.
[B0068][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][EVAL][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] One benchmark for checking advanced bruiser execution proficiency through supplemental PvE training is being able to clear veteran ヴァテシュラン洞穴（Vateshran Hollows） reliably and with comfortable margin while remaining on the PvP build.
[B0069][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][EVAL][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] When using veteran ヴァテシュラン洞穴（Vateshran Hollows） as practice with a PvP build, do not treat the clear itself as a direct measurement of PvP strength. Use it as a supplementary benchmark for the stability of combat processing: whether you can continue offense and defense while maintaining self-healing, resource management, position management, awareness of enemy positions, and judgment of the next required action.
[B0070][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][EVAL][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] In solo-arena practice with a PvP build, compatibility among the class, skills, equipment configuration, and PvE-specific mechanics can greatly change clear suitability; therefore, do not directly convert differences in clear speed, ability to meet DPS checks, or remaining margin into differences in PvP ability.
[B0071][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][CAUSE][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] Combat against an advanced PvP player is more difficult than ヴァテシュラン洞穴（Vateshran Hollows） because, among the primary reasons, the opposing player changes behavior in response to your offense, defense, position, resource state, and similar information and actively rearranges the timing of CC, pressure, burst, defense, disengagement, and other actions, creating a higher-load perception-decision-action loop and greater time pressure than fixed PvE.
[B0072][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][RULE][ROLE_BRUISER] For a Bruiser, treat pressure as the most important element; even when combining another role, do not lose pressure that continuously forces enemies into defense or healing.
[B0073][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRIORITY][RULE][ROLE_BRUISER][BUILD_GENERAL] When designing a Bruiser, whether pure or combined with another role, first secure a level at which the primary attack used most often deals sufficient effective damage against the intended opposing Bruiser. In many configurations this is the spammable; treat other offensive elements as tools that intensify pressure when layered onto a sufficiently effective primary attack, not as a basic plan for replacing and lifting an almost harmless primary attack from inadequate performance to viability.
[B0074][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_BRUISER][PRESSURE_VS_FINISH] Evaluate a Bruiser's threat on the premise that actual damage can force enemies into defense and healing, not on the premise that enemies mistakenly believe low damage is high damage. Even without securing a kill, forcing sufficient self-defense frequency has value as pressure.
[B0075][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][ROLE_BRUISER][PRESSURE_VS_FINISH] In a frontal one-on-one exchange between Bruisers, if you are repeatedly forced into defense or healing to maintain high HP while a fully committed offensive turn with repeated hits from your primary attack still forces little or no defense or healing from the opponent, diagnose the current frontal offense-defense exchange performance as heavily unfavorable.
[B0076][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][RULE][ROLE_BRUISER][SUSTAIN] When treating opponent resource exhaustion as a win condition, infer expenditure and recovery from actions such as Block, Roll Dodge, Break Free, healing, attacks, and resource-restoration actions. If your offense has forced little major defensive or healing expenditure from the opponent, require especially strong independent observational evidence before judging that the opponent is close to resource exhaustion.
[B0077][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_RANGED_BRUISER][RANGE_COST] If an advanced ranged Bruiser allocates performance budget to range and thereby has lower frontal exchange performance than a similar melee Bruiser, evaluate in Pure Bruiser use whether that deficit can be recovered through the benefits of range, such as pressure before contact, continued pursuit pressure, and safe attack time. If those range benefits cannot be recovered sufficiently in actual combat under the relevant conditions, the build tends to be disadvantaged as a Pure Bruiser.
[B0078][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][CAUSE][ROLE_BRUISER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] Do not treat a Bruiser as a hero who charges alone into the enemy and solves everything; account for the burden imposed on nearby allies when entering alone.
[B0079][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][ROLE_BRUISER] As a basic rule, one player's combat power is treated as one player's worth.
[B0080][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CLAIM][ROLE_BRUISER] Differences in combat power between individual players tend to be less extreme among advanced players.
[B0081][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][CLAIM][ROLE_BRUISER] Even if one button can easily kill multiple players, do not treat that as evidence of overwhelming personal strength; the primary cause is that the opponents were weak. Combat against advanced players is not that simple.
[B0082][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][RULE][ROLE_BRUISER] Because the system does not allow making your own specifications many times greater than an advanced player's, do not underestimate enemy combat power; allocate vigilance equivalent to one player's worth to each one-player enemy and build tactics on the assumption of that combat power.

### Advanced Bruiser: Defensive Decisions, Group Detection, and Practice

[B0083][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] An advanced Bruiser should not determine the transition into a defensive sequence from current HP alone; predict near-future danger from current HP, the burst the enemy can establish in a short window, enemy attack preparation, remaining defensive resources, position, CC state, and similar factors. Transition to defense before lethal pressure becomes established, while also avoiding an overly early abandonment of the offensive turn merely because of light damage that can still be handled with ordinary defensive and healing capacity; continue attacking while a safe margin remains.
[B0084][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PRACTICE][ROLE_BRUISER][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Advanced players may sometimes turn to offense below maximum HP after reading initiative, remaining healing over time, enemy attack preparation, remaining defensive resources, and similar factors. Treat this as an advanced situational judgment that accepts reduced HP margin in exchange for taking the available time and attack opportunity instead of waiting to recover to maximum HP.
[B0085][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][DOT][ROLE_BRUISER][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Even when the exchange under sustained pressure is unfavorable, if the opponent has low durability and there is a concrete kill window in which you will not be defeated first even after turning to offense while existing DoTs or similar pressure remain on you, evaluate counterattacking for a reversal as an option in addition to early disengagement.
[B0086][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] In advanced group combat, expect the player under pressure to detect their own developing danger early and transition to self-defense at a high standard; if allies detect the same danger in advance and preemptively provide healing, shields, CC, pressure diversion, or similar help, evaluate that as advanced additional group contribution. Do not demand the same detection precision from the target and surrounding allies.
[B0087][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] Practicing PvE such as solo arenas in a PvP build can be used as auxiliary training that is based not merely on attack practice but on predicting your own developing danger and successfully defending while also processing additional actions such as attacking, checking surroundings, checking behind, and adjusting position according to the situation.
[B0088][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] In solo-arena practice with a PvP build, repeatedly practice a self-defense sequence that, from the stage when danger is detected, combines disengagement, self-healing, defense, positioning adjustment, and similar actions as needed to return to a state where you can fight again. Make one practice objective progressing from searching for individual inputs only after the situation becomes urgent toward entering defense while there is still margin and executing the sequence stably in parallel with other situational decisions.
[B0089][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][PRACTICE][PROGRESSIVE_LEARNING][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] In solo-arena practice with a PvP build, first establish the ability to continue combat without losing control of survival, healing, resources, position, and surrounding awareness. As processing capacity increases, incorporate additional operations such as burst into the same combat load and increase the frequency with which they can be executed while preserving the foundational processing.
[B0090][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][EVAL][PRACTICE][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] Do not treat the ability to establish a self-defense sequence reliably in a solo arena with a PvP build, by itself, as proof that the same processing will succeed in advanced PvP. On the other hand, if self-healing, defense, disengagement, and similar recovery from increasing danger cannot be handled reliably even in a highly predictable solo-arena environment with lower decision load than player-versus-player combat, treat that as a foundational deficiency in self-defense execution to improve before moving on to fights against advanced players.
[B0091][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][LIMIT][PVE_PVP_INTERACTION][ROLE_BRUISER] Because fixed PvE behavior cannot sufficiently reproduce the perception-action loop in which an opposing player changes behavior in response to your decisions and you then process that response, interpersonal time pressure, and failure feedback, do not treat it as a substitute for actual advanced Bruiser combat experience.

## B5 Evaluation of Ranged DD and Hybrid Roles in Advanced Fixed Small-Group Combat


### B5.1 Ranged Pure Bruiser: Range Cost, Focus Fire, and Self-Defense Floor

[B0092][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][DIAG][ROLE_RANGED_BRUISER][ROLE_PURE_BRUISER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][OPPORTUNITY_COST][UNDER_FOCUS] When evaluating a ranged Pure Bruiser in advanced fixed small-group combat, even if range reduces incoming damage to that player, if the resulting enemy focus shifts disproportionately to allies and the player's own pressure also fails to compensate for the frontal exchange performance actually lost for range, do not treat personal safety alone as sufficient group contribution.
[B0093][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_RANGED_BRUISER][ROLE_PURE_BRUISER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][UNDER_FOCUS][ESCAPE_ROUTE] When a ranged Pure Bruiser uses distance, LoS, and mobility for self-defense in advanced fixed small-group combat, maintain or quickly resume effective combat participation while consuming enemy time, movement, and attacks directed toward you, and do not remain away from the main battlefield so long that excessive numerical or focus-fire burden is pushed onto allies.
[B0094][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CAUSE][DIAG][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][UNDER_FOCUS][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN] If a DD that sacrifices self-defense in advanced fixed small-group combat falls to a level where it cannot handle focused attacks even briefly, that weakness converts from merely reduced durability into enemy priority targeting, loss of continuous attack time, and numerical burden on allies through forced disengagement or death; therefore evaluate a minimum survival floor under focus independently.
[B0095][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][DEFAULT][ROLE_RANGED_BRUISER][ROLE_PURE_BRUISER][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION][DEFENSIVE_DESIGN][OPPORTUNITY_COST][ESCAPE_ROUTE] In advanced 4v4, for a ranged Pure Bruiser where the opportunity cost actually paid for range makes it difficult to secure both necessary pressure and frontal durability equivalent to a similar melee Bruiser, treat as a strong basic solution prioritizing required offensive pressure over perfectly reproducing frontal durability, while handling periods under pressure through enough self-defense to avoid immediate death plus mobility and LoS.

### B5.2 Hybrid Roles: Role Compression and Reevaluation of Range

[B0096][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][EVAL][ROLE_COMPRESSION_EVALUATION][TIME_BUDGET][OPPORTUNITY_COST] In an advanced build, when multiple role functions that would normally require separate GCDs, resources, and preparation time can be obtained effectively from the same action, maintenance action, equipment effect, or similar source, evaluate as the value of role compression the additional GCDs, resources, and rebuilding time saved by that overlap and the uptime preserved for the primary role. Evaluate not merely possession of multiple effects, but how many separate payments are actually avoided in combat.
[B0097][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_RANGED_BRUISER][ALTERNATIVE][OPPORTUNITY_COST][GROUP_CONTENT][GROUP_CONTRIBUTION] If the cost of compensating for insufficient frontal exchange performance as a Pure Bruiser is high for an advanced ranged DD, treat redesign into a role or hybrid role where range converts directly into group value—such as Skirmisher, off-healing, or debuffing—as a strong candidate instead of only forcing Pure Bruiser performance upward.

## B7 Ganker / Bomber

[B0189][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][CAUSE][ROLE_GANKER][GROUP_CONTENT] In Battlegrounds (BG) 4v4, as the participants become more heavily composed of top-skilled players, targets execute defensive routines such as 打破（Break Free）, ブロック（Block）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, and large heals more quickly and maintain higher vigilance against ambushes; therefore Gankers / Bombers have more difficulty obtaining effective target states and attack-start opportunities, increasing participation difficulty.
[B0190][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][CORE_JUDGMENT][PRIORITY][ROLE_GANKER][TARGET_TIMING_CONTROL][DEFENSIVE_SEQUENCE] When targeting an advanced PvP player for assassination, do not use merely being able to approach from stealth as the attack-start condition; prioritize moments when the target is less able to fully execute post-opening defensive routines such as 打破（Break Free）, ブロック（Block）, ロール回避（Roll Dodge）, or a large heal.
[B0191][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_GANKER][TARGET_TIMING_CONTROL] In assassinations against advanced players, situations that make a target vulnerable may also be predictable to that target as assassination opportunities, so evaluate the target's vulnerability separately from their vigilance toward the situation and do not equate an obvious opportunity with an unguarded opportunity.
[B0192][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][SCOPE][ROLE_GANKER][TARGET_TIMING_CONTROL] When analyzing advanced Pure Ganker operation, assume that the necessary burst preparation and combo execution are already established, and place the primary evaluation on the attack-start decision of which target state and timing should receive that damage.
[B0193][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][PRIORITY][ROLE_GANKER][TARGET_TIMING_CONTROL] If a Pure melee Ganker's maximum combo sufficiently exceeds the assumed durability range of its targets, prioritize securing attack-start conditions that allow the combo to land effectively over adding more damage.
[B0194][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][ROLE_GANKER][ALTERNATIVE_REASONING] Evaluate the specialization value of a Pure Ganker not only by successful assassinations against opponents that an ordinary PvP setup could also kill easily, but by the difference in extending the killable range to high-durability, high-self-defense targets that are difficult for ordinary setups to kill.
[B0195][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][RULE][ROLE_GANKER][TARGET_TIMING_CONTROL] In environments such as 帝都（Imperial City） where target encounter frequency is low, a Pure Ganker should not assume it can select the best target from multiple candidates; decide whether to attack or pass based on whether the currently encountered target meets predetermined assassination-success conditions.
[B0196][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][ROLE_GANKER][OPPORTUNITY_COST] A Pure Ganker that has heavily sacrificed damage tolerance and re-disengagement capability has difficulty surviving and retrying after a failed first assassination, so it requires a higher probability of success from each attack start than a high-mobility Ganker capable of disengaging again.
[B0197][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][EVAL][ROLE_GANKER][OPPORTUNITY_COST][MOVEMENT_SPEED] Even if a high-mobility Ganker has a narrower single-attempt killable range than a Pure melee Ganker, it may have high practical value across the full assassination cycle if the time and losses required for searching, following, position formation, re-disengagement after failure, and retrying are lower.
[B0198][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][ROLE_GANKER] If the effective burst of a ranged Ganker is lower than that of a Pure melee Ganker, it must filter whether a target is within its killable range before deciding to initiate, and therefore has stronger target restrictions based on durability than a Pure melee Ganker.
[B0199][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][ROLE_GANKER][TARGET_TIMING_CONTROL][ADVANTAGE_CREATION_CONVERSION] If a target engaged continuously against a roughly equal opponent is already spending action opportunities and resources on offense, defense, healing, and positional adjustment in that existing fight, evaluate that target as an assassination candidate that can exploit the existing engagement.
[B0200][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][COND][EVAL][ROLE_GANKER][OPPORTUNITY_COST] In an environment such as 帝都（Imperial City） where the practical reward from one player kill is large, even a Pure Ganker with long preparation and waiting times and high losses on failure can more easily recover the opportunity cost of the assassination cycle, so the same assassination performance may have greater use suitability than on a battlefield where one kill yields little practical benefit.
[B0201][ADVANCED_ONLY][NOT_FOR_BEGINNER_REQUIREMENT][AUTHOR_VIEW][SCOPE][LIMIT][ROLE_GANKER] In advanced Ganker operation in 帝都（Imperial City）, assume prior knowledge of the fundamental environment needed for continued combat there, such as major terrain, monsters, bosses, and movement routes, and do not treat learning that environment itself as an advanced Ganker-specific tactic.
